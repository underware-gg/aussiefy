MAMMOTH HOT SPRINGS, Wyo. – From Friday, Sept. 4, the last of the fishin' restrictions on the Firehole, Madison and Gibbon (downstream of Norris Campground) rivers and all the creeks that feed 'em will be lifted, cos the weather and the water have cooled off. Fishos'll be able to fish these rivers, as well as most other park rivers, creeks and lakes, every day from sunup to sundown, as set out in the 2026 Fishing Regulations booklet.

These rivers were first shut to fishin' altogether on July 15, then wound back to part-day restrictions on Aug. 28 as things picked up. The restrictions and closures had to happen during a stretch of low water and high water temperatures over 68 degrees Fahrenheit (20 degrees Celsius) that can kill fish.

Staff'll keep an eye on the water temperatures. Temporary restrictions or closures might be brought in, dependin' on how things are lookin'.

Update: Yellowstone lifts full closures for Firehole, Madison and Gibbon rivers

Part-day restrictions still on from 2 p.m. to sunup the next day

MAMMOTH HOT SPRINGS, Wyo. – From Friday, Aug. 28, fishos can fish the Firehole, Madison and Gibbon (downstream of Norris Campground) rivers and all the creeks that feed 'em from sunup till 2 p.m. every day. Coolin' weather and water temperatures, plus the rain in the forecast, mean the full closures can be lifted.

The full closures were brought in on July 15 cos low water and high water temperatures over 68 degrees Fahrenheit (20 degrees Celsius) can kill fish. The water's been coolin' off overnight, so there's fishin' for part of the day. Fishos can fish most other park rivers, creeks and lakes every day from sunup to sundown, as set out in the 2026 Fishing Regulations booklet.

Staff'll keep an eye on the water temperatures. Temporary restrictions might be lifted, or more closures put in place, dependin' on how things are lookin'.

Fishos: Please fish in the coolest part of the day and land yer fish quick. Don't play a hooked trout till it's knackered. Handle fish gently and in the water as much as ya can, and let 'em come good before ya let 'em go. Yer help will look after the park's fisheries and might save 'em havin' to ban fishin' at all hours on some rivers and creeks if things get worse.
