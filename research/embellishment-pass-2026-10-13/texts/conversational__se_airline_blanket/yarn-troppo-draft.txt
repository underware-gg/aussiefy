Knocked off an airline blanket by accident; had no flamin' clue it was off limits. What do I do?

Pull up a stool. Now, I'm about as new to this travelling caper as you can get. I'm on this long-haul flight, and the airline's handing out blankets and pillows like lollies. Me, I've got no idea you're meant to leave the blanket behind, so when I'm packing up all me gear I scoop it up with the lot and off I go. I'd figured it was a cheap-as-chips sort of blanket, so it must've come with the ticket price or something. Wasn't like I was hanging out to keep it, mind. I was half asleep and bloody freezing, and I just grabbed it in with the rest of me stuff. And no bugger said a word. Not a peep.

Anyway, it's not till I get home that I find the blanket again, and there's this tiny little tag on it: 'do not remove from plane.' Well, didn't I feel rotten. Pinched the thing, accidental or not. I'll never do it again, obviously, but I still feel dead guilty about this time. Is there some way I can get it back to the airline? Or do I just let it go and call it a lesson learned?

Sorry if it's a dopey question, mate, I just sort of went to pieces when I twigged I'd gone and nicked it by accident!!

And just so you know, the airline was Aer Lingus. The Irish mob.

* * *

Righto, if you want to shake the guilt, give the airline a ring and ask 'em what they'd like you to do with the blanket. Tell 'em you took it by mistake cos you weren't all there when you got off the plane, which, let's face it, nobody is after a long-haul.

Odds are they'll tell you to keep it, cos it'd cost 'em more to get it back in the post and chuck it back in the blanket pool than to just buy a new one. And once they've told you straight out to hang onto it, you've got nothing to stress about. Then again, if (less likely) they do want it sent back, well, you send it back, and that'll clear any leftover guilt as well. Either way you're square.
