Accidentally nicked a plane blanket off the airline; had no clue you weren't allowed. What do I do?

Listen to this. I'm flamin' new to the whole travelling caper. I was on a long-haul flight and the airline handed out blankets and pillows. Now, I had no idea I was meant to leave the blanket behind, so when I packed up all my gear I took it with me and off I went. I'd figured it was a cheapish sort of blanket, so it must've been part of the ticket price or something. I wasn't that fussed about keeping it, but I was half asleep and bloody cold and I just scooped it up with the rest of my stuff. No bugger said a word to me while I did it.

It wasn't till I got home that I came across the blanket again and spotted a tiny tag: 'do not remove from plane.' Course, I felt rotten about pinching it, even by accident. I'll never do it again, obviously, but I still feel dead guilty about doing it this time. Is there some way I can get it back to the airline? Or should I just let it go, lesson learned?

Sorry if it's a silly question, I just sort of panicked when I twigged I'd nicked it by accident!!

Just so you know, the airline was Aer Lingus.

* * *

If you want to stop feeling guilty about it, give the airline a ring and ask what they'd like you to do with the blanket. Tell them you took it by mistake cos you weren't all there when you got off the plane.

Chances are they'll tell you to keep it, cos it'd cost them more to get it back in the post and put it back in the blanket pool than to just buy a new one. And if they've told you straight out to hang onto it, you've got nothing to worry about. Then again, if (less likely) they do want it sent back, you can send it back, and that'll clear any leftover guilt too.

You can find out how to get hold of them at https://www.aerlingus.com/support/contact-us.
