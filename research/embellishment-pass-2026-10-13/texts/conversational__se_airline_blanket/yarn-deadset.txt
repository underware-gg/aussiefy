Nicked the airline's plane blanket by accident; didn't realise it wasn't allowed. What should I do?

I'm dead new to travelling in general. When I flew on a long-haul flight, the airline gave us blankets and pillows. I, having no idea I was meant to leave the blanket behind, took it as I grabbed all my gear, and left. I'd figured that since it was a fairly cheap blanket they were part of the ticket price or something. I wasn't fussed about keeping it, but I was half asleep and freezing and I just grabbed it with my other stuff. No bugger said anything to me as I did it.

It wasn't till I got home that I found the blanket again and spotted a tiny tag that said 'do not remove from plane.' Course, I felt rotten about pinching the blanket by accident. Obviously I'll never do this again, but I still feel flamin' guilty for doing it this time. Is there a way I can give it back to the airline? Or should I just forget about it, having learned my lesson?

Sorry if this is a silly question, I just sort of panicked when I realised I'd nicked it by accident!!

Just so you know, the airline was Aer Lingus.

* * *

So you're not left feeling guilty, you can ring the airline and ask them what they'd like you to do with the blanket. Tell them you took it by mistake because you weren't fully with it when you got off the plane.

Most likely they'll tell you to keep it, because it'd cost them more to get it back in the post and put it back in the blanket pool than it would to just buy a new blanket. But if they've told you straight out to keep it, you won't have to worry about a thing. And if (less likely) they do ask you to send it back, you can send it back, which'll also get rid of any leftover guilt.

You can find out how to get in touch with them at https://www.aerlingus.com/support/contact-us.
