## deadset

- Planned phrase "dead set on" for "committed to" is the right sense; kept it.
- Lint suggestions in the wrong sense: "christen" (SANCTIFY) for "dedicated" in "talented and dedicated" (devoted, not consecrated), and "punters" for "the public" in "serve the public" (punters are customers or gamblers, and it's flippant about a public service). Both fire at every setting.
- With only one planned phrase, the brief gives little to work with for a level-3 letter; the Aussie weight came from my own choices ("top bunch", "fired up", "stoked", "a red-hot go").
- Accepted on the first submit.
