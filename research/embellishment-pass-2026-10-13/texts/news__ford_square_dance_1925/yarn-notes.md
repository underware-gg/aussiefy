## dinkum

- Accepted after 1 submit; the plan was the same four phrases, all fine.
- The dinkum ENGINE_DRAFT puts swearing inside Henry Ford's quote ("Come back? Bloody hell, they're here!", "Fair fuckin' dinkum, what we are trying to do..."), against the news genre's "quoted speech stays as said", and opens with "Crikey, DETROIT, Dec. 13 (AP)" and "Oh, shit, it was reported...".
