The best way to look after yerself when it comes to lightnin' is to steer clear of it altogether. Ya just don't wanna get caught out in a storm. 'Ave a lightnin' safety plan, and pull the pin on things or put 'em off early if thunderstorms are forecast. Keep an eye on the weather and get to a safe spot before it turns nasty. Solid buildings and hard-topped cars are safe bets. Rain shelters, little sheds and open vehicles aren't safe.

When yer inside, don't touch anythin' plugged into a power point, the plumbin', or phones with a cord. Mobiles and cordless phones are right. Also, keep away from outside doors and windows, and don't lie on a garage floor.

Cuttin' the Risk

To keep yer own risk of gettin' hit by lightnin' as low as it'll go, when ya head outside, plan ahead so ya can get to a safe spot quick smart if a thunderstorm's brewin'. If the sky's lookin' nasty or ya hear thunder, get inside a safe spot straight away. Once yer inside, keep off phones with a cord, electrical gear, the plumbin', and windows and doors. Last of all, wait 30 minutes after the last lightnin' or thunder before ya head back outside. If every bloke and sheila stuck to those simple rules, the number of lightnin' casualties in this country could be cut right down.

Lightnin' Victims

If someone gets struck by lightnin', they might need medical help straight away. Lightnin' victims don't carry an electrical charge and are safe to touch. Call 911 and keep an eye on the victim. Start CPR or use an Automated External Defibrillator if needed.
