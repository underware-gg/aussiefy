## About the auction

### What is FOLD Auction 2?

Righto. FOLD Auction 2 was the second public FOLD auction, and it ran on the Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction mechanism (more on that beauty below).

It followed on from the first FOLD Auction, which was held July 8–10, 2026.

Now, Auction 2 was kept to **2% of total FOLD supply**, and that was on purpose, not stinginess. It was held right around the changeover to general FOLD transferability. The whole point was to get FOLD into more people's mitts and help get deeper, longer-term on-chain liquidity off the ground.

### Why was there a second FOLD Auction?

Think of Auction 2 as a little warm-up lap: a limited step for spreading FOLD round before general FOLD transferability came in on Aug 19.

It was small on purpose, only **2% of total FOLD supply**. And it kicked off with a floor that matched the clearing price of the first FOLD Auction, so it picked up right where that one left off.

The auction gave everyone an open, public way of getting a bit more FOLD out there before FOLD became generally transferable, all above board. It gave long-term on-chain liquidity a leg-up while it was at it.

**50% of proceeds were locked in for long-term on-chain liquidity support.** Half the takings, straight in.

Auction 2 wrapped up just before general FOLD transferability got switched on, cutting it fine.

### What happened in the first FOLD Auction?

The first FOLD Auction ran from **July 8–10, 2026**, on the same Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction mechanism.

That one's done and dusted. Its clearing price came in at **0.02154816 USDC / FOLD**, and that same figure's the floor for FOLD Auction 2.

If you had a go in the first auction, you can still get to claiming and post-auction info through the official [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction). It's all still there waiting for ya.

### What is a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction?

A Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) is a price-discovery mechanism for handing out tokens. In plain English, it's how the price gets worked out. It's built on Uniswap infrastructure.

- The auction runs over a string of separate periods, one after the other.
- Participants put their bids in through the official Uniswap CCA interface.
- Bids that get up within a period all clear at the same clearing price. Everyone pays the same.
- For each following period, the minimum price is whichever's higher: the original floor price, or the clearing price of the period before.
- The minimum price can climb in later periods. It won't ever drop below the applicable prior clearing price or the original floor, though.
- Any tokens left over from earlier are still up for grabs in the final period, so it's anyone's game right to the end.

So the CCA hands tokens out bit by bit, at the one price within each period. Why bother? It's set up like that to cut down on gas competition, take the wind out of the snipers who'd jump in at the last second, and let clearing prices find their own level as people get stuck into the auction.

[Read more about Uniswap CCAs](https://cca.uniswap.org).

### Did the auction determine the price of FOLD after transferability?

Nah, nah. The auction sorted out allocations and a clearing price inside the auction itself, and that's where it stopped. It didn't set the market price of FOLD once general transferability kicked in.

And where future market prices go? Can't be predicted. Anyone who reckons they know is having a lend of ya.

## Participation

<Callout type='warning'>
  Auction 2 closed on Aug 19, 2026 at 13:06 UTC. The steps in this section only show how the auction worked, for the record. You can't put in a new bid, so don't go looking for the button.

### How did participation work?

1. Participants headed over to the official [FOLD Auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).
2. They followed the verified link through to the Uniswap CCA interface.
3. They got through the required verification process via the official flow, the proper way.
4. They put their bids in while the auction window was open.
5. Once the auction closed, they could claim any allocated FOLD and unspent USDC through the verified auction interface.

Participants needed USDC on Ethereum Mainnet. And before they jumped in, they had to go over the auction terms and eligibility requirements, the fine print and all.

### Who could participate?

Taking part was open to eligible participants. Mind you, it was subject to jurisdiction, identity verification, AML and sanctions screening, and other applicable restrictions, so it wasn't a free-for-all.

It was the official verification process that decided eligibility, and the auction terms that governed it.

And getting through verification didn't guarantee you eligibility or an allocation, either. Passing the door check isn't the same as getting served.

### Was KYC required?

Yep. Before taking part, participants had to get through identity verification via the official verification process. Every one of them.

That process decided eligibility and did the applicable AML and sanctions screening.

Taking part was subject to the auction terms and applicable restrictions.

## Claiming and transferability

### When can participants claim FOLD?

Claiming was set to open straight after the auction closed. Quick smart.

Participants claim their allocated FOLD and any unspent USDC through the verified auction interface.

Now don't get these two mixed up: claiming and general FOLD transferability are separate things.

### When did general FOLD transferability begin?

FOLD Auction 2 closed Aug 19 at **13:06 UTC (9:06 AM ET)**.

General FOLD transferability could be switched on not long after, at or after **14:00 UTC (10 AM ET)**.

Here's the clever bit. From Unix timestamp **`1787148000`** on, anyone at all could call the `tge()` function on the FOLD contract to switch on general transferability. Anyone. And the TGE transaction went through on Aug 19, 2026 at 14:00:23 UTC, twenty-three seconds past the hour. `tgeTimestamp()` returns `1787148023`. So FOLD is now generally transferable. Job done.

FOLD Auction 2, claiming, general FOLD transferability, and Network Alpha are all separate milestones in the bigger Interfold launch sequence. For the nitty-gritty, check out [FOLD Transferability](/faq/transferability).

## Safety

### How do I know I’m using the official auction?

This is the bit to listen to. Stick to auction, claiming, verification, and support links put out through official Interfold channels, and only those.

The official Interfold channels are:

- [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction)
- [Interfold website](https://www.theinterfold.com/)
- [Interfold X account](https://x.com/theinterfold)
- [Interfold documentation](https://docs.theinterfold.com/)

Don't trust:

- Auction, claiming, or support links that rock up out of the blue in DMs.
- Copied token or contract addresses from unofficial sources.
- Impersonator accounts.
- Private wallet-connection links.
- Anyone asking for your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
- Anyone asking to be paid in exchange for support.

If it smells shonky, it probably is.

  Interfold team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet backup, or access to your wallet. Never ever. Anyone who does isn't one of ours.
</Callout>
