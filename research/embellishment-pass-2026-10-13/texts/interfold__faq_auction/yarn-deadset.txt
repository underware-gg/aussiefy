## About the auction

### What is FOLD Auction 2?

FOLD Auction 2 was the second public FOLD auction. It used the Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction mechanism.

It came after the first FOLD Auction, which ran July 8–10, 2026.

Auction 2 was kept to **2% of total FOLD supply** on purpose. It was held around the switch to general FOLD transferability. The idea was to spread FOLD round more widely and help get deeper, longer-term on-chain liquidity up and running.

### Why was there a second FOLD Auction?

Auction 2 was a limited step for handing out FOLD before general FOLD transferability on Aug 19.

It was kept small on purpose, at **2% of total FOLD supply**. It kicked off with a floor that matched the clearing price of the first FOLD Auction.

The auction gave an open, public way of handing out more FOLD before FOLD became generally transferable. It also backed long-term on-chain liquidity.

**50% of proceeds were set aside for long-term on-chain liquidity support.**

Auction 2 wrapped up just before general FOLD transferability was switched on.

### What happened in the first FOLD Auction?

The first FOLD Auction ran from **July 8–10, 2026**. It used the Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction mechanism.

The first auction is done and dusted. Its clearing price was **0.02154816 USDC / FOLD**, which is also the floor for FOLD Auction 2.

People who took part in the first auction can still get to claiming and post-auction info through the official [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).

### What is a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction?

A Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) is a price-discovery mechanism for handing out tokens. It's built on Uniswap infrastructure.

- The auction runs over a number of separate periods.
- Participants put in bids through the official Uniswap CCA interface.
- Bids that get up within a period clear at the same clearing price.
- The minimum price for each following period is the higher of the original floor price or the clearing price of the period before.
- The minimum price may go up in later periods. It doesn't drop below the applicable prior clearing price or the original floor.
- Any tokens not handed out earlier are still up for grabs in the final period.

The CCA hands out tokens bit by bit and uses the one price within each period. It's built this way to cut down on gas competition, take the sting out of sniping, and let clearing prices settle through people taking part in the auction.

[Read more about Uniswap CCAs](https://cca.uniswap.org).

### Did the auction determine the price of FOLD after transferability?

Nah, nah. The auction settled allocations and a clearing price within the auction itself. It didn't settle the market price of FOLD when general transferability kicked in.

Nobody can tell what future market prices will be.

## Participation

<Callout type='warning'>
  Auction 2 closed on Aug 19, 2026 at 13:06 UTC. The steps in this section show how the auction worked. You can't put in a new bid.
</Callout>

### How did participation work?

1. Participants went to the official [FOLD Auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).
2. They followed the verified link to the Uniswap CCA interface.
3. They got through the required verification process through the official flow.
4. They put in bids while the auction was open.
5. Once the auction closed, they could claim any allocated FOLD and unspent USDC through the verified auction interface.

Participants needed USDC on Ethereum Mainnet. They had to read through the auction terms and eligibility requirements before they had a go.

### Who could participate?

Taking part was open to eligible participants. It was subject to jurisdiction, identity verification, AML and sanctions screening, and other applicable restrictions.

The official verification process decided eligibility. The auction terms governed eligibility.

Getting through verification didn't guarantee eligibility or an allocation.

### Was KYC required?

Yep. Participants had to get through identity verification through the official verification process before they took part.

The verification process decided eligibility and did the applicable AML and sanctions screening.

Taking part was subject to the auction terms and applicable restrictions.

## Claiming and transferability

### When can participants claim FOLD?

Claiming was set to open straight after the auction closed.

Participants claim allocated FOLD and any unspent USDC through the verified auction interface.

Claiming and general FOLD transferability are two separate things.

### When did general FOLD transferability begin?

FOLD Auction 2 closed Aug 19 at **13:06 UTC (9:06 AM ET)**.

General FOLD transferability could be switched on not long after, at or after **14:00 UTC (10 AM ET)**.

At or after Unix timestamp **`1787148000`**, anyone could call the `tge()` function on the FOLD contract to switch on general transferability. The TGE transaction went through on Aug 19, 2026 at 14:00:23 UTC. `tgeTimestamp()` returns `1787148023`. FOLD is now generally transferable.

FOLD Auction 2, claiming, general FOLD transferability, and Network Alpha are separate milestones within the wider Interfold launch sequence. For the details, see [FOLD Transferability](/faq/transferability).

## Safety

### How do I know I’m using the official auction?

Only use auction, claiming, verification, and support links put out through official Interfold channels.

Official Interfold channels are:

- [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction)
- [Interfold website](https://www.theinterfold.com/)
- [Interfold X account](https://x.com/theinterfold)
- [Interfold documentation](https://docs.theinterfold.com/)

Don't trust:

- Auction, claiming, or support links sent to you out of the blue in DMs.
- Copied token or contract addresses from unofficial sources.
- Impersonator accounts.
- Private wallet-connection links.
- Requests for your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
- Requests for payment in exchange for support.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Interfold team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet backup, or access to your wallet.
</Callout>
