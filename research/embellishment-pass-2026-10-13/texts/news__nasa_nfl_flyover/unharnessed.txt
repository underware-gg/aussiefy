As part of the agency's Inspiration Tour flyover, NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman is gonna be at the stick of one of three F-5 fighter jets to kick off the New York Jets vs. Cleveland Browns game in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on Sunday, Oct. 11. Not a bad way to start the footy, eh?

The NASA crew will be having a yarn with the fans at the agency's Experience Zone, set up at the Jets Tailgate Zone outside the stadium, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. EDT before the game. Punters can find out more about NASA heading back to the Moon under the agency's Artemis dome, have a crack at some interactive games, and grab a few happy snaps with a bloody big inflatable SLS (Space Launch System) rocket.

With stops right across the country, NASA's Inspiration Tour rounds up the academic, industry and public sector mob to hook the agency up with the people, technologies and organisations driving American leadership in space.

The tour wraps up with a bang at MAX POWER, a public expo of American air and space know-how, on Saturday, Nov. 7, and Sunday, Nov. 8, on and around the agency's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Held to mark America's big 250th birthday, the multi-day, family-friendly shindig will show off the next-generation aircraft, spacecraft, autonomous vehicles and technologies that'll help shape the future of getting about in air and space.
