As part of the agency's Inspiration Tour flyover, NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman will fly one of three F-5 fighter jets to kick off the New York Jets vs. Cleveland Browns gridiron game in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on Sunday, Oct. 11.

NASA's crew will have a yarn with fans at the agency's Experience Zone, set up at the Jets Tailgate Zone out the front of the stadium, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. EDT before the game. Fans can find out more about NASA heading back to the Moon under the agency's Artemis dome, have a go at the interactive games, and grab a happy snap with a whopping blow-up SLS (Space Launch System) rocket.

With stops right round the country, NASA's Inspiration Tour gets academic, industry and public sector players together to hook the agency up with the people, technologies and organisations that keep America out in front in space.

The tour will wind up with MAX POWER, a public expo of American air and space know-how, on Saturday, Nov. 7, and Sunday, Nov. 8, on and round the agency's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Held to mark America's historic 250th anniversary, the multi-day, family-friendly do will show off the next lot of aircraft, spacecraft, driverless vehicles and technologies that'll help shape the future of getting about in air and space.
