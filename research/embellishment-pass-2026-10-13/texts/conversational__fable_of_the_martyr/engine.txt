Once in a Country Town there was a Man with a Weak Back.

He could put a Grindstone into a Farm Wagon if any one wanted to bet him the Segars, but every time he lifted an Ax, something caught him right in the Spine and he had to go into the House and lie down. So his Wife took Boarders and did the Cooking herself.

Mind you, he was willing to divide the Labor, however; so he did the Marketing. Only, when he had bought the Victuals, he would squat on a Shoe-Box with the Basket between his Legs and say that he couldn't see what Parliament House wuz thinkin' of.

Now, he had certain Theories in regard to the Alaskan Boundary and he was against any Anglo-American Alliance becuz Uncle Sam could take care of himself at any Turn in the Road, comin' right down to it, and the American People wuz superior to any other Naytionality in every Way, Shape, Manner and Form, as fur as that's concerned. Then his missus would have to send Word for him to come on with the Groceries so she could get tea.

Nearly Everybody Sympathized with her, because she had to put up with such a bloody big Hulk of a no-account Husband. Well, she was looked upon as a Martyr.

One Day the Husband was Sunstruck, being too Lazy to move into the Shade, and next Day he Passed Away without an Effort. The Widow gave him the best Funeral of the Year and then put all the folding stuff she could rake and scrape into a Marble Shaft marked "At Rest."

A good many People said she was Better Off without him, and it was defo a Good Riddance of Bad Rubbish.

Now, they hoped that if she ever Married again she'd pick out some bugger that wuzn't afraid to Work, and had nous enough to pound Sand into a Rat-Hole.

There was General Satisfaction when she became the Wife of Mr. Gladden, who owned the General Store. Struth, he knocked together a brand-spankin'-new House, hired a Girl and had the Washing sent out. She could go into the Store and pick out Anything she wanted, and he took her riding in his new Runabout every Evening.

Mind you, Consequently, she was down in the dumps, thinking of the Jewel she had lost.

MORAL: _If the Woman reckons he's All Right, you keep on your own Side of the Fence._
