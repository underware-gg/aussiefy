yarn-dinkum: A natural, faithful level-4 retelling that keeps the guide's practical tone.
  minor: 'Pipe tape, also called teflon tape, you can get at most hardware shops;' (for 'Pipe tape, also called teflon tape, is available at most hardware stores'): Fronted object is a little awkward.

yarn-troppo: The liveliest and most authentic level-4 voice, keeping every fact; the exclamatory asides are slightly overused.
  minor: "Not bad for an arvo's tinkering." (added): Implies the fixes take an afternoon, a small claim the original does not make.
  minor: 'Every year!' (added): One of several exclamatory asides that become a tic.

yarn-deadset: Clean, faithful and natural, with helpful metric conversions, but closer to level 3 than 4.
  minor: 'Leak detection and flow monitoring gadgets can help you find leaks or tip you off to odd water use at home' (for 'Leak detection and flow monitoring devices can help you identify leaks or alert you to irregular water use in a home'): Close to the original phrasing; the text sits lighter than the level-4 brief overall.

unharnessed: A decent level-4 retelling, faithful, but with a scatter of American and British forms.
  minor: 'save you water and a bit of coin' (for 'save water and money'): "Coin" for money is American.
  minor: 'to help folks get their heads around' (for 'to help consumers understand'): "Folks" is American.
  minor: "first you've gotta work out" (for 'you first need to determine'): "Gotta" spelling reads American.
  minor: "That's enough water for more than 180 showers, strewth!" (for "That's the amount of water needed to take more than 180 showers!"): "Strewth" tacked on at the end feels bolted on.
  minor: 'Toilet Leaks' (for 'Toilet Leaks'): Keeps "toilet" in headings while using "dunny" in the body, inconsistently.

engine: Mostly the untouched original with a few word swaps, several of them in the wrong sense.
  major: 'Give a stuff about the width of the flapper' (for 'Pay attention to the width of the flapper'): "Give a stuff" is used in the negative ("couldn't give a stuff"); as a positive instruction it reads wrong and mildly rude.
  major: 'wash 60 heaps of dishes in your dishwasher' (for 'wash 60 loads of dishes in your dishwasher'): "Loads" (dishwasher cycles) swapped for "heaps", which changes the measure.
  minor: 'to ensure a beaut seal' (for 'to ensure a good seal'): "Beaut" strains in technical advice.
  minor: 'If any colour rocks up in the bowl' (for 'If any color shows up in the bowl'): "Rocks up" is for people arriving, forced for dye appearing.
  major: 'Leak detection and flow monitoring devices can help you identify leaks or alert you to irregular water use in a home' (for 'Leak detection and flow monitoring devices can help you identify leaks or alert you to irregular water use in a home'): Largely untranslated, far below the level-4 brief.
