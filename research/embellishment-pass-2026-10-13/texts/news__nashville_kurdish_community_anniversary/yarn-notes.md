- Wrong-sense planned phrase: "fronted up" for "arrived" (families arriving in the U.S. in 1976). Fronting up is presenting yourself to face something; migrants landing in a country aren't. Declined.
- "The Big Smoke" for "The city" (Nashville) is fine sense-wise, though it carries a bush-eye-view the original doesn't have.
- Lookup "important, significant" came back wrong: only "up yourself" and "tickets on yourself" (self-importance), nothing for important/major. Had to fall back on plain "big hub", "pullin' its weight".
- Lint suggestions "mitts" for hands and "heaps" for plenty were useful. Accepted first submit.

## dinkum
