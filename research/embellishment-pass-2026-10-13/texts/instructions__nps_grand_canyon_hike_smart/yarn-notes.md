## deadset
- All four planned phrases had the right sense ("Bushwalking", "bushwalkers", "arvo", "ripper"). The engine applied "Bushwalking" to only some of the "hiking"s, so its draft switched between hike and bushwalk.
- Lint suggested "esky" for "cooler" in "your body stays cooler". Wrong sense: that "cooler" is the comparative adjective, not the cool box. It also offered "clear out"/"shoot through" for "Get going" (setting off on a walk), which is the wrong sense; those mean leave or run off.
- Lint suggestions that helped: "stay out of strife" for "staying out of trouble", and "heaps" for "a lot". The `repeated_phrase` warning on "keep your energy up" ×4 was fair.
- 16 of the sentences were listed as difficult (hydration, nutrition, essential...). That was useful: it is where the Aussie wording has to come from the writer.

## dinkum
- The engine draft tacked insults onto two instructions: "even if you don't feel thirsty, ya flamin' mongrel" and "soak your clothing and body, ya old bastard". The instructions genre says "no insults aimed at the reader", and "bastard" in a safety notice is wrong anyway. I left both out. Neither was a planned phrase, so I had nothing to decline.
- The openings for an instructions text were "Fair dinkum, this happened." and "(start with a line of dialogue)". Neither fits a how-to; I didn't use one at dinkum.
- Lint suggested "beaut" for "good" three times. I took it once ("a beaut walk").

## troppo
- Lookups that came back empty or wrong, all for everyday Australian phrases: "save your bacon" (empty); "wringing wet" (returned "down in the dumps"); "works a treat" (returned "{subj} {be} {poss} shout"); "the go" as in "salty snacks are the go" (only "go off", "fair go" and the like).
- Lint again offered "esky" for the adjective "cooler", and "have a barney" for "Can't argue with that", which is the wrong sense (an idiom of agreement, not a quarrel).
- Troppo against instructions was clear enough: the colour went into reactions and the connecting prose ("It's like walking into an oven", "Don't be one of them"), and every number, time and step stayed exact.
