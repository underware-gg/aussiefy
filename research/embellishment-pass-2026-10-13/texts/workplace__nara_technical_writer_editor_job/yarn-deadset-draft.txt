Here are the duties of this job at the GS-12. If you're picked at a lower grade level, you'll get the chance to learn to do all these duties, and you'll get training to help you grow in the job.

As a TECHNICAL WRITER-EDITOR, you will:

Properly handle the contents of documents and of Federal Register publications so they're published in a way that's legally effective, on time and accurate.

Manage work, whether done on your own, as part of a team, or leading others, to get all documents published on time and accurately and to meet all program goals.

Understand and properly look after customers' needs across every part of the Federal Register publication process, in carrying out the Office of the Federal Register's program responsibilities.

Understand and properly use the technical resources available for, and tied to, the Federal Register publication processes, including IT, legacy systems and other technical resources.

Qualifications

You must meet the following requirements by the closing date of this announcement.

Specialised Experience: For the GS-09, you must have one year of specialised experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equal to the GS-07 grade level in the Federal service. Specialised experience for this job includes:

Editing legal or regulatory documents written by others to make sure they stick to style and formatting requirements before they're prepared for publication; AND

Spotting and analysing production problems and coming up with fixes for them; AND

Getting information across effectively and diplomatically to internal and/or external audiences to further program goals.

OR

EDUCATION: A master's or equivalent graduate degree or 2 full years of progressively higher level graduate education leading to such a degree or LL.B. or J.D., if related.

OR

COMBINATION OF EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: Note: Only graduate study beyond the first year (18 semester hours) counts as qualifying for the GS-9.

NOTE: Undergraduate and Graduate Education: Must have included a total of 15 semester hours in a relevant scientific, technical, or social science field(s), and at least one course above the introductory level in the field(s) the position covers. For technical manuals and specifications writers or editors, the equivalent of 15 semester hours may have been gained through vocational or educational training above high school level at a public, private, or Armed Forces school.
