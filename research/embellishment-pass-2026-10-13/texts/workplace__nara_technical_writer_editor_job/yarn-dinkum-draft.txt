Here's what the job involves at the GS-12. If you get picked at a lower grade level, no worries: you'll get the chance to learn to do all of it, and there's training to help you grow into the role.

As a TECHNICAL WRITER-EDITOR, you'll:

Handle the contents of documents and of Federal Register publications properly, so they get published legally effective, on time and spot on.

Manage the work, whether you're doing it on your own, pitching in as part of a team, or leading others, so every document gets published on time and accurately and all the program goals get ticked off.

Get your head around what customers need across every part of the Federal Register publication process, and look after them properly, as part of carrying out the Office of the Federal Register's program responsibilities.

Know your way around the technical resources available for, and tied to, the Federal Register publication processes, including IT, legacy systems and other technical resources, and use them properly.

Qualifications

You've got to meet the following requirements by the closing date of this announcement.

Specialised Experience: For the GS-09, you need one year of specialised experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equal to the GS-07 grade level in the Federal service. Specialised experience for this job includes:

Editing legal or regulatory documents written by other people to make sure they stick to the style and formatting requirements before they're prepared for publication; AND

Spotting and nutting out production problems and coming up with fixes for them; AND

Getting information across well and tactfully to internal and/or external audiences to help meet program goals.

OR

EDUCATION: A master's or equivalent graduate degree or 2 full years of progressively higher level graduate education leading to such a degree or LL.B. or J.D., if related.

OR

COMBINATION OF EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: Note: Only graduate study past the first year (18 semester hours) counts towards qualifying for the GS-9.

NOTE: Undergraduate and Graduate Education: Must have included a total of 15 semester hours in a relevant scientific, technical, or social science field(s), and at least one course above the introductory level in the field(s) the position covers. For technical manuals and specifications writers or editors, the equivalent of 15 semester hours can come from vocational or educational training above high school level at a public, private, or Armed Forces school.
