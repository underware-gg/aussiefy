## deadset

- Wrong-sense planned phrase (borderline): "little fellas" for "small children" in "Pick up small children immediately". A safety step covers every small child, and "little fellas" reads as boys (or pups) and a pet name. Declined; I used the neutral "littlies" (the dictionary has "littlie").
- "tucker" for "food" planned only at "Getting your food" and "accessing your food"; the engine left "allow the bear access to your food" alone. I'd put "tucker" there as well, then changed it back to "food" so I wasn't working a phrase in somewhere it wasn't planned. The rule is unclear on whether another occurrence of the same word counts as "elsewhere".
- No "bushwalk" planned for "Hike". Lookup "hike"/"bushwalk" gave only "a walking track"/"a bushwalker" (nouns); the verb "bushwalk" is missing.
- Lookups wrong: "stay put" returned "keep your trap shut", "an ice brick", "work back"; "keep an eye on" returned "an ice brick"; "back off" returned "bludge", "wag school"; "run away" returned "nick off"/"rack off"/"wag school"/"chuck out". Empty: "bear", "backpack", "hold your ground", "ranger", "high-pitched".
- Lint's "heaps" for "go up a lot" fit. Accepted on the first submit. The original's "ﬁ"/"ﬂ" ligatures written as plain letters raised no complaints.

## troppo

- First lint: `too_vulgar` on "bugger" ("every poor bugger") and "arse" ("go arse over"). Both were fair catches: the troppo guidance's examples ("bastards", "prick", "bloody") pull you over vulgarity 0, and nothing tells you to tone them down at nan.
- Lookup "Buckley's" came back empty at level 2 with the note "No entry ... Consider proposing it", but it's in the dictionary at level 3 ("Buckley's chance"). The note should say it exists above the level. The style guide itself uses "reckoning it had Buckley's" as an example in a level 2 brief. I took it out.
- "sticky beak" was empty; only the verb "stickybeak around" is in, not the noun "a stickybeak" (have a stickybeak). "twig" (= realise) was empty. "a feed" was there.
- "Safety comes before colour" was clear; I kept every DO NOT step and the duplicated "loud noises" warning in order, and the colour stuck to images (sideways "like a crab", the pack as "armour").
- Accepted on the first submit (after the two lint fixes), at 885 vs 729 words.
