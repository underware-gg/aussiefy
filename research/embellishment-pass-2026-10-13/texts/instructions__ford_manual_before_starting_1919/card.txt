yarn-troppo: A lively, full-weight mechanic's retelling that keeps every instruction, with a few stock and jokey bolt-ons.
  minor: 'before ya so much as touch the starter' (for 'Before trying to start the car'): Invents a starter; the original only says before starting.
  minor: 'Bring it down a notch at a time, notch by notch' (for 'it should be moved down notch by notch'): Says the same thing twice.
  minor: "and they do all the talkin'" (added): Odd figure for two levers; strains.
  minor: "like it's tellin' ya off" (for 'a dull knock will be noticed in the engine'): Jokey aside slightly lightens a warning sign.

yarn-dinkum: Clean, natural and accurate, a touch lighter than A.
  minor: "For the first few days yer drivin' a brand-spankin'-new car" (for 'During the first few days that a new car is being driven'): Stock phrase.
  minor: 'Get down under the car and in the flywheel casing' (for 'Down under the car in the flywheel casing'): Stays close to the manual's lines in places; a shade light for level 5.

yarn-deadset: Faithful and natural, with good conversions and a couple of slightly off phrases.
  minor: 'leave a gunk of sediment' (for 'deposit sediment'): Unnatural phrase.
  minor: "Pushin' this lever forward" (for 'The advancing of this lever'): Forward contradicts the down-toward-the-driver movement the next clause describes.

unharnessed: Accurate, smooth and natural, with well-placed swearing on the warnings, but lighter than the level-5 brief.
  minor: 'medium-light, top-quality gas engine oil' (for 'medium light, high-grade gas engine oil'): gas left in; petrol engine oil would be the Aussie form.
  major: 'The spark lever should usually go in about the third or fourth notch of the quadrant' (for 'The spark lever should usually be put in about the third or fourth notch of the quadrant'): Much of the text is plain English, short of the level-5 yarn asked for.

engine: The original with dropped g's and a couple of flamin's bolted on; faithful but barely Aussie and clunky.
  major: 'Upon receipt of the car see that a supply of medium light, high-grade gas engine oil is poured' (for 'Upon receipt of the car see that a supply of medium light, high-grade gas engine oil is poured'): Left untranslated; most of the text is the original.
  major: "After the engine has become flamin' limbered up" (for 'After the engine has become thoroughly limbered up'): Intensifier swapped in for thoroughly; wrong register, jarring.
  minor: "the vapour is flamin' volatile'n travels rapidly" (for 'the vapor is extremely volatile and travels rapidly'): Clunky contraction bolted onto formal prose.
  minor: 'What about Gasoline?' (for 'What about Gasoline?'): US Gasoline left in the heading though petrol is used in the body; carburetor left in US spelling.
