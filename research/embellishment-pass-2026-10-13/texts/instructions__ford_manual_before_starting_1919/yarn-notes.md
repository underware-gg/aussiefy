## deadset

- Accepted after 2 submits. The rejection: "a beaut is in, but every "a good" it was planned for is still there". The "a good" left was my own, for "it is advisable" ("it's a good idea to strain it"), not the original's "a good plan" (which I'd replaced with "a beaut idea"). The moved check counts "a good" in the draft, not what it stands for; a false positive when the writer brings the words in elsewhere.
- Wrong-sense in the engine draft (not the plan): "flamin' limbered up" for "thoroughly limbered up"; "flamin'" was planned for "extremely" (volatile) and that's where I kept it. The draft also keeps "gas engine oil", which means petrol engine oil here, and "outta".
- Lint: "ya'll" (for "you'll") flagged as US dialect, fair, it reads as y'all. "Gasoline" warned as a dropped name; it's the capitalised heading word ("What about Gasoline?"), localised to Petrol on purpose. Lint suggested "come hell or high water" for "no matter what" in a safety line; too jokey for the warning, left it.
- Units: kept "three gallons" and "ten-gallon" with litres in brackets (US gallons: about 11 and 38 litres); the brief's rule is clear on this.

## dinkum

- Accepted after 1 submit. Wrong-sense plan: "fuckin'" for "thoroughly" ("thoroughly limbered up" = well run in), a swear standing in for a degree adverb in a manual's oiling step; declined. ("dead" for "extremely" volatile was fine.)
- The dinkum ENGINE_DRAFT shows the failure mode in an instructions text: "Stone the flamin' crows, what must be done...", "Jeez Louise, the water supply should be replenished", "Bugger me, all other parts...", "superior to bloody hard water", and an insult, "no matter what some drongo reckons", which the genre forbids ("no insults aimed at the reader").
- OPENINGS for a car manual: "You'll like this one.", "Ever hear the one about ...?" are yarn openers and wrong for instructions at dinkum; I ignored them and opened with "First things first:" on the first step.
- Lint: "Gasoline" dropped-name warning again (heading word). Suggested "mitts" for "hands" in "beats hard water hands down" (an idiom; only the other "hands" took it), and "the whole box and dice" for "Everything else", which drops the "else".

## troppo

- Accepted after 1 submit. "fuckin'" for "thoroughly" was planned again; at troppo it's only vocabulary, and I left it out (wrong sense, and a manual's step).
- The setting vs the genre: troppo says "perform it at the pub", instructions say "safety before colour, never make light of a hazard". In practice the colour went on the routine steps ("nice 'n easy", "a dull knock... like it's tellin' ya off") and the petrol warnings got firmer instead ("Now here's where ya don't muck about", "end of story"). The brief could say that outright: troppo on instructions means a mechanic's voice, not jokes on the warnings.
- OPENINGS still offer "You'll like this one." / "Ever hear the one about ...?"; I took "(start straight in with the action)".
- Lint: only the "Gasoline" dropped-name warning (the heading word) and "reckon" for "think". I cut an addition of my own ("Fine as a hair, some of 'em", about the tubes) because it adds a fact; lint's length check wouldn't catch that kind of thing.
