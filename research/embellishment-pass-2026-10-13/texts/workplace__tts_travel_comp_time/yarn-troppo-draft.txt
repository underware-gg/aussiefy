## Travel comp time, for everyone

Righto, travel comp time. Say you've been approved to travel for work, and the travelling happens outside your regular working hours. Good news: you can put in for travel comp time. Now, it's for “temporary duty,” also known as “TDY” travel, _not_ local travel, and not your daily commute either, so don't get any ideas. TDY travel runs more than 12 hours, and you need a pre-approved authorisation in Concur before you go.

And it's for everyone, too. There are no limits on earning travel comp time based on salary level or schedule type. Top of the ladder or bottom, same deal.

If you want the full rundown, have a squiz at OPM’s [Travel Comp Time Fact Sheet](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/pay-administration/fact-sheets/compensatory-time-off-for-travel/).

### Understanding travel comp time

So here's the thing. Any travel during your usual work day counts as regular hours, and that includes the trip from your place to the airport. Sitting in traffic on the way is just regular hours, nothing extra, sorry.

As a rule, travel comp time is the “gap filler”. It's for when you're travelling outside your regular work hours on a regular work day, but you're not officially “performing work.”

Current GSA staff who want to get into the nuts and bolts can check the [Compensation for Government Travel slidedeck](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RFSq_4KdMza_pkcHGp9Hy2RNR2ryLs0b/view?usp=sharing) to work out how your hours break down. Still scratching your head? Sing out to your Timekeeper or an HR Specialist at your agency. That's what they're there for.

### Calculating travel comp time

Right, the maths. When you're working out travel comp time hours, travel ends the minute you check in at the hotel or walk in your front door. The hours are based on elapsed time, and you work that out using the time zone you left from, not the one you landed in.

To get your travel time right, we recommend keeping a log of:

- When you left home and got to the airport
- When your flights actually took off and landed
- When you left the destination airport and got to your hotel

Jot it all down as you go and you won't be guessing later.

### Using travel comp time

Here's the catch: travel comp time doesn't keep. It runs out one year (26 pay periods) after you earn it. Hit the expiry date and you forfeit the leave, gone. And if you leave GSA, any travel comp time you haven't used is forfeited as well. So use it or lose it.
