yarn-troppo: The fullest and most varied retelling, accurate and helpfully converted, slightly over-tagged.
  minor: "Pull up a stool, here's the weather." (added): A staged pub framing that renders nothing.
  minor: 'Rug up.' (added): One of several closing tags ("Dig the jumper out.", "Hang onto your hat.") that turn into a formula.

yarn-dinkum: Smooth, natural and accurate, with helpful conversions; a shade lighter than C.
  minor: 'will back deeper mixing and so gusty NW winds' (for 'will support deeper mixing and thus gusty NW winds'): Some technical stretches left close to the original; a little light for level 4.

yarn-deadset: Accurate, natural and well converted, but on the light side of level 4.
  minor: 'The tighter gradient and CAA to help steepen lapse rates will back deeper mixing and so gusty NW winds.' (for 'The tighter gradient and CAA to help steepen lapse rates will support deeper mixing and thus gusty NW winds.'): Long technical stretches barely retold; noticeably lighter than level 4.
  minor: 'a light shower/a few spits at most' (for 'at most a light shower/sprinkle'): The slash join reads awkwardly with the new idiom.

unharnessed: A good-weight retelling marred by one bolted-on stock simile and a repeated strained "back up".
  major: "because the lower levels are drier than a dead dingo's donger" (for 'due to the drier lower levels'): A stock pub simile bolted on that exaggerates a comparative into bone dry and jars in a technical discussion.
  minor: 'will back up deeper mixing' (for 'will support deeper mixing'): "back up" for physical support strains and is repeated three times.
  minor: 'highs in the 70s in plenty of spots' (for 'high temperatures in the 70s in many areas'): Fahrenheit left unconverted; an Australian reader would not read 70s as warm without a Celsius figure.

engine: Barely translated, and its few swaps take the wrong sense and garble technical terms.
  critical: 'An esky airmass will also have moved in' (for 'A cooler airmass will also have moved in'): "cooler" (comparative of cool) taken as a cool box; the line is nonsense.
  critical: 'northern creek shortwave trough' (for 'northern stream shortwave trough'): The jet-stream branch rendered as a watercourse; wrong sense of a technical term.
  critical: 'RH paddocks show residual moisture' (for 'RH fields show residual moisture'): Model fields rendered as farmland.
  major: 'Highs will likely do it tough to get out of the 40s' (for 'Highs will likely struggle to get out of the 40s'): "do it tough" is about people enduring hardship; it does not take an infinitive and does not fit temperatures.
  major: 'a warm fall day' (for 'a warm fall day'): Left untranslated for a level-4 brief, US "fall" kept.
