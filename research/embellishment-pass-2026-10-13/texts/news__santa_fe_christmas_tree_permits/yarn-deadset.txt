Chrissie tree permits will be available to buy online through Recreation.gov and in person at all Santa Fe National Forest District Offices and the Supervisor's Office from November 13, 2025. Details about designated cutting areas, dates, and types of trees that may be cut can be found here.

Buy your Chrissie tree permit:

Online through Recreation.gov, and search for Santa Fe National Forest Christmas Tree Permits. It's important to read the overview and need-to-know information carefully before buying the permit. Visitors will also need to set up or log in to a Recreation.gov account to finish the transaction.

In person at the Supervisor's Office in Santa Fe, N.M., or the Jemez, Cuba, Coyote, Española, and Pecos/Las Vegas District Offices. Contact us for current opening hours.

Permits will be sold in person at the Walatowa Visitor Center only on Saturdays from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. from November 22 to December 20, 2024.

Pre-cut Chrissie trees may be available to buy at district offices. Availability depends on the weather and supply, with priority given to people who can't cut their own tree.

The SFNF's non-refundable Chrissie tree permits are $10. Permits bought online through recreation.gov will include an extra $2.50 service fee. One tag is valid for a tree up to 5 inches (13 cm) in diameter and 10 feet (3 m) in height. Trees taller than 10 feet and/or wider than 5 inches need an extra tag. For example, a 15-foot (4.6 m) tree needs two tags.

Year 4 kids with an Every Kid Outdoors pass are eligible for a free Chrissie tree permit and can apply by entering the pass or voucher number when buying a permit (note: a $2.50 service fee will be applied).

Cutting a Chrissie tree also improves the health of the bush. The permit system helps thin out thick stands of small-diameter trees. Local forest health experts pick out areas that benefit from thinning trees that tend to be the perfect size for Chrissie trees. Removing these trees in designated areas helps other trees grow bigger and can open up areas that provide feed for wildlife.
