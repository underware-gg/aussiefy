## Calibration and integration limits

- Attested term, selected meaning, Australian use, historical date, present commonness and `reads_as` are different claims. A British header is not proof of non-Australian use; equally, an absent Australian qualifier is not invented. A historical Australian newspaper or novel does not establish current commonness.
- First dates are recorded only with sense/form/witness scope. Publisher copyright years, article publication dates and an author's reported earliest-find are not absolute first attestations.
- Partial/support rows require assessment review. A root meaning can help research a full mapping without attesting it; a noun does not automatically license a verb; singular/plural, possessive and preposition changes can matter. Missing fields do not certify defaults.
- Wiktionary and user dictionaries remain user-edited witnesses; unvetted travel/lifestyle lists are labelled publisher-claim-only. Their region/origin claims are not automatically authoritative. No-find does not justify fabricated/bulldust status.
- `independence_group` prevents obvious double counting: Dennis editions/transcriptions; Paterson same poem; KoalaNet reproduced in GP guide; Wordnik reproducing Wiktionary; Dictionary.com reproducing Collins; scholarly/blog articles quoting Macquarie. Groups are conservative aids and still need final source-family/weight calibration. Reproduced historical quotations were not relabelled independently inspected original scans.
- This pass settles disposition coverage, not every citation weakness or the product's evidence thresholds. Replacing blocked references, checking191 blocked-only IDs, finding full-construction witnesses, applying scoped metadata and resolving flagged mappings remain separate review work.
