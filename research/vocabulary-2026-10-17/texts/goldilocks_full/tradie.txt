Once there was a little girl called Goldilocks. One morning she went for a wander in the woods. She kept walking for ages, till she was dragging her feet and her belly was growling like a mixer full of rocks.

After a bit she found a little house in the middle of the forest. She gave the door a knock. No answer. So she opened it and let herself in.

There were three bowls of porridge on the kitchen table. Goldilocks was starving, so she reckoned she'd give them a go.

She tried the first bowl. “Bloody hell, this porridge is too hot!”

She tried the second. “This one's too cold!”

Then she tried the third. “That's the ticket!” she said, happy as anything, and scraped the bowl clean.

Having had a feed, she wanted to park her backside. Three chairs stood in the living room. She sat on the first. “This chair's too big!” Onto the second. “This one's too hard!” Then the third. “Now we're talking!” But she'd barely settled her bum before the thing came apart like a flat-pack in a cyclone, dumping her on the floor.

Goldilocks was out on her feet by then, so upstairs she went to the bedroom. Three beds. The first was too hard when she lay on it; the second was too soft. The third was just right. She pulled up the blanket and was dead to the world.

Soon the three bears came home from their walk.

“Someone's been into my porridge!” said Father Bear in his great booming voice.

“Someone's been into my porridge!” said Mother Bear.

“Someone's been into my porridge, and they've scoffed the whole bloody lot!” cried Baby Bear.

The bears went into the living room.

“Someone's been sitting in my chair!” growled Father Bear, getting cranky.

“Someone's been sitting in my chair!” said Mother Bear.

“Someone's been sitting in my chair, and they've smashed it to bits!” cried Baby Bear.

The bears were ropeable now. They headed upstairs to the bedroom.

“Someone's been sleeping in my bed!” said Father Bear.

“Someone's been sleeping in my bed!” said Mother Bear.

“Someone's been sleeping in my bed, and she's still here!” cried Baby Bear.

Right then Goldilocks woke up. Three bears looming over her bed put the absolute wind up her. “Help!” she screamed. She shot out of bed, down the stairs and out of the house as fast as her legs would carry her. Didn't even stop to get her shoes on.

Goldilocks never went back to that house in the woods. Once was plenty.
