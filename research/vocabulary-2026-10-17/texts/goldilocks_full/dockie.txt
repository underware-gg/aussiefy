There was once a little girl called Goldilocks. One morning she went for a walk in the woods and kept going a fair old while, till her legs were about done and her stomach was growling like a dog behind a fence.

After a bit she came across a small house right in the middle of the forest. She knocked, got no answer, then opened the door and let herself in.

On the kitchen table were three bowls of porridge. She was hungry as hell, so she decided she'd give them a try.

She tried the first bowl. “Strewth, that's too hot!” she said.

She tried the second. “That's too cold!”

Then she tried the third. “Now you're talking! That's just right!” And she tucked in and scraped the bowl clean.

Once she'd eaten, she wanted to sit down. Three chairs in the living room. She tried the first. “Too big, this one!” Then the second. “Hard as a bloody church pew!” Then the third. “That's more like it. Just right.” But she'd barely settled her backside when the chair went to pieces underneath her and dumped her on the floor.

Goldilocks was properly knocked up by now, so she went upstairs to the bedroom. Three beds there. She lay on the first, but it was too hard. The second was too soft. Then she tried the third, and that was just right. She pulled up the blanket and was out like a light.

Soon the three bears got back from their walk.

“Someone's been eating my porridge!” boomed Father Bear.

“Someone's been eating my porridge!” said Mother Bear.

“Someone's been eating my porridge, and they've scoffed the bloody lot!” cried Baby Bear.

Into the living room went the bears.

“Someone's been sitting in my chair!” growled Father Bear, getting his back up.

“Someone's been sitting in my chair!” said Mother Bear.

“Someone's been sitting in my chair, and they've smashed it to bits!” cried Baby Bear.

They were all getting pretty steamed up now. Upstairs they went to the bedroom.

“Someone's been sleeping in my bed!” said Father Bear.

“Someone's been sleeping in my bed!” said Mother Bear.

“Someone's been sleeping in my bed, and she's still here!” cried Baby Bear.

Just then Goldilocks woke. There were three bears standing over her, and she was scared witless. “Help!” she screamed. Out of bed, down the stairs and out of the house she flew, going like the clappers. Didn't even stop to put her shoes on.

She never went near that house in the woods again.
