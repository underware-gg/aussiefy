When things get to the point where one people has to cut the political ties joining it to another, and take its own separate, equal place among the powers of the world—a place the laws of nature and nature's God entitle it to—common bloody respect for everyone else's opinion means laying out why the split has become necessary.

We reckon these truths are plain as the nose on your face: all men are created equal, and their Creator has given them certain rights nobody can take off them. Among those are life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness. Governments get set up among people to keep those rights safe, and whatever rightful power they have comes from the say-so of the people being governed. Whenever any government starts wrecking those purposes, the people have the right to change it or scrap it and build a new one, with principles and an arrangement of powers they reckon will best look after their safety and happiness. Now, you don't rip out a government that's stood for years over every passing gripe or bit of minor bullshit. Good sense says that, and experience shows people generally put up with trouble while it's still bearable rather than pull down the arrangements they're used to. But when there's a long, unbroken run of abuses and power grabs, all heading for the same rotten end—getting people completely under a despot's boot—then throwing that government off is their right and their duty. They have to put new safeguards in place for their future security. That's the crap these Colonies have patiently put up with, and that's why they've now got no choice but to change their former systems of government. The present King of Great Britain's record is one injury and power grab after another, all aimed squarely at setting up absolute tyranny over these States.
