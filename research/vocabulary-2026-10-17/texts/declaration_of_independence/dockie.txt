When human affairs get to the point where one people has to cut the political ties binding it to another, and take its own separate, equal place among the powers of the earth—a place the laws of Nature and Nature's God entitle it to—common decency towards the rest of humanity means explaining just what has brought about the split.

We reckon these truths stand plain as day: all men are created equal; their Creator gives them certain rights nobody can rightfully take away, including Life, Liberty and the pursuit of Happiness. Governments are set up among people to keep those rights safe, and whatever rightful power they have comes from the say-so of the people they govern. Whenever any kind of government starts wrecking those purposes, the people have the right to change it or chuck it out and set up a new one, built on the principles and arranged in the way they think most likely to secure their Safety and Happiness. Now, a bit of sense tells you not to pull down a government that's stood a long time over every passing gripe or flash in the pan. Experience shows people would sooner put up with troubles they can bear than tear up the arrangements they're used to. But when abuse after abuse and power-grab after power-grab all point the same way, showing a plan to grind people down under absolute despotism, then throwing off that government is their right and their duty. They've got to put fresh safeguards in place for their future security. That's what these Colonies have patiently copped, and that's why they're now obliged to change their old systems of government. The present King of Great Britain has a record of repeated injuries and stolen powers, all aimed straight at setting up absolute tyranny over these States. To make that case, we'll put the facts before a fair-minded world.
