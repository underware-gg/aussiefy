Sometimes, in the course of human affairs, one lot of people has to cut the political ties binding them to another. They have to take their own separate, equal place among the powers of the world—the place the Laws of Nature and Nature's God entitle them to. And if you've got a decent respect for what the rest of humanity thinks, you owe them an explanation of what drove you to make that break.

We reckon these truths are plain as the nose on your face: all men are created equal. Their Creator has given them certain rights that no bastard gets to take away, including Life, Liberty and the pursuit of Happiness. Governments are set up among people to protect those rights, and whatever proper power they have comes from the consent of the people being governed. Whenever a form of government starts wrecking the very things it's meant to protect, the people have the right to change it or get rid of it, and put a new government in its place. They can lay its foundations on the principles, and arrange its powers in the way, they reckon will best secure their Safety and Happiness.

Now, common sense says you don't chuck a long-established government overboard because of some little annoyance that'll blow over by Tuesday. Experience shows people would sooner put up with wrongs they can still bear than fix the problem by throwing out the arrangements they're used to. But when there's a long, unbroken run of abuses and grabs for power, all heading for the same bloody destination, and it plainly shows a plan to grind people down under absolute Despotism, then throwing off that government is their right and their duty. They must put new safeguards in place for their future security.

That's the sort of patient suffering these Colonies have endured. That's why they're now forced to change their old Systems of Government. The present King of Great Britain's record is one injury and one grab for power after another, all aimed straight at setting up absolute Tyranny over these States. To prove it, we'll put the Facts before a fair-minded world.
