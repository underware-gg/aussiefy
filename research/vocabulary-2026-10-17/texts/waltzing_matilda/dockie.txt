Once a cheery old swagman camped beside a billabong,
In the shade of a coolibah tree,
And he sang while he watched, waiting on his billy to boil,
“You'll come a-waltzing Matilda with me.”

Waltzing Matilda, waltzing Matilda,
You'll come a-waltzing Matilda with me,
And he sang while he watched, waiting on his billy to boil,
“You'll come a-waltzing Matilda with me.”

Down came a jumbuck for a drink at that billabong,
Up shot the swagman, pleased as could be,
And he sang as he stuffed that sheep into his tucker bag,
“You'll come a-waltzing Matilda with me.”

Up rode the squatter on his flash thoroughbred,
Down came the troopers, one, two, three,
“Where's that bloody jumbuck you've stashed in your tucker bag?
You'll come a-waltzing Matilda with me.”

Up sprang the swagman and he jumped into the billabong,
“You'll never take me alive!” said he,
And his ghost you can hear as you pass by that billabong,
“You'll come a-waltzing Matilda with me.”
