A cheerful old swagman camped down by a billabong,
Coolibah shade keeping sun off his hide,
Sang while he watched for his bloody billy boiling,
"You'll come a-waltzing Matilda by my side."

Waltzing Matilda, waltzing Matilda,
"You'll come a-waltzing Matilda by my side,"
Sang while he watched for his bloody billy boiling,
"You'll come a-waltzing Matilda by my side."

Down came a jumbuck for a drink at the billabong,
Up shot the swaggie, grabbed the woolly little prize,
Sang as he shoved that sheep into his tucker bag,
"You'll come a-waltzing Matilda by my side."

Up rode the squatter on his flash bloody thoroughbred,
In came the troopers, one, two, three,
"Where's that cheerful bloody jumbuck in your tucker bag?
You'll come a-waltzing Matilda with me."

Up leapt the swagman, straight into the billabong,
"You'll never take me alive!" yelled he,
And you'll still hear his ghost when you pass that old billabong,
"You'll come a-waltzing Matilda with me."
