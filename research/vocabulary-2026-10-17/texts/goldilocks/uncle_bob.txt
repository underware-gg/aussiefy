There was this little girl, Goldilocks, who went for a walk in the woods. She was knackered and starving when she found a small house. Nobody home, so in she wandered, bold as brass. Three bowls of porridge sat on the table. First one, too bloody hot. Second one, too cold. Third one, just right, so she scoffed the lot. Then she sat in the little chair, but it was broken. She was dead on her feet, so up the stairs she went and fell asleep in the smallest bed. Home came the three bears. Father Bear was ropeable. "Someone's been eating my porridge!" he said. Mother Bear was very upset. Baby Bear cried, "Someone's been sleeping in my bed, and she's still there!" Goldilocks woke up, screamed blue murder and took off like a scalded cat. Never came back, either.
