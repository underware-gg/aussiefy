Once there was a little girl called Goldilocks who went for a wander in the woods. She was knackered and hungry enough to eat the arse out of a low-flying duck when she found a small house. Nobody home, so in she went. Three bowls of porridge sat on the table. First one was too bloody hot, second one too cold, third one spot-on, so she polished off the lot. Then she sat in the little chair, but the thing was broken. Absolutely buggered by now, she headed upstairs and fell asleep in the smallest bed. When the three bears came home, Father Bear went off like a firecracker. “Someone's been eating my porridge!” he said. Mother Bear was properly upset. Baby Bear bawled, “Someone's been sleeping in my bed, and she's still bloody there!” Goldilocks woke, screamed, and bolted like she'd put her foot through a wasp nest. She never came back.
