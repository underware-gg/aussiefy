Once there was a little girl called Goldilocks who went for a wander in the woods. Before long she was tired and hungry enough to eat the arse out of a low-flying duck, and she found a small house. Nobody home, so in she went. Three bowls of porridge sat on the table. First one was too hot. Second one was too cold. Third was just the shot, so she polished off the lot. Then she sat in the little chair, but the thing was broken. She was absolutely rooted by then, so upstairs she went and fell asleep in the smallest bed. When the three bears came home, Father Bear was ropable. “Someone's been eating my porridge!” he said. Mother Bear was terribly upset. Baby Bear bawled, “Someone's been sleeping in my bed, and she's still bloody there!” Goldilocks woke, let out a scream and took off like a scalded cat. She never came back.
