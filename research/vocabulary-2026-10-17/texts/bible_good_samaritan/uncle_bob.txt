Righto, this lawyer gets to his feet, reckons he'll put the teacher through his paces. "Teacher," he says, "what do I have to do to inherit eternal life?"

"What's the law say?" he asks him. "How do you read it?"

The lawyer says, "Love the Lord your God with your whole heart, your whole soul, all your strength and every bit of your mind. And love your neighbour as you love yourself."

"You've got it," he says. "Do that and you'll live."

But the lawyer wants to make himself look right, so he asks Jesus, "Yeah, but who counts as my neighbour?"

Jesus says, "A bloke was heading down from Jerusalem to Jericho when some robbing bastards got hold of him. Stripped him, belted the living daylights out of him, and cleared off, leaving him half dead. A priest happened to come down the same road. Saw the poor bugger and crossed to the other side, like decency might get on his shoes. Then a Levite came along, had a look, and did exactly the same bloody thing.

"But a Samaritan travelling through came upon him, and the sight of that beaten bloke tore at him. He went over, poured oil and wine on the wounds and bound them up. Put the bloke on his own animal, took him to an inn and looked after him. Next day, when he had to leave, he pulled out two denarii and handed them to the innkeeper. 'Look after him,' he said. 'If it costs you more than that, I'll square you up when I come back.'

"So which of those three, do you reckon, acted like a neighbour to the bloke the robbers got?"

"The one who showed him mercy," says the lawyer.

And Jesus says, "Well, get out there and do the same."
