A lawyer got to his feet, looking to put him through the wringer. “Teacher, what have I got to do to inherit eternal life?”

He said, “What does the law say? How do you read the bloody thing?”

The lawyer answered, “Love the Lord your God with your whole heart, your whole soul, every bit of strength you've got, and all your mind; and love your neighbour the way you love yourself.”

“Right on the money,” he said. “Do that, and you'll live.”

But the lawyer wanted to make himself look a bit better, so he asked Jesus, “Righto, then who's my neighbour?”

Jesus said, “There was a bloke heading down from Jerusalem to Jericho. A mob of robbers got hold of him, stripped him, belted the living daylights out of him and cleared off, leaving him half dead. As luck would have it, a priest came down that road. He clocked the poor bastard and crossed to the other side. A Levite came along too, saw the state he was in, and gave him the same wide berth. Then a Samaritan travelling that way reached him. One look, and his heart went out to the bloke. He went over, dressed his wounds with oil and wine and bandaged him up. Got him onto his own animal, brought him to an inn and looked after him. Next day, before he headed off, he pulled out two denarii and gave them to the innkeeper. ‘Look after him. If the bill runs over that, I'll square you up when I come back.’ Now, which of those three reckon's been a neighbour to the bloke the robbers knocked about?”

The lawyer said, “The one who showed him mercy.”

Then Jesus said, “Well, get out there and do the same.”
