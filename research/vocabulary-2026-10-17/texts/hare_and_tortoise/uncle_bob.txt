One day the Hare was taking the piss out of the Tortoise's short feet and slow shuffle. She laughed at him. "You might go like the wind, but I'll beat you in a race."

The Hare reckoned she had Buckley's of doing that, so he agreed. They settled on the Fox to pick the course and set the finish. Come race day, they started together. The Tortoise just kept plugging away, never stopped once, slow and steady all the way to the end.

The Hare, cocky as a rooster on a new fence, lay down beside the track and went fast asleep. When he finally woke, he legged it as fast as he could—only to find the Tortoise had already crossed the finish and was having a comfortable doze after all her hard work.

Slow and steady wins the bloody race.
