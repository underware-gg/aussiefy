One day the Hare was taking the piss out of the Tortoise's stumpy feet and crawling pace. She just laughed. “You might go like the clappers, but I'll beat you in a race.” The Hare reckoned she had Buckley's of doing that, so he agreed. They got the Fox to pick the course and mark the finish. On the set day they both took off together. The Tortoise didn't stop for a single bloody moment: she just plugged away, slow and steady, straight to the end. The Hare flopped down beside the track and went to sleep. When he finally woke and tore off as hard as he could, he found the Tortoise already at the finish, comfortably having a kip after her hard yakka. Slow and steady gets the job done.
