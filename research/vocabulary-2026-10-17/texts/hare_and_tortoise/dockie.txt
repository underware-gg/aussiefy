One day the Hare was giving the Tortoise a serve about her little feet and the way she crawled along. She just laughed. “You might go like the wind, mate, but I'll beat you in a race.” The Hare reckoned she had Buckley's chance, so he agreed, and they got the Fox to choose the course and mark the finish.

Come race day, off they went together. The Tortoise never stopped for a breather; she kept plugging away, slow and steady, right to the end of the course. The Hare stretched out beside the track and went dead to the world. When he finally woke, he put the foot down for all he was worth, only to find the Tortoise already over the line, having a comfortable little doze after her hard yakka.

Slow and steady gets the job done.
