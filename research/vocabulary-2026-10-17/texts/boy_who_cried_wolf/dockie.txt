There was this shepherd's boy minding his sheep near a village, and he reckoned it'd be a beaut joke to pull the villagers' legs. Pretend a wolf was getting stuck into the flock, see. So he lets rip: “Wolf! Wolf!” Out come the villagers, running their guts out to help, and the little bugger laughs at them for all their trouble. He pulls that stunt more than once, and every time they're left standing there like spare parts, because there's no bloody wolf.

Then a wolf really does turn up. “Wolf! Wolf!” the boy bellows, giving it everything he's got. But the villagers have heard his carry-on so often they don't take a blind bit of notice. And the wolf has the whole show to himself, killing one sheep after another at his own sweet leisure.

Once a liar's burnt his credit, even the truth won't get him a hearing.
