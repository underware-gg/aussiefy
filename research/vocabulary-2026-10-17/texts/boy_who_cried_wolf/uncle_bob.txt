A shepherd's boy was minding his sheep near a village when he decided it'd be a great bloody laugh to pull the villagers' legs. Pretend a wolf was attacking the flock, that was his bright idea.

"Wolf! Wolf!" he yelled. Out came the people, running to help, and the little bugger laughed at them for their trouble.

He pulled that stunt more than once. Every time they hurried up, every time they'd been had. Not a wolf within cooee.

Then a real wolf turned up.

"Wolf! Wolf!" screamed the boy, loud as he could. But the villagers had heard that bullshit so often they paid him no attention. So the wolf had a free run at it, killing sheep after sheep whenever it pleased.

Once you've made yourself a liar, even the truth comes out smelling crook.
