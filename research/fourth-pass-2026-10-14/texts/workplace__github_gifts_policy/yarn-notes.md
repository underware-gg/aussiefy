## dinkum
- Declined "pressie"/"pressies" for "gift(s)": in an anti-bribery policy "gift" is the regulated term (the heading, "informal gift", "gifts you receive exceeding $150"); a birthday-present word trivialises the obligation. The planner should not swap a policy's key noun in official genre. "mate" for "friend" was right; also used it for "friend's kid".
- RIFF for "dosh": "burnin' a hole in their pocket" is the wrong sense here (money you're itching to spend vs. a bribe in a suitcase); not used.
- Lint suggestions were mostly wrong-sense for this text: "dinner" -> "tea" (a client dinner in a restaurant isn't "tea"), "kid" -> "littlie" (a kid applying to private school), "chocolates" -> "choccy" (a box of chocolates as a corporate gift). Ignored.
- "Swag" (merch) collides with the Australian "swag" (bedroll); kept since it's the company's term, but the engine/lint didn't notice the clash either way.
- Accepted first submit.
