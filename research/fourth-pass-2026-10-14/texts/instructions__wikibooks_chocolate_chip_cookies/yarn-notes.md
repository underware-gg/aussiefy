## troppo

- Accepted on the first submit. OPENINGS were offered ("Strewth, where do I start?") for a recipe that
  starts with an "Ingredients" heading; I didn't use one, since the heading keeps its place. Openings
  shouldn't be offered for an instructions text that opens with a heading.
- Lint false positives: "getting going" (gluten starting to form) -> LEAVE ("clear out", "shoot through");
  "nothing sticks" -> "bugger all" (would change the claim); "don't go mad" (overdo it) -> "go troppo",
  "lose the plot" (go crazy), a different sense.
- Troppo in instructions meant a chattier cook's voice in the prose ("Don't go mad here", "every oven's
  different", "believe it or not", "top-shelf biscuit") with lists, quantities and the hot-tray step exact.
