yarn-troppo: The most natural and varied level-4 retelling; faithful, with colour that hangs off the original.
  minor: 'Picnics and a cook-up on the barbie go together like a pie and sauce.' (for 'Grilling and picnicking often go hand-in-hand.'): A slightly stock simile, though natural enough.

dinkum-3: Natural, well-pitched level-4 guide; small sense stretches and some leaned-on words.
  minor: 'Meat, chook and seafood' (for 'Meat, poultry, and seafood'): "chook" is chicken; poultry also covers turkey, duck and the rest.
  minor: 'bacteria multiply quick smart' (for 'bacteria multiply rapidly'): "quick smart" means promptly, not fast growth; it strains here.
  minor: 'make sure your mitts and the surfaces are clean. Cleaning Your Mitts Outdoors' (for 'make sure hands and surfaces are clean. Outdoor Hand Cleaning'): "mitts" three times in quick succession.

yarn-dinkum: Faithful and natural, slightly light for level 4, with "tucker" overused.
  minor: 'Keep cold tucker cold. Put cold tucker in an esky' (for 'Keep cold food cold. Place cold food in a cooler'): "tucker" swapped in for nearly every "food"; a reader notices the repetition.
  minor: 'Pack and Cart Your Tucker Safely' (for 'Pack and Transport Food Safely'): "Cart" is an odd, un-Australian choice for transport.

unharnessed: Colourful and varied but strains several terms in the opening and setting.
  major: 'all that warm-weather carry-on also gives the nasty bugs in your tucker a real chance to go off' (for 'these warm weather events also present opportunities for foodborne bacteria to thrive'): "carry-on" means fuss; "go off" means spoil or party. Both take the wrong sense.
  minor: 'out in the bush too' (for 'including in outdoor settings'): The bush is not just "outdoors"; it changes the setting.
  minor: 'Using the same platter or tongs' (for 'Using the same platter or utensils'): Narrows utensils to tongs.

engine: A word-swap pass: "tucker" and "esky" dropped into the original, far lighter than level 4.
  major: 'Organize esky contents. Consider packing beverages in one cooler' (for 'Organize cooler contents. Consider packing beverages in one cooler'): US spelling left in, and "esky" and "cooler" mixed in one line.
  major: 'Cook tucker thoroughly.' (for 'Cook food thoroughly.'): "tucker" substituted mechanically throughout an otherwise untranslated text.
