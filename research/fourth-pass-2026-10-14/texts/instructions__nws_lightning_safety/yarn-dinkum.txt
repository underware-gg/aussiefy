There's no safe spot outside when there's thunderstorms about. If ya can hear thunder, you're prob'ly close enough for the storm to hit ya. Just remember, “When Thunder Roars, Go Indoors!” Too many people leave it far too bloody late to get somewhere safe when a storm's rollin' in. Sadly, mucking about like that is behind heaps of the lightnin' deaths and injuries in the U.S.

The best way to keep yerself safe from lightnin' is to steer clear of it altogether. Ya just don't wanna get caught outside in a storm. Have a lightnin' safety plan, and call off or put off whatever you've got on, early, if storms are forecast. Keep an eye on the weather and get somewhere safe before it turns nasty. A proper solid buildin' or a car with a hard roof are safe bets. Rain shelters, little sheds and open-top vehicles are not safe.

Once you're inside, don't touch anythin' plugged into a power point, the plumbin', or phones with a cord. Mobiles and cordless phones are fine. And keep away from outside doors and windows, and don't lie on the garage floor.

Cuttin' Down the Risk

To cut yer own risk of gettin' struck by lightnin', when you're headin' outside, plan ahead so ya can get somewhere safe quick smart if a storm blows in. If the sky's lookin' ugly or ya hear thunder, get inside somewhere safe straight away. Once you're in, keep off phones with a cord, electrical gear, the plumbin', and windows and doors. And last, wait 30 minutes after the last lightnin' or thunder before ya go back outside. If every bloke and sheila stuck to those simple rules, the number of people killed or hurt by lightnin' in this country could drop right down.

Remember, it's what you do with storms in the area that decides yer own risk of gettin' struck by lightnin'. When Thunder Roars, Go Indoors!

If Someone Gets Struck

If someone's been struck by lightnin', they might need medical help straight away. People struck by lightnin' don't carry an electrical charge, so they're safe to touch. Call 911 and keep an eye on 'em. Start CPR or use an Automated External Defibrillator if needed.
