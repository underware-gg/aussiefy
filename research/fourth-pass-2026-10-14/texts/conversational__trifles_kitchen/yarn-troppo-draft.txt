SHERIFF: Nothin' here, mate. Sweet bugger-all but kitchen gear.

(_The_ COUNTY ATTORNEY, _not one to take the Sheriff's word for it, has another good squiz round the kitchen, then opens the door of a cupboard closet. Up he gets on a chair for a stickybeak along a shelf. Pulls his hand back, sticky as_.)

COUNTY ATTORNEY: Well, here's a beaut mess, isn't it?

(_The women edge in closer for a geek_.)

MRS PETERS: (_to the other woman_) Oh, her fruit; it did freeze, (_to the_ LAWYER) She was beside herself about that when it went so cold. Said the fire'd go out and her jars'd crack, every last one of 'em.

SHERIFF: Well, can you beat the women! Up for murder, and she's fussin' over her bloody preserves.

COUNTY ATTORNEY: I'd say before we're done with her she'll have something a whole lot heavier than jam jars to lose sleep over.

HALE: Ah, well, women'll always get their knickers in a twist over trifles.

(_The two women shuffle a little closer together, shoulder to shoulder_.)

COUNTY ATTORNEY: (_laying on the charm like a young pollie after your vote_) And yet, for all their frettin', what would we blokes do without the ladies, ay? (_the women don't crack a smile. Not a flicker. He wanders over to the sink, scoops a dipperful of water out of the pail, tips it into a basin and gives his mitts a wash. Goes to dry them on the roller-towel, then spins it round looking for a bit that's not so bad_) Grotty towels! (_gives the pans under the sink a kick_) Not much chop as a housekeeper, would you say, ladies?

MRS HALE: (_stiff as a board_) There's heaps of work to get through on a farm, Mr Henderson.

COUNTY ATTORNEY: Oh, no doubt, no doubt. And yet (_with a little bow to her, smooth as you like_) I happen to know there's a few Dickson county farmhouses that don't have towels in that state. (_He gives it a yank to show off the full length of it again_.)

MRS HALE: Those towels get filthy in no time. Men's hands aren't always as clean as they might be, are they?

COUNTY ATTORNEY: Ah, sticking up for the sisterhood, I see. But you and Mrs Wright were neighbours. Reckon you were mates as well.

MRS HALE: (_shaking her head_) I've not seen much of her these last few years. I've not set foot in this house--it's more than a year.

COUNTY ATTORNEY: And why's that, then? Didn't like her?

MRS HALE: I liked her well enough. Farmers' wives are flat out, Mr Henderson. And then--

COUNTY ATTORNEY: Yeah--? Go on.

MRS HALE: (_having a Captain Cook round_) It never seemed a very cheerful sort of a place.

COUNTY ATTORNEY: No--it's not cheerful, is it? Can't say she had much of a knack for homemaking.

MRS HALE: Well, I dunno as Wright had, either.

COUNTY ATTORNEY: You mean the two of 'em didn't get on too flamin' well?

MRS HALE: Yeah, but nah, I don't mean anything by it. But I don't reckon any place'd be cheerier for having John Wright in it.

COUNTY ATTORNEY: I'd like to pick that up with you a bit later. Right now I want to get the lay of the land upstairs. (_Off he goes to the left, where three steps lead up to a stair door_.)

SHERIFF: S'pose whatever Mrs Peters does'll be right, ay. She was to take in some clothes for her, you know, and a few little odds and ends. We cleared out of here in such a rush yesterday.

COUNTY ATTORNEY: Yeah, but I'd like a look at what you take, Mrs Peters, and keep your eyes peeled for anything that might come in handy for us.

MRS PETERS: Yes, Mr Henderson.

(_The women listen to the blokes clomping up the stairs, then have a look about the kitchen_.)

MRS HALE: I'd hate to have men barging into my kitchen, stickybeaking round and picking holes in everything.

(_She puts back the pans under the sink that the_ LAWYER _had kicked all over the shop_.)

MRS PETERS: Course, they're only doing their duty.

MRS HALE: Duty's all well and good, but I'd bet that deputy sheriff that came out to get the fire going might've put a bit of this on, too. (_gives the roller towel a tug_) Wish I'd thought of that sooner. Seems a bit low to rubbish her for not having the place spick and span when she had to up and leave in such a hurry.
