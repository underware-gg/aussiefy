## dinkum

- Wrong-sense planned phrases, both declined: "fronted up" for "arrived" (its gloss is "present yourself, often
  to face something"; migrant families arriving in a country aren't fronting up); "The Big Smoke" for "The city"
  (the city seen from the bush; here "the city" is just an anaphor for Nashville). The Big Smoke riff ("coffee at
  six bucks a pop") would add content to a news report.
- Lookups: "a big rap" found as "give {obj} a big wrap" (the spelling "wrap" is the usual one; good);
  "pull its weight" and "got it up" (to pass a motion, council/political sense) came back empty or wrong
  ("stony broke" for "got it up").
- Lint suggestion "the joint" for "The place" (the city) is too dismissive for news about a community.
- Accepted on the first submit.
