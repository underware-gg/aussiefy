  minor: 'The sentiment behind it is perfectly beaut' (for 'The sentiment in this attitude is perfectly bully'): 'Perfectly beaut' is not how 'beaut' is used; a forced pairing.

dinkum-3: Natural, light and faithful; loses a little of the original's arch formality.
  minor: 'dead careful, almost sneaky about it' (for 'very cautiously, almost clandestinely'): Loses the comic formality of 'clandestinely'.
  minor: 'Never touch a sonnet before brekkie.' (for 'Never touch a sonnet before breakfast.'): Repeats 'brekkie'; the arch maxim reads better plain.

unharnessed: Faithful and natural, a little light, with one forced word.
  minor: 'The feeling behind it is perfectly bonzer' (for 'The sentiment in this attitude is perfectly bully'): 'Perfectly bonzer' is a forced pairing; an Australian would not say it.
  minor: 'Scratch the bath as too noisy.' (for 'Eliminate the tub as too noisy.'): Keeps the original's 'as too noisy' frame, which jars with 'Scratch'.

yarn-troppo: Lively and varied, but overdone for the brief, with stock similes and an invented frame.
  major: "Back in the day, a weekend at someone's place came with its own little traps, and getting up in the morning was the first of 'em." (for 'Take, for example, the matter of arising in the morning.'): Invents a framing that recasts the essay as reminiscence.
  minor: 'head cocked to one side like a magpie listening for a worm' (for 'with head cocked on one side'): Bolted-on simile.
  minor: 'careful as a cat' (for 'very cautiously'): Stock simile.
  minor: 'Lovely sentiment, beaut of a sentiment' (for 'The sentiment in this attitude is perfectly bully'): Stacked repetition; 'beaut of a sentiment' is forced.
  major: "Mate, that's when you're in for a frightful mix-up." (for 'But if you are the only guest there is apt to be a frightful misunderstanding.'): 'Mate' and the matey address push the register well past level 2 and change the tone.

  major: 'The sentiment in this attitude is perfectly bully' (for 'The sentiment in this attitude is perfectly bully'): US 'bully' left in; the text is largely untranslated, far under the brief.
