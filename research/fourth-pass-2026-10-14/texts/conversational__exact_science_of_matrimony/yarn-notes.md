## dinkum

- Accepted on the first submit; no planned phrase declined. "staying back" for "working overtime" is a fair
  fit here; "heaps" for "a lot of watered stock" works only because the "lot" reads as quantity (a "lot" of
  shares as a parcel would be the wrong sense).
- The brief didn't warn that the original's "graft"/"grafting" (US: swindling) is a false friend: Australian
  "grafting" is honest hard work. I rendered it as "lurk" and "on the take". Lookup "lurk" came back empty
  though it's everyday Australian (a lurk, lurks and perks); worth a dictionary entry.
- Lookup "drunk" returned "a mongrel bastard" ("a thoroughly rotten person"): wrong sense, matched on "rotten".
- Lint false positives: name_dropped "Twas" (an archaic contraction, not a name); suggestion "got going" ->
  LEAVE ("clear out", "shoot through") when it meant "started a scheme"; "dollar" -> "quid" would change the
  money fact in a dollar-denominated story.
- Lint's "reckon" 3+ warning was useful: the planned "reckoned" plus my own hedges added up fast.
