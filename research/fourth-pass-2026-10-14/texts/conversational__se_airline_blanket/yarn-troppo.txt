Accidentally nicked a blanket off a plane; had no clue you weren't allowed. What do I do, ay?

Look, I'm a total newbie at this travel caper. I was on a long flight, the long-haul kind where your bum goes numb, and the airline handed round blankets and pillows. Me, not having the foggiest I was meant to leave the blanket behind, bundled it up with the rest of my gear and off I went. I'd figured it was a cheapish sort of blanket, so it must've come with the ticket or something. I wasn't hanging out to keep it, fair dinkum, but I was half asleep and cold as, and I just scooped it up with my other stuff. Not one bugger said a word to me while I did it.

It wasn't till I got home and unpacked that the blanket turned up again, and there's this tiny little tag on it: 'do not remove from plane.' Well, didn't I feel like a proper crook. Pinched a blanket by accident! I'll never do it again, obviously, but I still feel dead guilty about doing it this once. Is there some way I can get it back to the airline? Or do I just let it go, lesson learned, and stop carrying on?

Sorry if this is a dopey question, I just had a bit of a panic when I clocked I'd knocked it off by accident!!

Just so you know, the airline was Aer Lingus.

* * *

If you want to stop that guilt hanging round, give the airline a bell and ask them what they'd like you to do with the blanket. Tell them straight you took it by mistake cos you were still a bit off with the fairies when you got off the plane.

Odds are they'll tell you to hang onto it, cos it'd cost them more to get it back in the post and chuck it back in the blanket pool than to just buy a new blanket. But once they've told you flat out to keep it, you've got nothing to worry about. Sweet as. And if (less likely) they do want it sent back, well, you send it back, and that's your conscience squared away too.

You can find out how to get hold of them at https://www.aerlingus.com/support/contact-us.
