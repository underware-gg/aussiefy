Nicked an airline blanket off the plane by accident; didn't realise you weren't allowed. What do I do?

I'm pretty green when it comes to travelling. On a long-haul flight the airline handed out blankets and pillows. I had no idea I was meant to leave the blanket behind, so I scooped it up with the rest of my gear and hopped off. I'd figured that since it was a fairly cheap blanket, it was part of the ticket price or something. I wasn't desperate to keep it, but I was half asleep and bloody cold and just grabbed it with my other stuff. No bugger said a word to me while I did it.

It wasn't till I got home that I found the blanket again and spotted a tiny tag that said 'do not remove from plane.' Course, I felt rotten about pinching it by accident. I'll never do it again, obviously, but I still feel dead guilty about doing it this time. Is there a way I can get it back to the airline? Or should I just forget about it, lesson learned?

Sorry if this is a silly question, I just sort of panicked when I realised I'd knocked it off by accident!!

Just so you know, the airline was Aer Lingus.

* * *

So you're not left feeling guilty, give the airline a ring and ask them what they'd like you to do with the blanket. Tell them you took it by mistake cos you weren't fully with it when you got off the plane.

Odds are they'll tell you to keep it, cos it'd cost them more to get it back in the post and put it back in the blanket pool than to just buy a new one. But if they've told you straight out to keep it, you've got nothing to worry about. And if (less likely) they do ask you to send it back, you can send it back, and that'll clear your conscience too.

You can find out how to get in touch with them at https://www.aerlingus.com/support/contact-us.
