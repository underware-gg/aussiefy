_Terre Haute, Indiana, September 6._

FRIEND AL: Well, Al old mate, I s'pose you seen in the paper how I been sold to the White Sox. Fair dinkum Al it come as a surprise to me and I bet it did to all you old mates back home too. You could of knocked me over with a feather when the old man come up to me and goes Jack I've sold you to the Chicago Americans.

I had no idea nothing like that was on the cards. For five minutes I was just struck dum and couldn't get a word out.

He goes We aren't getting what you're worth but I want you to get up to that big league and show them fellas there's a Central League on the map. He goes Just pitch the ball you been pitching down here and it'll be easy as. He goes All you need is a bit of nerve and Walsh or no one else won't have sweet bugger-all on you.

So I says I'd do the best I could and I thanked him for how they treated me in Terre Haute. They always looked after me here and though I did more than my share I always felt my work was appresiated. We're finishing second and I done most of it. I can't help being proud of my first year in professional baseball and you know I'm not skiting when I say that Al.

Well Al it'll feel a bit funny being up there in the big show when I never been in a proper big city before. But I reckon I seen enough of life not to get spooked by a few tall buildings, ay Al?

I'll just give them what I got and if they don't like it they can send me back to the old Central and I'll be happy as Larry.

I didn't know anyone was having a squiz at me, but one of the boys told me Jack Doyle the White Sox scout was down here watching me when Grand Rapids was in town. I beat them twice in that serious. You know Grand Rapids never had a hope against me when I was on song. I shut them out in the first game and they only got one run in the second cos Flynn misjuged that fly ball. Anyway Doyle liked what he seen and he wired Comiskey to buy me. Comiskey come back with an offer and they excepted it. I dunno how much they got for me but anyway I'm sold to the big league and believe me Al I'm gonna make good.

Well Al I'll be home in a few days and we'll have some of them beaut old times. Say g'day to all the boys and tell them I'm still their mate and not getting up myself over this big league business.

Your mate, JACK.
