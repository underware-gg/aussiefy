_Terre Haute, Indiana, September 6._

FRIEND AL: Well, Al old mate I s'pose you seen in the paper where I been sold to the White Sox. Fair dinkum Al it come as a surprise to me and I bet it did to all you good old mates down home too. You could of knocked me over with a feather when the old bloke come up to me and goes Jack I've sold you to the Chicago Americans.

I had no idea nothing like that was on the cards. For five minutes I was just dum and couldn't get a word out.

He says We aren't getting what you're worth but I want you to go up to that big league and show them blokes there's a Central League on the map. He says Go and pitch the ball you been pitching down here and there's bugger all to it. He says All you need is the nerve and Walsh or nobody else won't have a thing on you.

So I says I'd do me best and I thanked him for how they done right by me in Terre Haute. They always was good to me here and though I pulled more than my weight I always felt my work was appresiated. We're finishing second and I done most of it. I can't help being proud of my first year's record in professional baseball and you know I'm not skiting when I say that Al.

Well Al it'll be a funny thing being up there in the big show when I never been in a proper big city before. But I reckon I seen enough of life not to be spooked by the tall buildings eh Al?

I'll just give them what I got and if they don't like it they can send me back to the old Central and I'll be happy as Larry.

I didn't know nobody was having a squiz at me, but one of the boys told me Jack Doyle the White Sox scout was down here giving me the once-over when Grand Rapids was in town. I beat them twice in that serious. You know Grand Rapids never had a hope against me when I was on song. I shut them out in the first game and they got one run in the second cos Flynn misjuged that fly ball. Anyway Doyle liked what he seen and he wired Comiskey to buy me. Comiskey come back with an offer and they excepted it. I dunno how much they got but anyway I'm sold to the big league and mark my words Al I'll make good.

Well Al I'll be home in a few days and we'll have some of the good old times. Regards to all the boys and tell them I'm still their mate and not all up meself over this big league business.

Your mate, JACK.
