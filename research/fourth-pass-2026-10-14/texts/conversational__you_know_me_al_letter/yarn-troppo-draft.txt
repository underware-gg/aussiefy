_Terre Haute, Indiana, September 6._

FRIEND AL: Well, Al me old mate I s'pose you seen in the paper where I been sold to the White Sox. Strike me pink Al it come as a real shock to me and I bet it did to all you good old mates down home and all. You could of knocked me over with a feather when the old bloke wanders up to me and goes Jack I've sold you to the Chicago Americans. Just like that.

I had no idea nothing like that was on the cards. For five minutes there I was just dum, stood there like a stunned mullet and couldn't get a word out.

He goes We aren't getting what you're worth but I want you to go up to that big league and show them flash blokes there's a Central League on the map. He goes Go and pitch the ball you been pitching down here and there's bugger all to it. He goes All you need is the nerve and Walsh or no one else won't have a thing on you. Not a thing.

So I says I'd give it me best shot and I thanked him for the fair go I got in Terre Haute. They always been good to me here and though I carried more than my share of the load I always felt my work was appresiated. We're finishing second and I done most of the hard yakka. I can't help being proud as punch of my first year's record in professional baseball and you know I'm not skiting when I say that Al. It's just the facts.

Well Al it'll be a funny old thing being up there in the big show when I never been in a proper big city before. But I reckon I seen enough of life not to be spooked by a few tall buildings eh Al? They're only bricks.

I'll just give them what I got and if they don't like it they can send me back to the old Central and I'll be happy as Larry, no worries.

I never knew anybody was running the ruler over me, but one of the boys tells me Jack Doyle the White Sox scout was down here having a good squiz at me when Grand Rapids was in town. I beat them twice in that serious. You know Grand Rapids never had Buckley's against me when I was on song. I shut them out in the first game and they only got one run in the second cos Flynn went and misjuged that fly ball. Wasn't my fault, that one. Anyway Doyle liked what he seen and he wired Comiskey to buy me. Comiskey come back with an offer and they excepted it. I dunno how much they got for me but anyway I'm sold to the big league and mark my words Al I'll make good.

Well Al I'll be home in a few days and we'll have some of the good old times. Regards to all the boys and tell them I'm still their mate and not all up meself over this big league business. Same old Jack.

Your mate, JACK.
