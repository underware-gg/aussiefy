## Preserve breadth and label evidence honestly

- **Sickie:** edited definitions allow genuine illness as well as a false claim. Do not make deception
  compulsory for the bare noun.
- **Oldies and singlet:** preserve parents/older people and outerwear/underwear readings respectively.
- **Muso, mob and pommy:** an alternate derogatory, angry-crowd or broader British reading does not erase
  the selected Australian use. Tone and existing gates remain separate from basic meaning evidence.
- **Drop bear:** the museum's mock species profile is a folklore joke, corroborated by its own explanation
  of the joke. Do not treat invented classification and measurements as zoological facts.
- **Red cordial:** primary quoted speech supports the complete high-energy metaphor, not a causal claim
  about sugar or a requirement that the referent be a child.
- **Clipped and composed forms:** sources for `sure as eggs is eggs`, affirmation `dead set`, `vego`,
  `no dramas` or component nouns do not automatically certify every shortened form, spelling or sentence
  type. The ledger distinguishes those limits.
- **Evidence quality varies:** Macquarie's community Word Map is labelled as community evidence; bilingual
  and collaborative dictionary support is not presented as an independently inspected historical primary
  citation. Green's references discovered through other pages were not read directly and are not counted
  as direct confirmation. ABC programme descriptions are not claimed to be complete audio transcripts.

Two selected uses remain provisional: **`the-go`** in the best-plan sense and the exact
**`take-longer-than-a-wet-week`** construction. Searches found other `go` senses and an informal wet-week
discussion, but not sufficiently clear edited/primary confirmation of those exact uses. Both remain
preserved; a bounded failed lookup is not evidence of fabrication.
