"Right, so you wanna hear about the worst night of my life? Righto. Pull up a stool.

It was last winter, and it was blowing a dog off its chain, the kind of wind that bends the trees. I'd been round at my cousin's place, and I'll admit I was a bit pissed. Not falling over, but, you know, happy.

So I'm walking home, and next door's mutt gets out. Big black mongrel of a thing, loud as, making a hell of a racket, barking at everything. Chases me down the street. I was packing death, honestly. I hopped a fence into someone's yard and landed smack in their fish pond. Absolutely saturated. Like a drowned rat, head to toe.

Then the bloke opens the back door with a torch, and I'm standing there dripping, holding a garden gnome I'd grabbed on the way down. I was red as a beetroot. Told him I was looking for my cat. I don't even have a cat. Worst bullshitter you've ever met. Couldn't tell a porky to save my life.

Anyway, he was a bit off about it, sort of quiet and weird as, and he just goes, 'Climbing fences at night's a bit dodgy, you know.' Then he shut the door. Bit stiff, wasn't I? That's pretty much the whole story."
