Monday, 3 February

First day at the new job. I was that toey on the train I nearly got off at the wrong station. The office is massive, and my desk is a pokey little thing, jammed in next to the photocopier. Everyone seemed friendly enough, though one bloke in accounts was a bit off. He stared at me the whole meeting and never said boo.

Wednesday, 5 February

Absolute shocker of a day. The computer system carked it twice and I lost a whole morning's work. By six o'clock I was completely stuffed. Came home, ate toast standing at the bench, and hit the sack.

Thursday, 6 February

Stressing out all morning about the presentation. My hands were shaking. But it went really well! My manager said it was the clearest summary she'd seen all year. I was stoked. Rang Mum from the car park.

Friday, 7 February

Bit of a hiccup tonight. Daniel and I had a bit of a blue about whose turn it was to cook. Silly blue, really. We ended up getting pizza and cracking up about it. One week down. Reckon I'm gonna like it there.
