One day a Hare was paying out on the Tortoise for her stumpy little legs and for being slow as a wet week. The Tortoise just laughed and said, "You might be quick as the wind, mate, but I'll still flog you in a race." The Hare reckoned that was never gonna happen, but said "You're on"; and they shook on it that the Fox would pick the course and set the finish line. On the day they'd settled on for the race, the two took off together. The Tortoise never stopped for a sec, but just kept plugging away at her slow, steady plod, straight to the end of the course. The Hare had a lie-down by the side of the track and nodded off. When he finally woke up and took off flat chat, he saw the Tortoise had already made it to the finish line and was having a nanna nap, nice and comfy, after wearing herself out. Slow and steady wins the race.
