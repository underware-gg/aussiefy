RIVERSIDE BREAKS 30-YEAR DROUGHT FOR FIRST FLAG

Riverside Football Club supporters were over the moon on Saturday arvo after the side snuck home by two points in the regional grand final.

More than four thousand people packed the ground for the match. Conditions were tough going, with heavy rain in the first half leaving the deck sodden and greasy, but it cleared up later in the afternoon.

Riverside trailed for most of the day. The match-winner came in the dying minute from eighteen-year-old Liam Carter, who said he was "too nervous to look" as the ball sailed through.

Not everyone was happy. Several visiting fans were spitting chips over a late umpiring call and had to be asked to leave the stand. Police said no arrests were made.

Club president Maria Vella said the community had been hanging out for this day for a long time. "The players are stuffed, but they'll be celebrating tonight," she said. "Thanks to the volunteers, the mums and dads and the families who cook, wash the jumpers and drive the team to every game."

Celebrations were expected to kick on late into the night at the clubrooms. The side will be back on the track for training in February.
