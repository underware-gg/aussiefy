Once upon a time, there was a little tacker called Goldilocks. One morning she went for a wander through the bush. She walked for ages, and pretty soon she was buggered and starving.

After a while she came across a little place in the middle of the bush. She knocked on the door, but nobody came. So she let herself in.

On the kitchen table there were three bowls of porridge. Goldilocks was starving, so she reckoned she'd have a crack.

She had a taste of the porridge in the first bowl. "Far out, this porridge is way too hot!" she said.

She had a taste of the porridge in the second bowl. "This porridge is cold as!" she said.

Then she had a taste of the porridge in the third bowl. "This porridge is spot on!" she said with a big grin, and she scoffed the lot.

Once she'd had her feed, she wanted a sit-down. In the lounge room there were three chairs. She plonked herself in the first chair. "This chair's way too big!" she said. She plonked herself in the second chair. "This chair's hard as!" she said. Then she plonked herself in the third chair. "This chair's spot on!" she said. But as soon as she got comfy, the chair carked it and fell to bits, and she landed on the floor.

By now Goldilocks was absolutely buggered, so she headed upstairs to the bedroom. There were three beds. She lay down on the first bed, but it was too hard. She lay down on the second bed, but it was too soft. Then she lay down on the third bed, and it was spot on. She pulled up the doona and was out like a light.

Before long the three bears got home from their walk.

"Someone's been into my porridge!" said Dad Bear in his great big booming voice.

"Someone's been into my porridge!" said Mum Bear.

"Someone's been into my porridge, and they've scoffed the lot!" sang out Baby Bear.

The bears went into the lounge room.

"Someone's been sitting in my chair!" said Dad Bear, cranky as.

"Someone's been sitting in my chair!" said Mum Bear.

"Someone's been sitting in my chair, and they've busted it to bits!" wailed Baby Bear.

The bears were filthy now. They went upstairs to the bedroom.

"Someone's been kipping in my bed!" said Dad Bear.

"Someone's been kipping in my bed!" said Mum Bear.

"Someone's been kipping in my bed, and she's still in it!" squealed Baby Bear.

Just then Goldilocks woke up. She saw the three bears standing over her and she was scared stiff. "Help!" she screamed. She shot out of bed, legged it down the stairs and bolted out of the house as fast as her legs would carry her. She didn't even stop to chuck her shoes on.

Goldilocks never went back to that house in the bush again.
