Our Week Up the Coast

We drove up on the Saturday morning and got there nice and early, before the traffic. The beach shack we'd rented was small and old, but it was only a short stroll to the beach, and there was an outdoor shower for hosing off the sand.

The first three days were absolute crackers, warm and sunny with clear blue skies. We swam every morning and spent the arvos reading under a beach umbrella. In the evenings we'd wander along the water's edge, taking our time, as the sun went down, and most nights we had tea at a seafood place just down the road.

On the Wednesday the weather turned. It started blowing a gale, and the surf got huge and pretty hairy. The lifesavers shut the beach, and the red flags stayed up for two days. Bit of rough luck, I s'pose. We spent Thursday playing cards and watching the rain belt against the windows. The kids were bored stiff by lunchtime.

By Friday the wind had dropped off. We went down late in the arvo and had the beach pretty much to ourselves, the sand still soggy from the storm. It was a magic evening, the pick of the whole trip. Nobody wanted to go home.
