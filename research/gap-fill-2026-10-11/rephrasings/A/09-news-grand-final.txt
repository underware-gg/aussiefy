LOCAL CLUB BREAKS THIRTY-YEAR PREMIERSHIP DROUGHT

Riverside Football Club supporters were rapt on Saturday arvo after the side won the regional grand final by two points.

The match was played in front of a crowd of more than four thousand. Conditions were tough going, with heavy rain in the first half leaving the ground boggy and greasy, but the weather cleared later in the afternoon.

Riverside trailed for most of the game. The match-winner came in the dying minute from eighteen-year-old Liam Carter, who said he was "too nervous to look" as the ball sailed through.

Not everyone was happy. Several visiting fans were spitting chips over a late umpiring call and had to be asked to leave the stand. Police said no arrests were made.

Club president Maria Vella said the community had been hanging out for this day for a long time. "Our players are cooked, but they'll celebrate tonight," she said. "Thanks to the volunteers, the mums and dads and the families who cook, wash the jumpers and drive the team to every game."

Celebrations were expected to kick on late into the night at the clubrooms. The side will be back for training in February.
