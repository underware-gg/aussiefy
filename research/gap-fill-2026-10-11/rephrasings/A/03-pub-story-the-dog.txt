"Right, so you wanna hear about the worst night of my life? Righto. Sit down.

It was last winter, and it was blowing a dog off its chain, the kind of wind that bends the trees right over. I'd been round at my cousin's, and I'll admit I was a bit full. Not falling over, but, you know, happy.

So I'm walking home, and next door's dog gets out. Big black mongrel of a thing, loud as, barking its head off at everything. Chases me down the street. I was packing it, honestly. I hopped a fence into someone's yard and landed straight in their fish pond. Absolutely saturated. Sopping wet from head to toe.

Then the owner opens the back door with a torch, and I'm standing there dripping, holding a garden gnome I'd grabbed on the way down. I was so embarrassed, felt like a right galah. Told him I was looking for my cat. I don't even have a cat. Shocking liar, me. Couldn't tell a porky to save my life.

Anyway, he was a bit funny about it, sort of quiet and a bit weird, and he just goes, 'Climbing fences at night's dangerous, you know.' Then he shut the door. Bit stiff, eh? That's pretty much the whole story."
