"So, how was the do on Saturday? Heard it went till two."

"Oh, it was good. Pretty much the whole street rocked up. Trev did the barbie, and his missus made about six salads."

"Trev's a generous bugger like that, eh? Gave half the street a lift to the airport last Christmas."

"He is. Mind you, his brother turned up, and he's a bit of a pain. Told the same joke four times. Then he stood by the fence having a durry and banging on about what his new boat cost."

"Was he big-noting himself again?"

"Yeah, sort of. Apparently he made an absolute motza selling his business. And he's a bit of a spunk, so all the neighbours were checking him out. I reckon Trev's a bit jealous, if I'm honest."

"Well, I'd be jealous as too. A boat, but? Bloke gets seasick down the beach."

"I know! Anyway, don't repeat any of that. Sandra's a shocking gossip, and if she hears I said it, the whole suburb'll know by Tuesday."

"Nah, nah, I won't. We shouldn't be spreading goss anyway, should we? Chuck us the tongs, will ya? These snags are burning."
