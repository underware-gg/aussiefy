One day a Hare was having a go at the Tortoise for her short little legs and for being slow as a wet week. The Tortoise just laughed and said, "You might be as quick as the wind, mate, but I'll still flog you in a race." The Hare reckoned that was flat-out impossible, but said, "You're on." They shook on it that the Fox would pick the course and set the finish line. On race day the two of them took off together. The Tortoise never stopped for a sec, just kept plodding along, slow and steady, straight to the end of the course. The Hare plonked himself down by the side of the track and was soon out to it. When he finally woke up and went for it as fast as he could, he saw the Tortoise had already made it to the finish line and was having a nana nap, nice and comfy, after all her hard yakka. Slow and steady wins the race.
