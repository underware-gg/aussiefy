Monday, 3 February

First day at the new job. I was a bundle of nerves on the train and nearly got off at the wrong station. The office is massive, and my desk is tiny, jammed in next to the photocopier. Everyone seemed pretty friendly, though one bloke in accounts was a bit of a strange one. He stared at me the whole meeting and never said boo.

Wednesday, 5 February

Shocker of a day. The computer system crashed twice and I lost a whole morning's work. By six I was absolutely buggered. Came home, ate toast standing up, and went straight to bed.

Thursday, 6 February

Had the jitters all morning about the presentation. My hands were shaking. But it went really well! My manager said it was the clearest summary she'd seen all year. I was stoked. Rang Mum from the car park.

Friday, 7 February

Bit of a hiccup tonight. Daniel and I got into a blue about whose turn it was to cook. It was a silly blue, really. We ended up getting pizza and having a laugh about it. One week down. Reckon I'm going to like it there.
