Our Week on the Coast

We drove up on the Saturday morning and got there bright and early, before the traffic. The shack we'd rented was small and a bit run-down, but it was only a short walk from the beach, and there was an outdoor shower for hosing off the sand.

The first three days were absolute beauts, warm with clear blue skies. We swam every morning and spent the arvos reading under a beach umbrella. Of an evening we'd go for a slow wander along the water as the sun went down, and most nights we had tea at a fish restaurant down the road.

On the Wednesday the weather turned. It got really blowy, and the surf got huge and pretty hairy. The lifesavers closed the beach, and the red flags stayed up for two days. We copped a bit of rotten luck, I s'pose. We spent Thursday playing cards and watching the rain against the windows. The kids were over it by lunchtime.

By Friday the wind had dropped off. We headed down late in the arvo and found the beach just about empty, the sand still wet from the storm. It was an absolute ripper of an evening, the best of the whole trip. Nobody wanted to go home.
