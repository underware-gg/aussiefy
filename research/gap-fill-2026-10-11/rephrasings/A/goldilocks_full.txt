Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Goldilocks. One morning she went for a walk in the bush. She walked for ages, and before long she was pretty tired and absolutely starving.

After a while she came across a little house smack bang in the middle of the bush. She knocked on the door, but no-one came. So she opened the door and let herself in.

On the kitchen table there were three bowls of porridge. Goldilocks was starving, so she reckoned she'd have a go at some.

She had a taste of the porridge in the first bowl. "This porridge is way too hot!" she said.

She had a taste of the porridge in the second bowl. "This porridge is way too cold!" she said.

Then she had a taste of the porridge in the third bowl. "This porridge is spot on!" she said, stoked, and she wolfed the lot.

After her feed, she wanted a sit-down. In the lounge room there were three chairs. She plonked herself in the first chair. "This chair's too big!" she said. She sat in the second chair. "This chair's too hard!" she said. Then she sat in the third chair. "This chair's just right!" she said. But as soon as she got comfy, the chair broke into bits, and she came a cropper on the floor.

By now Goldilocks was stuffed, so she headed upstairs to the bedroom. There were three beds. She lay down on the first bed, but it was too hard. She lay down on the second bed, but it was too soft. Then she lay down on the third bed, and it was just right. She pulled the doona up and went out like a light.

Before long the three bears came home from their walk.

"Someone's been eating my porridge!" said Father Bear in his great big booming voice.

"Someone's been eating my porridge!" said Mother Bear.

"Someone's been eating my porridge, and they've scoffed the lot!" sang out Baby Bear.

The bears went into the lounge room.

"Someone's been sitting in my chair!" said Father Bear, ropeable.

"Someone's been sitting in my chair!" said Mother Bear.

"Someone's been sitting in my chair, and they've busted it to bits!" sang out Baby Bear.

The bears were filthy now. They went upstairs to the bedroom.

"Someone's been sleeping in my bed!" said Father Bear.

"Someone's been sleeping in my bed!" said Mother Bear.

"Someone's been sleeping in my bed, and she's still here!" sang out Baby Bear.

Just then Goldilocks woke up. She saw the three bears standing over her and she was scared stiff. "Help!" she screamed. She shot out of bed, bolted down the stairs, and was out of that house like a shot. She didn't even stop to put her shoes on.

Goldilocks never went back to that house in the bush again.
