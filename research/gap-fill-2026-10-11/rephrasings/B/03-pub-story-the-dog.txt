"Righto, so ya wanna hear about the worst night of me life? Orright. Pull up a stool.

It was last winter, and it was blowin' a gale, the kinda wind that bends the trees over. I'd been round me cousin's place, and I'll admit I was a bit full. Not legless, but, ya know, merry.

So I'm walkin' home, and next door's mongrel gets out. Big black thing, loud as, makin' a hell of a racket, barkin' its bloody head off at everythin'. Chases me down the street. I was shittin' meself, fair dinkum. I hopped a fence into some bloke's yard and landed smack in his fishpond. Like a drowned rat. Wringin' wet from head to toe.

Then the owner opens the back door with a torch, and I'm standin' there drippin', holdin' a garden gnome I'd grabbed on the way down. Felt like a right dill, I tell ya. Told him I was lookin' for me cat. I don't even have a cat. Shockin' bullshit artist, me. Couldn't tell a porky to save me life.

Anyway, he was a bit funny about it, sorta quiet and a bit off, and he just goes, 'Climbin' fences at night's a bit dicey, ya know.' Then he shut the door. Bloody stiff, wasn't I? And that's about the size of it."
