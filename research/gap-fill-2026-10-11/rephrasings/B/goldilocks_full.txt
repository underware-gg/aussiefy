Once upon a time, there was a little sheila called Goldilocks. One mornin' she went for a walk in the bush. She walked for ages, and before long she was buggered and so hungry she could've eaten a horse and chased the jockey.

After a bit she come across a little shack smack in the middle of the bush. She knocked on the door, but nobody sang out. So she opened the door and let herself in.

On the kitchen table there were three bowls of porridge. Goldilocks was starvin', so she reckoned she'd have a crack at it.

She tasted the porridge in the first bowl. "Strewth, this porridge is bloody scorchin'!" she said.

She tasted the porridge in the second bowl. "This porridge is stone cold!" she said.

Then she tasted the porridge in the third bowl. "This porridge is sweet as!" she said, happy as Larry, and she wolfed the lot.

Once she'd had her feed, she wanted to take a load off. In the lounge room there were three chairs. She sat in the first chair. "This chair's too bloody big!" she said. She sat in the second chair. "This one's hard as a rock!" she said. Then she sat in the third chair. "This one's sweet as!" she said. But soon as she got comfy, the chair carked it and fell to bits, and she landed on her arse on the floor.

By now Goldilocks was dead on her feet, so she headed upstairs to the bedroom. There were three beds. She lay down on the first bed, but it was hard as nails. She lay down on the second bed, but it was way too soft. Then she lay down on the third bed, and it was just the ticket. She pulled up the doona and went out like a light.

Before long the three bears come home from their walk.

"Someone's been into me porridge!" said Father Bear in his great big voice.

"Someone's been into me porridge!" said Mother Bear.

"Someone's been into me porridge, and they've scoffed the lot!" howled Baby Bear.

The bears went into the lounge room.

"Someone's been sittin' in me chair!" said Father Bear, spewin'.

"Someone's been sittin' in me chair!" said Mother Bear.

"Someone's been sittin' in me chair, and they've busted it to bits!" howled Baby Bear.

The bears were ropeable now. They went upstairs to the bedroom.

"Someone's been sleepin' in me bed!" said Father Bear.

"Someone's been sleepin' in me bed!" said Mother Bear.

"Someone's been sleepin' in me bed, and she's still bloody here!" howled Baby Bear.

Just then Goldilocks woke up. She saw the three bears standin' over her and she was scared shitless. "Help!" she screamed. She was outta that bed like a shot, pelted down the stairs, and shot out of the shack like a rat up a drainpipe. Didn't even stop to chuck her shoes on.

Goldilocks never set foot in that shack in the bush again.
