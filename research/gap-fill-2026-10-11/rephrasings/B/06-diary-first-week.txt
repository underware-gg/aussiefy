Monday, 3 February

First day at the new job. I was that toey on the train I nearly got off at the wrong station. The office is massive, and me desk is the size of a postage stamp, jammed in next to the photocopier. Everyone seemed a good bunch, though one bloke in accounts was a bit of a weirdo. Stared at me the whole meetin' and never said boo.

Wednesday, 5 February

Absolute bastard of a day. The computer system carked it twice and I lost a whole mornin's work. By six I was completely stuffed. Came home, ate toast standin' up, and hit the sack.

Thursday, 6 February

Me guts were in knots all mornin' over the presentation. Hands shakin' like a leaf. But it went sweet as! Boss reckoned it was the clearest summary she'd seen all year. Stoked, I was. Rang Mum from the car park.

Friday, 7 February

Bit of a hiccup tonight. Daniel and me got into a blue about whose turn it was to cook. Stupid little blue, really. Ended up gettin' pizza and havin' a laugh about it. One week down. Reckon I'm gonna like it there.
