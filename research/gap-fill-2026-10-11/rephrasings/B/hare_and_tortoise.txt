One day a Hare was rubbishin' the Tortoise for her stumpy little legs and the way she plodded along, slower than a wet week. The Tortoise just laughed and came back with: "You might be quick as greased lightnin', mate, but I'll still flog ya in a race." The Hare reckoned she was dreamin', but said righto, you're on; and they shook on it that the Fox would pick the track and set the finish line. On the day they'd locked in for the race, the two of 'em took off together. The Tortoise never stopped, not even for a tick, but kept pluggin' away at her same old plod, straight to the end of the track. The Hare had a lie-down by the side of the road and went out like a light. When he finally come to, and went flat out as fast as he could, he saw the Tortoise had already made it to the finish and was havin' a nice comfy kip after all her hard yakka. Slow and steady wins it, mate.
