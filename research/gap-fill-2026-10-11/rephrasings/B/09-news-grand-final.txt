LOCAL CLUB BREAKS THIRTY-YEAR DROUGHT FOR FIRST FLAG

Riverside Footy Club supporters were absolutely rapt Satdy arvo after the boys won the regional granny by two points.

Over four thousand punters rocked up to watch. Conditions were rough as guts, with the rain pissin' down in the first half and turnin' the deck into a slippery bog, but it cleared up later in the arvo.

Riverside were behind for most of the game. The match-winner came in the dyin' minute from eighteen-year-old Liam Carter, who reckons he had "the jitters that bad I couldn't even look" as the ball sailed through.

Not everyone was happy. A handful of visitin' fans were ropeable over a late call from the umpie and had to be asked to leave the stand. The coppers said no-one got pinched.

Club president Maria Vella said the locals had been hangin' out for this day for donkey's years. "Our players are cooked, but they'll be kickin' on tonight," she said. "Cheers to the volunteers, the mums and dads and the families who cook, wash the jumpers and cart the team to every game."

Celebrations were expected to kick on till all hours at the clubrooms. The team'll be back on the track for trainin' in February.
