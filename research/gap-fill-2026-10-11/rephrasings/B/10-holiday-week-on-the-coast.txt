Our Week on the Coast

We drove up Satdy mornin' and got in bright and early, before the traffic. The shack we'd rented was small and a bit tired-lookin', but it was only a hop, step and a jump from the beach, and there was an outdoor shower for hosin' off the sand.

The first three days were beaut, sunny as and warm, not a cloud in the sky. We had a swim every mornin' and spent the arvos readin' under a beach umbrella. Of an evenin' we'd wander along the water nice and slow as the sun went down, and most nights we had tea at the fish joint just up the road.

Wednesdy, the weather turned. It got windy as buggery, and the surf got big and hairy. The lifesavers shut the beach, and the red flags stayed up for two days. Copped the rough end of the pineapple a bit, I s'pose. We spent Thursdy playin' cards and watchin' the rain belt down on the windows. The kids were bored outta their brains by lunchtime.

By Friday the wind had dropped right off. We headed down late in the arvo and found the beach near enough empty, the sand still soggy from the storm. It was an absolute cracker of an evenin', best of the whole trip. No-one wanted to head home.
