"Right, so you want to hear about the worst night of my life? Okay. Sit down.

It was last winter, and it was really windy, the kind of wind that bends the trees. I'd been at my cousin's place, and I'll admit I was a bit inebriated. Not falling over, but, you know, cheerful.

I'm walking home, and next door's dog gets out. Big black thing, very loud, very noisy, barking at everything. It chases me down the street. I was petrified, honestly. I jumped over a fence into someone's garden and landed straight in their fish pond. Completely soaked. Wet from head to toe.

Then the owner opens the back door with a torch, and I'm standing there dripping, holding a garden gnome I'd grabbed on the way down. I was so embarrassed. I told him I was looking for my cat. I don't even have a cat. Terrible liar. I can't lie to save my life.

Anyway, he was a bit odd about it, sort of quiet and weird, and he just said, 'Climbing fences at night is dangerous, you know.' Then he closed the door. Unlucky, wasn't I? That's pretty much the whole story."
