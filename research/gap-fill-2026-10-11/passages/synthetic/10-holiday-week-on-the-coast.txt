Our Week on the Coast

We drove up on a Saturday morning and arrived early, before the traffic. The house we had rented was small and old, but it was only a short walk from the beach, and there was an outdoor shower for washing off the sand.

The first three days were sunny and warm, with clear blue skies. We swam every morning and spent the afternoons reading under an umbrella. In the evenings we walked slowly along the water as the sun went down, and we usually had dinner at a fish restaurant nearby.

On Wednesday the weather changed. It became very windy, and the waves grew large and dangerous. The lifeguards closed the beach, and the red flags stayed up for two days. We were a little unlucky, I suppose. We spent Thursday playing cards and watching the rain against the windows. The children were bored by lunchtime.

By Friday the wind had dropped. We went out late in the afternoon and found the beach almost empty, the sand still wet from the storm. It was the most beautiful evening of the whole trip. Nobody wanted to go home.
