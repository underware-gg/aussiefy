"So, how was the party on Saturday? I heard it went on until two."

"Oh, it was good. Pretty much the whole street came. Trevor did the barbecue, and his wife made about six salads."

"Trevor's always so generous like that, isn't he? He gave half the street a lift to the airport last Christmas."

"He is. Mind you, his brother turned up, and he's a bit annoying. He told the same joke four times. Then he stood near the fence smoking a cigarette and talking about how much his new boat cost."

"Was he showing off again?"

"Yes, kind of. Apparently he made a heap of money selling his business. And he's quite handsome, so all the neighbours were looking at him. I think Trevor's a little jealous, to be honest."

"Well, I'd be jealous too. A boat, though? He gets seasick at the beach."

"I know! Anyway, don't repeat any of that. Sandra's a terrible gossip, and if she hears I said it, the whole suburb will know by Tuesday."

"No, no, I won't. We shouldn't gossip anyway, should we? Pass me the tongs, will you? These sausages are burning."
