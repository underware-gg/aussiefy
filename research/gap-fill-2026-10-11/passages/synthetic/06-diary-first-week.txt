Monday, 3 February

First day at the new job. I was so nervous on the train that I nearly got off at the wrong station. The office is enormous, and my desk is tiny, squeezed in next to the photocopier. Everyone seemed friendly, though one man in accounts was quite strange. He stared at me through the whole meeting and never said a word.

Wednesday, 5 February

Very difficult day. The computer system crashed twice and I lost a whole morning of work. By six o'clock I was utterly fatigued. I came home, ate toast standing up, and went straight to bed.

Thursday, 6 February

Anxious all morning about the presentation. My hands were shaking. But it went well! My manager said it was the clearest summary she had seen this year. I was elated. I rang Mum from the car park.

Friday, 7 February

Small problem tonight. Daniel and I started to argue about whose turn it was to cook. It was a silly argument, really. We ended up ordering pizza and laughing about it. One week down. I think I'm going to like it there.
