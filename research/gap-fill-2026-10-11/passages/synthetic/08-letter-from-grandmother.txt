Dear Emily,

Thank you for your lovely letter and the photographs from the wedding. You looked beautiful, and the flowers in your hair were very pretty. Your grandfather says your new husband is a very attractive young man, which made me laugh, because he never notices anything like that.

You asked about the old days, when I was a girl. Well, we were very poor. After my father lost his job, our family was destitute for nearly two years. We lived in two rooms above a shop, and there were five of us in one bedroom. Many families in our street were just as impoverished. My mother sold her mother's ring to buy us shoes for school.

But people were generous. The baker left bread on our step every Friday, and he never once mentioned it. I have never forgotten that. It is the reason I always give what I can, even now.

The garden is doing well this year. The lemon tree is full, and the roses are out. Come and visit when you are back from your trip, and bring your husband. I will make a cake.

With all my love,
Grandma
