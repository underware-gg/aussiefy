LOCAL CLUB WINS FIRST PREMIERSHIP IN THIRTY YEARS

Supporters of the Riverside Football Club were jubilant on Saturday afternoon after the team won the regional grand final by two points.

The match was played in front of a crowd of more than four thousand people. Conditions were difficult, with heavy rain in the first half leaving the ground wet and slippery, but the weather cleared later in the afternoon.

Riverside trailed for most of the game. The winning goal came in the final minute from eighteen-year-old Liam Carter, who said he was "too nervous to look" as the ball went through.

Not everyone was happy. Several visiting fans were enraged by a late umpiring decision and had to be asked to leave the stand. Police said no arrests were made.

Club president Maria Vella said the community had waited a long time for this day. "Our players are weary, but they will celebrate tonight," she said. "Thank you to the volunteers, the parents and the families who cook, wash uniforms and drive the team to every game."

Celebrations were expected to continue late into the evening at the clubrooms. The team will return for training in February.
