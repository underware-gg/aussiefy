The New Neighbours

When the new family moved into the house across the road, the street was curious. They arrived late on a Thursday night with a van, a trailer and a very large dog.

Within a week, opinions had formed. Mrs Patel at number twelve thought they were rude, because the father had not waved back at her. Mr Doyle thought they were noisy, because the dog barked at the postman. Old Mr Fenwick, who had once been a terrible gossip, said they looked weird and probably owed money.

Then, one hot Saturday, a fire started in the dry grass behind Mrs Patel's fence. The new father saw the smoke first. He ran across the road with a hose, jumped the fence and put it out before the fire brigade arrived. He came back soaked and covered in ash.

The next morning Mrs Patel knocked on their door with a cake. She stayed for an hour, having a long chat with the mother on the front step. By the end of the month, nobody on the street remembered that they had ever seemed odd at all.
