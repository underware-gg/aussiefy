# Dial goals — 19 November 2026

Rob authorised aim/cap for rudeness and embellishment together. Cap remains the Python/CLI/MCP default;
the site sends aim explicitly. cap-baseline.json captures 288 translations before implementation; all
continue byte-for-byte under cap. These are original inputs, not a copied corpus.

Aim uses separate data, eligible-tier balancing and mixed garnish. Metadata, fit, repetition, themes,
grammar, document permissions and exact overrides retain their force. Level is unchanged. Deadset adds
nothing; Dinkum/Troppo have their own rates/budgets rather than borrowing their frequency from level.
Restricted forms retain their resolved permissions, including Troppo. Share/replay calls must repeat the
original dial_mode alongside the other dials; the frozen response and pin schemas are unchanged.

voice-calibration.json records 32 seeds, levels 1/3/5, all four vulgarities, Dinkum/Troppo and both modes.
The fixed source is authored for this measurement. Swaggie/Tradie/Troppo garnish is 77/146 sweary in aim
versus 25/136 in cap; the stronger available tier predominates among swears, while 69 clean choices remain.
This is a bounded tuning witness, not a claim of measured national speech. Read the samples as ordinary
banter: a few reactions, variety and gaps, no compulsory insult or swear at every spot. In this source the
eligible openers/closers top out at Uncle Bob; aim does not invent Tradie words or force vocative insults.

Standalone Thanks already offers Cheers. The missing case was Thanks for: its complement failed the
interjection boundary guard. Authored following context now permits this complement without relaxing
nouns or causal thanks-to. Tests cover live changes, pinning and selection with output coordinates.
The short-query rebuke defect was false evidence, not simply a threshold: raw tokenisation treated don
from don't as shared subject matter. Lookup now uses normalised contractions and existing morphology,
retaining negation/anchor checks; straight and curly forms agree. No global threshold increase or phrase
exception was added. Both scalar and batched lookup use the same rule.
