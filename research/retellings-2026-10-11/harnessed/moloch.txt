What sort of sphinx, knocked up outta concrete 'n aluminium, cracked their scones open like eggs 'n scoffed the lot, brains, imagination, the whole kit 'n caboodle? Who does that to a bloke, ay?

Moloch! That's who. Moloch! Bein' lonely as a shag on a rock! Filth! Ugliness, the face-like-a-dropped-pie kind! Wheelie bins rottin' in the heat, and quid you'll never get yer mitts on, not a brass razoo to yer name! Ankle biters screamin' blue murder under the stairs! Young fellas, barely out of short pants, bawlin' their eyes out in whole armies! Old blokes blubberin' on the park benches!

Moloch! Moloch! Moloch the nightmare ya never wake up from! Moloch with no love in him, cold as a mother-in-law's kiss! Moloch livin' in yer noggin! Moloch the hangin' judge, passin' sentence on every bloke that walks past!
