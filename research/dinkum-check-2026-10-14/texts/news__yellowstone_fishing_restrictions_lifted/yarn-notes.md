## dinkum

- The riff "bone dry half the year" for creeks is wrong for this text: it adds a false fact (Yellowstone's streams aren't dry), and the engine draft spliced it into a list ("rivers, creeks, bone dry half the year, and lakes"). I didn't use it. Riffs that claim facts shouldn't be offered for news.
- The genre is unclear on a press release's direct appeal ("Anglers: Please fish..."). Is it an "official statement" to keep as said, or the release's own prose? I voiced it with every instruction kept ("quick smart", "come good", "till they're stuffed").
- The repeated-phrase warning ("and the water" x3) was fair.
- The headings are in plain text; I Aussified them lightly ("still on", "till sunup") and kept them short.
