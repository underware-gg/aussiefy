MAMMOTH HOT SPRINGS, Wyo. – From Friday, Sept. 4, the last of the fishin' restrictions on the Firehole, Madison and Gibbon (downstream of Norris Campground) rivers and every tributary that runs into 'em are comin' off, now the weather and the water have cooled down. Anglers'll be right to fish these rivers, plus most of the park's other rivers, creeks and lakes, every day from sunup to sundown, as set out in the 2026 Fishing Regulations booklet.

These rivers were shut to fishin' altogether from July 15, then that got wound back to partial restrictions on Aug. 28 once things picked up. The restrictions and closures had to go in while the rivers were runnin' low and the water was gettin' hotter than 68 degrees Fahrenheit (20 degrees Celsius), which can knock fish off.

Park staff'll keep an eye on the water temperatures. Depending on how they rate the conditions, temporary restrictions or closures could go back in.

Update: Yellowstone lifts full closures on Firehole, Madison and Gibbon rivers

Partial restrictions still on from 2 p.m. till sunup next day

MAMMOTH HOT SPRINGS, Wyo. – From Friday, Aug. 28, anglers can fish the Firehole, Madison and Gibbon (downstream of Norris Campground) rivers and every tributary that runs into 'em from sunup till 2 p.m. each day. With cooler weather and cooler water temperatures, and a bit of rain in the current forecast, the full closures can come off.

The full closures went in on July 15 cos low water flows and water temperatures hotter than 68 degrees Fahrenheit (20 degrees Celsius) can kill fish. The water's been coolin' down overnight, so there's fishin' for part of the day. Anglers can fish most of the park's other rivers, creeks and lakes every day from sunup to sundown, as set out in the 2026 Fishing Regulations booklet.

Staff'll keep checkin' the water temperatures. Depending on how the conditions shape up, the temporary restrictions might come off, or more closures could go in.

Anglers: Please fish in the coolest part of the day and land yer fish quick smart. Don't play hooked trout till they're stuffed. Handle fish gently and keep 'em in the water as much as ya can, and let 'em come good before you let 'em go. Pitchin' in like this'll look after the park’s fisheries, and if conditions get worse it might save the park havin' to ban fishin' at all hours on some rivers and creeks.
