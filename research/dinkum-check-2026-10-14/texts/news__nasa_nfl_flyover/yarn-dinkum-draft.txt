Now here's one for the gridiron fans. As part of the agency's Inspiration Tour flyover, NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman's going to be at the stick of one of three F-5 fighter jets, roaring over to kick off the New York Jets vs. Cleveland Browns game in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on Sunday, Oct. 11.

Before the game, the NASA crew will be out having a yarn with the fans at the agency's Experience Zone, set up at the Jets Tailgate Zone outside the stadium, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. EDT. Fans can pop under the agency's Artemis dome and find out more about NASA heading back to the Moon, have a crack at the hands-on games, and snap a few pics with a whopping great inflatable SLS (Space Launch System) rocket.

The Inspiration Tour's pulling up all over the country, getting the uni mob, industry and public sector folks together in the one spot to hook the agency up with the people, the tech and the organisations that keep America out in front in space.

The whole show wraps up with MAX POWER, a public expo of American air and space know-how, on Saturday, Nov. 7, and Sunday, Nov. 8, on and around the agency's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Put on to honour America's historic 250th anniversary, it's a multi-day do that's good for the whole family, showing off the next-gen aircraft, spacecraft, driverless vehicles and tech that'll help shape how people get about in air and space down the track.
