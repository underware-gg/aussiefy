As part of the agency’s Inspiration Tour flyover, NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman's going to be at the controls of one of three F-5 fighter jets to get the New York Jets vs. Cleveland Browns game underway in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on Sunday, Oct. 11.

Before the game, NASA's crew will be having a yarn with the fans at the agency’s Experience Zone, set up at the Jets Tailgate Zone outside the stadium, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. EDT. Fans can rock up under the agency’s Artemis dome and find out a bit more about NASA heading back to the Moon, have a go at the hands-on games, and snap a few pics with a big blow-up SLS (Space Launch System) rocket.

With stops right round the country, NASA’s Inspiration Tour gets the academic, industry and public sector mob together, so the agency can link up with the people, technologies and organisations that keep America out in front in space.

The whole tour wraps up with MAX POWER, a public show-and-tell of American air and space know-how, on Saturday, Nov. 7, and Sunday, Nov. 8, on and around the agency’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Put on in honour of America’s historic 250th anniversary, the multi-day, family-friendly do will show off the next-generation aircraft, spacecraft, autonomous vehicles and technologies that'll help shape how we get about by air and space down the track.
