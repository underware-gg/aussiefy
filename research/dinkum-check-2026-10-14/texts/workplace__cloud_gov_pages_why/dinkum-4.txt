The hardest bit often isn't writing the content or designing the site, it's getting the security and privacy compliance sorted so you can launch the actual website and keep it running. For that, you might have to bring in a contractor or pile extra work on your agency’s web team.

Fair go, it shouldn’t be like that.

## Why Pages?

Pages looks after our fellow federal partners by handling the backend and compliance work for launching and running a website, and doing it properly, so you can put your know-how into your content.

- **FedRAMP Certified Platform** Pages is hosted on cloud.gov, a platform with its security and compliance checked off by the FedRAMP Joint Authorization Board, so you can count on your site being fast, reliable, and ready for any surprise rush of traffic.

- **Easy previews and content approvals** Pages builds draft updates to your site at a special preview URL. That way your team knows their changes will come up right when they're pushed to the main site, and it's easy to get the tick from stakeholders on content updates.

## Why static websites?

Pages makes static websites rather than using a more complicated content management system (CMS) like Drupal or WordPress. Static websites have a few things going for them:

- **Less complexity and vulnerability:** Steering clear of a traditional CMS means steering clear of headaches like looking after server applications (no PHP or Apache / IIS), on top of database scaling and redundancy. A production-level static site generally needs one simple static file server or service, not dozens. So the whole website's easier to look after and has fewer weak spots.

- **Update content without writing code:** Static websites often keep their content in simple text files that anyone on the team can edit. The GitHub editor lets any team member make basic edits to their text files, however much or little code they've written.

- **Easy to host and maintain:** Hosting static website files on Pages is dead easy. You can make the most of scaling services like Amazon S3 through Pages, where you only pay for what you use.

- **Fully customizable:** Designers and developers can shape static websites into whatever form they like without having to strip out CMS-provided features and defaults. You can build all sorts of sites with static sites, from organisation homepages or intranets to pre-generated web application program interfaces that show data from a separate server.
