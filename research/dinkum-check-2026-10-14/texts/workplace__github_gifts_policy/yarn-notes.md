## dinkum

- Planned "littlie" for "kid" takes the wrong sense: "Recommending a friend’s kid to a prestigious private
  school" is a child of any school age, and "littlie" means a little kid. Declined. "mate" for the first "friend"
  was fine (I kept "friend’s" in the list item so it isn't used twice).
- Lint suggestions took the wrong sense in a policy whose subject is gifts: "gifts"/"gift" -> "pressie" (a
  term of art here; "accept an informal pressie" undercuts the policy); "ok" -> "righto" (OK_THEN) on "it isn't
  ok", which is the adjective, not the interjection; "dinner" -> "tea" is ambiguous for a customer dinner.
  Left them all.
- Lookups empty: "bleachers, cheap seats" (no "the cheap seats"), "pull strings"; "swag" returned only the
  bedroll sense, though the tech-merch "swag" is what the text means (and Aussies use it); "not a big deal" gave
  "a fair bit"; "check with someone first" gave "workmate", "how ya goin'".
- RIFF "burnin' a hole in their pocket" for "dosh" didn't fit the suitcase of cash for an official (it adds a
  joke the dinkum guidance rules out); not used.
- Accepted on the first submit.
