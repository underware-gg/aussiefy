# The FOLD Auction

This FAQ covers FOLD Auction 2, the second public FOLD auction. It's for anyone who had a go in the
auction, or who has to claim FOLD after it. The questions are split into four lots: the auction
itself, participation, claiming and transferability, and safety.

## About the auction

### What is FOLD Auction 2?

FOLD Auction 2 was the second public FOLD auction. It ran on the Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction
mechanism.

It came after the first FOLD Auction, which was held July 8–10, 2026.

Auction 2 was kept to **2% of total FOLD supply** on purpose. It was timed around the changeover to
general FOLD transferability. The idea was to spread FOLD a bit wider and help get deeper,
longer-term on-chain liquidity off the ground.

### Why was there a second FOLD Auction?

Auction 2 was a limited distribution step before general FOLD transferability kicked in on Aug 19.

It was kept small on purpose, at **2% of total FOLD supply**. It started with a floor that matched
the clearing price of the first FOLD Auction.

The auction gave everyone a transparent, public way of handing out more FOLD before it became
generally transferable. It also chipped in for long-term on-chain liquidity.

**50% of proceeds were committed to long-term on-chain liquidity support.**

Auction 2 wrapped up not long before general FOLD transferability was switched on.

### What happened in the first FOLD Auction?

The first FOLD Auction ran from **July 8–10, 2026**. It used the Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction
mechanism too.

That one's done and dusted. Its clearing price was **0.02154816 USDC / FOLD**, which is also the
floor for FOLD Auction 2.

Anyone who took part in the first auction can still get to claiming and the post-auction info
through the official [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).

### What is a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction?

A Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) is a price-discovery mechanism for handing out tokens. It's
built on Uniswap infrastructure.

- The auction runs over a number of separate periods.
- Participants put their bids in through the official Uniswap CCA interface.
- Successful bids within a period all clear at the same clearing price.
- The minimum price for each period after that is whichever is higher: the original floor price or
  the clearing price of the period before.
- The minimum price can go up in later periods. It never drops below the applicable earlier clearing
  price or the original floor.
- Any tokens that weren't handed out earlier are still up for grabs in the final period.

The CCA hands tokens out bit by bit and sets one price for everyone within each period. It's built
that way to cut down on gas competition, take the sting out of sniping, and let clearing prices
sort themselves out through people taking part in the auction.

[Read more about Uniswap CCAs](https://cca.uniswap.org).

### Did the auction determine the price of FOLD after transferability?

Nah. The auction worked out allocations and a clearing price within the auction itself. It didn't
set the market price of FOLD when general transferability began.

Nobody can tell you what future market prices will be.

## Participation

<Callout type='warning'>
  Auction 2 closed on Aug 19, 2026 at 13:06 UTC. The steps in this section show how the auction
  worked. You can't put in a new bid.
</Callout>

### How did participation work?

1. Participants headed to the official [FOLD Auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).
2. They followed the verified link through to the Uniswap CCA interface.
3. They got the required verification process done through the official flow.
4. They put their bids in during the auction window.
5. Once the auction closed, they could claim any allocated FOLD and unspent USDC through the
   verified auction interface.

Participants needed USDC on Ethereum Mainnet. They had to read through the auction terms and
eligibility requirements before they took part.

### Who could participate?

Participation was open to eligible participants. It was subject to jurisdiction, identity
verification, AML and sanctions screening, and other applicable restrictions.

The official verification process decided who was eligible. The auction terms governed eligibility.

Getting through verification didn't guarantee eligibility or an allocation.

### Was KYC required?

Yep. Participants had to get identity verification done through the official verification process
before they took part.

The verification process sorted out eligibility and did the applicable AML and sanctions screening.

Participation was subject to the auction terms and applicable restrictions.

## Claiming and transferability

### When can participants claim FOLD?

Claiming was set to open straight after the auction closed.

Participants claim allocated FOLD and any unspent USDC through the verified auction interface.

Claiming and general FOLD transferability are two separate things.

### When did general FOLD transferability begin?

FOLD Auction 2 closed Aug 19 at **13:06 UTC (9:06 AM ET)**.

General FOLD transferability could be switched on not long after, at or after **14:00 UTC (10 AM
ET)**.

At or after Unix timestamp **`1787148000`**, anyone could call the `tge()` function on the FOLD
contract to switch on general transferability. The TGE transaction went through on Aug 19, 2026 at
14:00:23 UTC. `tgeTimestamp()` returns `1787148023`. FOLD is now generally transferable.

FOLD Auction 2, claiming, general FOLD transferability, and Network Alpha are separate milestones
in the bigger Interfold launch sequence. For the details, have a squiz at
[FOLD Transferability](/faq/transferability).

## Safety

### How do I know I’m using the official auction?

Only use auction, claiming, verification, and support links published through official Interfold
channels.

The official Interfold channels are:

- [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction)
- [Interfold website](https://www.theinterfold.com/)
- [Interfold X account](https://x.com/theinterfold)
- [Interfold documentation](https://docs.theinterfold.com/)

Don't trust:

- Auction, claiming, or support links sent through DMs out of the blue.
- Token or contract addresses copied from unofficial sources.
- Impersonator accounts.
- Private wallet-connection links.
- Anyone asking for your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
- Anyone asking you to pay up in exchange for support.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Interfold team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet
  backup, or access to your wallet.
</Callout>
