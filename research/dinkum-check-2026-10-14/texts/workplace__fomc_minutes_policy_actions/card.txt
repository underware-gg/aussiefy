dinkum-3: Accurate and natural but lighter than a level-4 brief asks.
  minor: 'all members agreed to put the target range for the federal funds rate up 1/4 percentage point to 3-3/4 to 4 percent' (for 'all members agreed to raise the target range for the federal funds rate 1/4 percentage point to 3-3/4 to 4 percent'): US spelling percent left in the narration where Australian would write per cent.
  minor: 'voted unanimously to give a 1/4 percentage point increase in the primary credit rate the nod' (for 'voted unanimously to approve a 1/4 percentage point increase in the primary credit rate'): Split idiom around a long noun phrase is clunky.
  minor: 'Members noted the unemployment rate had hardly budged' (for 'Members noted that the unemployment rate was largely unchanged'): Register light for a level-4 brief; much of the narration stays near plain English.

yarn-dinkum: Most on-brief in weight, but one wrong-sense idiom and a few liberties.
  major: "they doubled down on the FOMC's policy of keeping ample reserves" (for "reaffirmed the FOMC's policy of maintaining ample reserves"): Doubling down means escalating a commitment, often stubbornly; the original only reaffirms it. Also an Americanism.
  minor: 'Members reckoned the unemployment rate had barely budged' (for 'Members noted that the unemployment rate was largely unchanged'): Reckoned makes a factual observation sound like an opinion.
  minor: 'Undertake open market operations as necessary to maintain the federal funds rate in a target range of 3-3/4 to 4 per cent.' (for 'Undertake open market operations as necessary to maintain the federal funds rate in a target range of 3-3/4 to 4 percent.'): Alters the wording of a verbatim official quotation.

dinkum-4: Accurate and careful but too light for the brief, with a couple of clunky joins.
  minor: 'The same vote also took in sign-off on the statement below for release at 2:00 p.m.' (for 'The vote also encompassed approval of the statement below for release at 2:00 p.m.'): Awkward, unidiomatic phrasing.
  minor: 'solid growth in economic activity had kept on' (for 'solid growth in economic activity had continued'): Kept on without a following verb reads as broken.
  major: 'At the end of the discussion, the Committee voted to direct the Federal Reserve Bank of New York' (for 'At the conclusion of the discussion, the Committee voted to direct the Federal Reserve Bank of New York'): Register well short of level 4; much of the narration is barely changed.
