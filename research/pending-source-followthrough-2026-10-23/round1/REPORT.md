## Refinements worth considering

- Nature strip: council definition covers the verge from property boundary to road, possibly including footpaths/trees. Current grass-between-footpath-and-road gloss is too narrow for that usage; VERGE remains appropriate.
- Drover: gender-neutral person/worker is clearer than man; no male-only occupation rule follows from evidence.
- Gully: technical eroded-channel source is narrower than the general valley/ravine gloss. Preserve partial scope.
- Tragic: new primary usage is cricket tragic, not isolated adjective tragic; do not erase the construction distinction.
- Bushfire: government source contrasts grassfire and bushfire; do not use it to certify every detail of the current broad gloss.
