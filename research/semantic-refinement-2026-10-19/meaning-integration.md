# Reviewed lexical and argument-context integration

Baseline: `2687d7f`. This implements the clear corrections from the 30- and 60-entry research batches.
Those ledgers remain historical snapshots; their proposed source files are now integrated for 85 entry
IDs (110 additional URLs). A URL attests only the scope recorded in its ledger, not every trigger.

## Applied boundaries

- `START_ACTIVITY` gives kick-off intransitive starts rather than creation or assembly. Rough-construction
  triggers retain knock-together; neutral putting together and bringing forth remain distinct.
- `COMRADESHIP` separates peers from unqualified devotion or loyalty.
- Political people remain distinct from generic authorities and government institutions.
- Another crack at retains attempt/opportunity contexts; rebirth and new-lease expressions stay distinct.
- Lout and driver senses are separate. Hoon can serve both; yobbo and peb do not erase a stated driving role.
  Redundant direct replacements on hoon were removed because they reintroduced both concepts at once.
- Sundowner retains attested vagrant/tramp uses, without turning an itinerant worker into a vagrant.
- Sleep-out requires sleeping accommodation; a bare enclosed porch does not establish that purpose.
- Tuckshop retains school food-service settings; a bare school lunchroom is not necessarily a shop.
- Tell-them wording preserves its plural or gender-neutral reference; the Castle gloss concerns asking prices.
- Wasted-result uses of for-sweet-bugger-all remain available under existing embellishment permissions;
  ambiguous causeless for-no-reason wording no longer establishes that concept.
- Clarified sledge, old-mate and not-the-full-quid glosses, and two dormant film/advertising provenance notes.
  Existing omission status and flags are unchanged.

Poor-buggers, brave-bastards and lucky-buggers-still-breathing still need the semantic-effect slice. Mob,
snags-short-of-a-barbie and the other provisional cases retain their recorded evidence limitations.

the Australian National Dictionary directly lists it:
https://www.abc.net.au/news/2016-08-23/australian-national-dictionary-thousands-of-words-added/7776480

Hills Hoist is a named clothesline reference. The Powerhouse's own collection record documents the brand
and winding mechanism; no generic clothesline is assigned the brand:
https://collection.powerhouse.com.au/object/166945

The two understated appetite phrases use existing feed/meal and tucker/food vocabulary. Their sources
attest those components. Direct conversions retain matching conditional wording; hunger plans and lookup
also offer writer-only templates where a person must realise tense, subject or modal force. This does not
claim automatic tense conversion for every appetite sentence, or exclusive Australian origin.

## Shared argument reading

The grammar change reuses existing POS tags and compiled pattern structure. Preposition-headed spans are
not noun arguments. Temporal ambiguity is declined only for an unanchored terminal noun slot immediately
after the pattern's head verb. Temporal subjects and lexically anchored arguments remain valid. Compound
heads and gerunds are retained; mixed object/time captures stay plain rather than undergo span trimming.
This remains bounded interpretation: location-adverb ambiguity and gerund/preposition boundaries are not
claimed solved. No second parser, model service or slang-specific engine branch was added.
