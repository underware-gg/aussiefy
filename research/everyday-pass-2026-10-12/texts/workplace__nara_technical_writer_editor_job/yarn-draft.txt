Here's what the job involves at the GS-12. If you get picked at a lower grade, no worries: you'll get the chance to learn the whole box and dice, and they'll give you training to help you grow into the role.

As a TECHNICAL WRITER-EDITOR, you'll:

Knock the documents and the Federal Register publications into proper shape, so they get published on time, without mistakes, and with full legal effect.

Keep the work on track, whether you're going it alone, pulling your weight in a team or running the show, so every document gets published on time and right, and all the program goals get ticked off.

Get your head around what the customers need at every step of the Federal Register publishing process, and look after them properly, as part of the Office of the Federal Register's job.

Know your way around the technical gear for the Federal Register publishing processes and use it properly, including the IT, the old legacy systems and the rest of the technical kit.

Specialised Experience: For the GS-09, you need one year of specialised experience at about the same level of difficulty and responsibility as the GS-07 grade in the Federal service. Specialised experience for this job includes:

Editing legal or regulatory documents written by other people, to make sure they stick to the style and formatting rules before they're got ready for publication; AND

Spotting and working through production problems, and coming up with fixes for them; AND

Getting information across well, and tactfully, to people inside and/or outside the organisation, to help hit the program goals.
