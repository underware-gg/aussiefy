Nicked an airline blanket by accident; didn't realise you weren't allowed. What do I do now?

Listen to this. I'm properly new to this travel caper. I was on a long-haul flight, and the airline handed out blankets and pillows. I had no idea I was meant to leave the blanket behind, so I scooped it up with the rest of my gear and off I went. I'd reckoned that since it was a bit of a cheap thing, it was part of the ticket price or something. I wasn't fussed about keeping it, but I was half asleep and dead cold and I just grabbed it with the rest of my stuff. No bugger said a word to me while I did it.

It wasn't till I got home that I found the blanket again and spotted a tiny tag that said 'do not remove from plane.' Naturally, I felt a shocker about nicking the thing by accident. I'll never do it again, obviously, but I still feel flamin' guilty about doing it this time. Is there a way I can get it back to the airline? Or should I just forget about it, lesson learned?

Sorry if this is a dumb question, I just went into a bit of a flap when I realised I'd pinched it by accident!!

Just so you know, the airline was Aer Lingus.

* * *

If you want to stop the guilt hanging round, give the airline a ring and ask them what they'd like you to do with the blanket. Tell them you took it by mistake because you were off with the fairies when you got off the plane.

Odds are they'll tell you to keep it, because it'd cost them more to get it back in the post and put it back in the blanket pile than it would to just buy a new one. But once they've told you straight to keep it, you've got nothing to worry about. And in the (less likely) case that they do ask for it back, you can post it back to them, and that'll get rid of the guilt too. No dramas either way.

You can find out how to get hold of them at https://www.aerlingus.com/support/contact-us.
