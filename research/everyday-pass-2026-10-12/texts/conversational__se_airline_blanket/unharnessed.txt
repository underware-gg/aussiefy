Pinched an airline blanket by accident, didn't know you weren't allowed. What do I do?

I'm pretty new to travelling in general. On a long flight, the airline handed out blankets and pillows. I had no idea I was meant to leave the blanket behind, so I scooped it up with all my gear and hopped off. I'd figured since it was a fairly cheap blanket it was part of the ticket price or something. I wasn't fussed about keeping it, but I was half asleep and freezing and just grabbed it with the rest of my stuff. Nobody said a word to me.

It wasn't till I got home that I found the blanket again and spotted a tiny tag saying 'do not remove from plane.' Course, I felt rotten about accidentally nicking it. I'll never do it again, obviously, but I still feel real guilty about this time. Is there some way I can give it back to the airline? Or should I just let it go, lesson learned?

Sorry if this is a daft question, I just sort of panicked when I realised I'd pinched it by accident!!

Just so you know, the airline was Aer Lingus.

* * *

To get rid of any guilt hanging round, you can ring the airline and ask what they'd like you to do with the blanket. Tell 'em you took it by mistake because you weren't properly awake when you got off the plane.

Odds are they'll tell you to keep it, because it'd cost them more to get it back in the post and put it back in the blanket pile than to just buy a new one. But if they've told you straight out to keep it, you've got nothing to worry about. And if (less likely) they do ask for it back, you can post it back, and that'll sort out any leftover guilt too.

You can find how to get hold of them at https://www.aerlingus.com/support/contact-us.
