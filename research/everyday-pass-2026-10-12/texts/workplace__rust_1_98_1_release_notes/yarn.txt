## What's in 1.98.1

Rust 1.98.1 sorts out a [miscompilation in vtable generation](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/161441).

Back in Rust 1.98.0, every now and then, rustc would go and spit out a trait object vtable with a null pointer sittin' where a function pointer should've been. Not flash. That means undefined behaviour in the code it emits. Sometimes this'll "just" give you a segfault when the null pointer gets loaded, but it can also be the excuse for any old thing to happen (as is par for the course with UB). The whole box and dice, basically, and none of it good.
