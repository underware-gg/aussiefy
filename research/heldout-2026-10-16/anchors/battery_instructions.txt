Before cleaning the demonstration lantern, disconnect it from the charger and switch it off. Wait five minutes before opening the battery cover. Hold the cover firmly while loosening the two screws. Do not touch the metal contacts with a wet cloth. Wipe the outside with a dry cloth, replace the cover, and tighten both screws by hand. If the case is cracked or the battery is leaking, stop using the lantern and contact the supplier. These instructions apply only to model L12.
