Here's what the job involves at the GS-12. If you get picked at a lower grade level, you'll get the chance to learn how to do all of these duties, and you'll get training to help you grow into the role.

As a TECHNICAL WRITER-EDITOR, you'll:

Handle the contents of documents and Federal Register publications properly so they get published in a way that's legally effective, on time and accurate.

Manage the work, whether you're doing it on your own, as part of a team, or leading others, so all documents get published on time and accurately and all the program goals get ticked off.

Understand and properly look after what customers need across every part of the Federal Register publication process, so the Office of the Federal Register's program responsibilities get met.

Understand and properly use the technical resources available for, and tied to, the Federal Register publication processes, including IT, legacy systems and other technical resources.
