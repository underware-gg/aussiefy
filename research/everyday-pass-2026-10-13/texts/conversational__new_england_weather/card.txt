yarn: A faithful, natural light Aussie retelling of Twain at the asked weight; the best of the three.
  minor: "Now, you might've heard this one." (added): An added yarn opener that renders nothing; a stock framing tag.
  minor: 'always flat out at its work' (for 'always attending strictly to business'): Flat out means very busy, which loses the joke of the weather as a punctilious tradesman attending strictly to business.

unharnessed: A sound light retelling, a touch more modernised than A, with a couple of forced choices.
  minor: 'I reverently reckon' (for 'I reverently believe'): Reckon clashes with reverently and repeats in the next sentence.
  minor: "Strewth, what awful thunder you've got here!" (for '“Why, what awful thunder you have here!”'): The speakers are strangers visiting New England; giving them an Aussie oath is odd.
  minor: 'muck about and learn the trade' (for 'experiment and learn how'): Muck about implies aimless fooling rather than experimenting.

engine: Mostly untranslated, with one wrong-sense insertion; faithful but under the brief.
  major: 'cannot, too right, know how the natives feel about spring' (for 'cannot, of course, know how the natives feel about spring'): Too right is an emphatic yes, not a parenthetical of course; it breaks the sentence.
  major: 'the Maker who made us all makes everything in New England but the weather' (for 'the Maker who made us all makes everything in New England but the weather'): Representative of the whole text being left untranslated, well under a level-2 brief.
