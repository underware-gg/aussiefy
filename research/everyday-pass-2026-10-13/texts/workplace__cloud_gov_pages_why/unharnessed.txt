The hardest bit often isn't writing the content or designing the site, it's getting the security and privacy compliance sorted so you can launch and keep the actual website running. For that, you might have to bring in a contractor or pile extra work on your agency's web team.

It shouldn't be like that.

## Why Pages?

Pages looks after our fellow federal partners by expertly handling the backend and compliance grunt work to launch and run a website, so you can put your know-how into your content.

- **FedRAMP Certified Platform** Pages is hosted on cloud.gov, a platform with security and compliance checked off by the FedRAMP Joint Authorization Board, which means you can trust your site'll be fast, reliable, and ready for any unexpected rush of traffic.

- **Easy previews and content approvals** Pages builds draft updates to your site at a special preview URL. That way your team knows their changes will show up right when they're pushed to the main site, and it's easy to get the tick-off on content updates from stakeholders.

## Why static websites?

Pages makes static websites instead of using a more complicated content management system (CMS) like Drupal or WordPress. Static websites have a few things going for them:

- **Less complexity and vulnerability:** Steering clear of a traditional CMS means steering clear of headaches like keeping server applications going (no PHP or Apache / IIS), on top of database scaling and redundancy. Production-level static sites usually need one simple static file server or service instead of dozens. That means the whole website's easier to look after and has fewer weak spots.

- **Update content without writing code:** Static websites often keep content in simple text files anyone on the team can edit. The GitHub editor lets any team member do basic edits to their text files, no matter how much code they've written before.

- **Easy to host and maintain:** It's dead easy to host static website files on Pages. You can easily make use of scaling services like Amazon S3 through Pages, where you only pay for what you use.

- **Fully customizable:** Designers and developers can shape static websites into whatever they like without having to rip out CMS-provided features and defaults. Static sites can be used to build all sorts of sites, from organisation homepages or intranets to pre-generated web application program interfaces that show data from a separate server.
