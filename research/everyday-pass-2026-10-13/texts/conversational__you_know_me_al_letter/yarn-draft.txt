_Terre Haute, Indiana, September 6._

FRIEND AL: Well, Al old mate, I reckon you seen in the paper where I been sold to the White Sox. Believe me Al it comes as a real surprise to me and I bet it did to all you good old mates down home. You could of knocked me over with a feather when the old man come up to me and says Jack I've sold you to the Chicago Americans.

I didn't have no idea that anything like that was coming off. For five minutes I was just dum, gobsmacked, couldn't say a word.

He says We aren't getting what you are worth but I want you to go up to that big league and show those blokes that there is a Central League on the map. He says Go and pitch the ball you been pitching down here and it'll be dead easy. He says All you need is the nerve and Walsh or no one else won't be a patch on you.

So I says I would do the best I could and I thanked him for the fair go I got in Terre Haute. They always was good to me here and though I did more than my share I always felt that my work was appresiated. We are finishing second and I done most of it. I can't help but be proud of my first year's record in professional baseball and you know I am not skiting when I say that Al.

Well Al it will seem funny to be up there in the big show when I never was properly in a big city before. But I reckon I seen enough of life not to be spooked by the high buildings eh Al?

I will just give them what I got and if they don't like it they can send me back to the old Central and I will be happy as Larry.

I didn't know anybody was giving me the once-over, but one of the blokes told me that Jack Doyle the White Sox scout was down here looking at me when Grand Rapids was here. I beat them twice in that serious. You know Grand Rapids never had Buckley's against me when I was right. I shut them out in the first game and they got one run in the second on account of Flynn misjuging that fly ball. Anyway Doyle liked my work and he wired Comiskey to buy me. Comiskey come back with an offer and they excepted it. I dunno how much they got but anyway I am sold to the big league and believe me Al I will make good.

Well Al I will be home in a few days and we will have some of the beaut old times. Regards to all the boys and tell them I am still their mate and not all up myself over this big league business.

Your mate, JACK.
