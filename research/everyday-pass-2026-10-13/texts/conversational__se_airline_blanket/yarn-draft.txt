Took an airline blanket off the plane by accident; didn't realise it wasn't allowed. What should I do?

Listen to this. I'm properly new to travelling in general. When I flew on a long flight, the airline handed out blankets and pillows. I had no idea I was meant to leave the blanket behind, so I took it when I grabbed all my things, and off I went. I'd figured that since it was a fairly cheap blanket, they were part of the ticket price or something. I wasn't all that keen on keeping it, but I was half asleep and dead cold and I just grabbed it with the rest of my stuff. No bugger said a word to me as I did it.

It wasn't till I got home that I found the blanket again and spotted a tiny tag that said 'do not remove from plane.' Course, I felt rotten about pinching the blanket by accident. I'll never do it again, obviously, but I still feel flamin' guilty about doing it this time. Is there a way I can get it back to the airline? Or should I just forget about it, now I've learned my lesson?

Sorry if this is a silly question, I just sort of panicked when I realised I'd nicked it by accident!!

Just so you know, the airline was Aer Lingus.

* * *

So you're not left feeling guilty, you can ring the airline and ask them what they'd like you to do with the blanket. Tell them you took it by mistake cos you weren't fully awake when you got off the plane.

Most likely they'll tell you to keep it, cos it'd cost them more to get it back in the post and put it back in the blanket pool than it would to just buy a new blanket. But if they've told you straight out to keep it, then you've got nothing to worry about. And in the (less likely) case they do ask you to send it back, you can send it back, which'll clear your conscience too.

You can find out how to get in touch with them at https://www.aerlingus.com/support/contact-us.
