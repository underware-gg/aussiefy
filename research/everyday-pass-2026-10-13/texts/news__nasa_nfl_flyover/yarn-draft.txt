NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman, the agency's top boss, will be at the controls of one of three F-5 fighter jets to kick off the New York Jets v Cleveland Browns gridiron game in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on Sunday 11 October, as part of the agency's Inspiration Tour flyover.

NASA team members will be having a yarn with fans at the agency's Experience Zone, set up at the Jets Tailgate Zone outside the stadium, from 10am to 1pm EDT before the game. Fans can find out more about NASA's return to the Moon under the agency's Artemis dome, have a crack at hands-on games, and snap photos with a big blow-up SLS (Space Launch System) rocket.

With stops right around the country, NASA's Inspiration Tour gets academics, industry and public sector players together to link the agency up with the people, technologies and organisations that drive American leadership in space.

The tour will wrap up with MAX POWER, a public showcase of American air and space know-how, on Saturday 7 November and Sunday 8 November, at and around the agency's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Held to mark America's historic 250th anniversary, the family-friendly event will run over several days and show off the next-generation aircraft, spacecraft, driverless vehicles and technologies that will help shape the future of getting about in air and space.
