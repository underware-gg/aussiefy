## Recommendations for active entries

These are bounded static-mapping findings and proposals, not a claim that every resulting output has
been reproduced. Confirm realised output before changing number or morphology. Preserve each expression;
correcting a mapping does not mean removing the vocabulary.

| Entry | Evidence-backed boundary | Proposed action |
|---|---|---|
| `tell-him-hes-dreamin` | The original film scenes reject unrealistic **asking prices**, not a lowball purchase offer. The trigger also accepts `them` while the stored form fixes `him`. | Correct the reversed gloss; verify and preserve the reference/number. Retain broader dream-on use. |
| `the-pollies` | Pollies denotes plural politicians; current triggers mix singular officeholders with government and generic authorities. | Preserve number and distinguish political people from institutions or non-political authority. |
| `another-crack-at` | An attempt/opportunity construction shares triggers with rebirth and a new lease of something. | Separate these senses and preserve complements/prepositions. Deliberate comic paraphrase may still be useful. |
| `sundowner` | Both the narrower late-arriving food/lodging seeker and broader tramp/vagrant meanings are documented. An itinerant worker need not be either. | Preserve the attested broad reading; review worker contexts and qualify the gloss's motive as a narrower usage. |
| `a-yobbo` | A general rude lout need not be a reckless driver; the shared concept includes specific driving roles. | Preserve lout uses and avoid dropping a stated driving role. |
| `the-tuckshop` | A school food service differs from a room used only for eating packed lunches. | Keep food-service uses; require that context for the bare school-lunchroom trigger. |

The strongest immediate correction is supported by [ABC's transcript replaying *The Castle*](https://www.abc.net.au/listen/programs/worldtoday/trading-post-published-for-last-time/1122154).
The person/number distinction has direct lexical support in [Collins's pollies entry](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/pollies).
The sundowner recommendation explicitly preserves the broader reading in [American Heritage](https://www.ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=sundowner)
alongside [Cambridge's narrower account](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/sundowner).
The school food-service boundary uses the [Queensland education department's own terminology](https://education.qld.gov.au/parents-and-carers/school-information/life-at-school/becoming-involved).

## Dormant records: fix before activation

Four of the ten recommendations concern **already omitted** entries. They are not current automatic-use
regressions. This evidence-only batch proposes no status or flag changes.

- `thatll-do-pig`: praise/satisfactory completion in *Babe* is a better description than generic agreement.
  The [AFI catalogue](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/55728-BABE?cxt=filmography) identifies Australian/US production;
  a perceived farm setting does not independently decide Australian repertoire membership.
- `cruisin-for-a-bruisin`: courting trouble differs from being angry. Preserve the phrase and remap if
  later enabled; see the [licensed Oxford reference](https://www.encyclopedia.com/humanities/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/cruising-bruising).
- `that-dog-wont-hunt`: rejecting an unworkable plan or unconvincing argument differs from every bare no.
  [Wilton's historical research](https://www.wordorigins.org/big-list-entries/that-dog-wont-hunt) documents the
  idiom and US evidence; that is not proof Australians never use it.
- `shrimp-on-the-barbie`: the stored version is a familiar paraphrase/misquotation, while the
  [NFSA archive identifies the actual tourism-ad line](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/item/shrimp-barbie-paul-hogan).
  Neither is a literal hello synonym. Keep provenance, geographical audience and everyday idiomatic use distinct.

## Supported ranges and unresolved cases

- `a-show-pony`: retain the broad attention-seeker/show-off sense in
  [Collins](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/show-pony?q=show+pony); do not require lack of ability merely
  because [Cambridge](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/show-pony) emphasises that narrower reading.
- `a-middy`: keep the attested half-pint comparison in [Collins](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/middy).
  [Macquarie's regional size discussion](https://www.macquariedictionary.com.au/a-deep-dive-into-aussie-beer-sizes/)
  adds useful context; it does not make the comparison categorically wrong. An unspecified small glass
  needs more context when exact quantity matters.
- `a-mufti-day`: [primary school usage](https://explain.scas.nsw.edu.au/support/uniform-guidelines) is clear.
  One school policy cannot prove office/general dress-down uses false; gather evidence before imposing a
  school-only limit or claiming universal interchangeability.
- `the-lucky-buggers-still-breathin`: component evidence supports informal person-reference, not the
  complete phrase's conventionality. Whether an assessment of good fortune can be added to a neutral
  reference to the living belongs to the embellishment/context policy. Preserve it as a composition;
  do not silently certify it as a traditional idiom or declare it fabricated.
- `a-grub`: [primary parliamentary speech](https://www.aph.gov.au/binaries/hansard/reps/dailys/dr170305.pdf)
  establishes a disparaging person-directed use. A stronger edited source is still desirable to distinguish
  slovenliness from moral contempt across all scoundrel/troublemaker triggers.
- `deadset-adj`: primary editorial usage is attested, but the tempting
  [Macquarie article](https://www.macquariedictionary.com.au/a-deadset-australianism/) actually explains
  **barrack**, not deadset. Do not import the deadset etymology attributed to it by a secondary page.
  Retrieve a suitable edited intensifier source before calling the whole UTTER mapping verified.

Other useful preservation points: joey covers marsupial young beyond kangaroos; king hit includes a
supported surprise-attack sense without every historical use having that restriction; come a gutser has
both fall and failure uses; fossick is not confined to minerals; a shoey is usually alcoholic but the
[source](https://www.macquariedictionary.com.au/chuck-a-shoey-in-moderation/) does not make alcohol compulsory.

## Integration, verification and limits

[sources-proposed.yaml](sources-proposed.yaml) contains **57 proposed per-entry sidecar records**, excluding
the three provisional cases. These URLs are candidates for parent review, not automatically applied
runtime provenance. The ledger distinguishes exact-construction evidence, components, meaning,
Australian use and history. Some selected senses have a single lower-strength educational or community
source; the supported category does not promise two independent professional dictionaries for each item.

The KoalaNet glossary reprinted by GP Supervision Australia is labelled as a **reprinted community
glossary**, not medical-institution lexicographic research. Its mirrors count as one source family.
Victoria University's teaching list also overlaps widely circulated glossary wording; no independent
historical corroboration is inferred merely from its different hostname. Collins pages on Dictionary.com
remain Collins sources. Film dialogue, a venue's own advertised event terminology, and a school policy are
primary evidence of their particular usage, not exhaustive semantic authorities.

Sources were manually consulted through browser pages and indexed text. Direct-fetch failures are
recorded where relevant; original historical books quoted by lexical researchers were not all retrieved
independently. Notes are original paraphrases and mapping analysis; source definitions, examples and
quotation collections are not redistributed. Omission of a meaning from one reference, or failure to
find an exact phrase in a bounded search, is not evidence it is false or fabricated.

Validation checked all 60 IDs against the original Git inventory, exclusion of the preceding 160 IDs,
unique counts, decision totals, source-reference integrity, sidecar exclusions and file digests. Parent is
concurrently editing semantic-selection files, so the digests identify the research snapshot rather than
assert a pristine HEAD. The recorded selected entries were not edited by this batch. Runtime regressions,
provenance declarations, generated fixtures, design review and commit checks belong to the parent task.

Following the software-design principles, encode lexical distinctions in data and use the shared semantic
context machinery for role, referent and setting preservation. Avoid special engine branches for these
individual slang strings. Shape only the genuine policy tradeoff: how much deliberate added judgement or
loose metaphor strong embellishment may permit while keeping factual roles and references intact.
