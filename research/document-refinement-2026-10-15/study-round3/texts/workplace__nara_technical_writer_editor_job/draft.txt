As a TECHNICAL WRITER-EDITOR, you'll:

Work properly with the contents of documents and Federal Register publications so they come out accurately, on time and with legal effect.

Manage the work, whether you're on your own, working alongside teammates or leading others, to get all documents published accurately and on time and meet all program goals.

Get to grips with customer needs across every part of the Federal Register publication process and address them properly, meeting the Office of the Federal Register's program responsibilities.

Get your head round the technical resources available for and connected with Federal Register publication processes, and use them properly. That includes information technology, legacy systems and other technical resources.

Editing legal or regulatory documents written by other people to bring them into line with style and formatting requirements before they're developed for publication; AND

Spotting and analysing production problems and working out solutions; AND

Getting information across effectively and diplomatically to internal and/or external audiences to advance program goals.
