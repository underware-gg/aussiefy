If you’re a program manager or a federal web developer, you’ve probably been handed a job that sounds simple enough: put together a website for an initiative at your agency. The biggest headache is often getting security and privacy compliance sorted so you can launch the site and keep it ticking over, rather than writing the content or designing it. You might have to bring in a contractor for that work, or pile more work onto your agency’s web team.

It shouldn’t be that way.

## Why Pages?

Pages does the backend and compliance legwork for our fellow federal partners, with the expertise to get a website up and running and keep it going. That frees you up to get stuck into what you know: your content.

- **FedRAMP Certified Platform** Pages runs on cloud.gov, a platform with security and compliance verified by the FedRAMP Joint Authorization Board. You can count on your site being fast, reliable, and ready for unexpected surges in traffic.

- **Preview it and get the go-ahead** Pages builds draft updates at a special preview URL. Your team can see that changes will come up right when they go onto the main site, and getting stakeholders to sign off on content updates is easy.

## Why static websites?

Pages generates static websites rather than using a more complex content management system (CMS), such as Drupal or WordPress. Static websites have a fair bit going for them:

- **Less to wrangle, fewer vulnerabilities:** Doing without a traditional CMS saves you the dramas of maintaining server applications (no PHP or Apache / IIS), along with database scaling and redundancy. Production-level static sites generally need one simple static file server or service, rather than dozens. That leaves you with a whole website that’s easier to look after and has fewer vulnerabilities.

- **Change the content without writing code:** Static websites often keep their content in simple text files that anyone on the team can edit. With the GitHub editor, any team member can make basic edits to those files, whatever their coding experience.

- **Easy to host and look after:** Hosting static website files on Pages is flamin' easy. Through Pages, it’s easy to make use of scaling services such as Amazon S3, and you only pay for what you use.

- **Make it your own:** Designers and developers can knock static websites into whatever shape they want, without first stripping out features and defaults supplied by a CMS. You can build all sorts of sites this way, from organisation homepages or intranets to pre-generated web application program interfaces that display data from a separate server.
