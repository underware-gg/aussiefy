Julio Teherán, a two-time Major League Baseball All-Star, announced on Monday he was calling time on his baseball career at 35. Two days earlier, a shoulder injury had ruled him out of a scheduled start for Team Colombia in the 2026 World Baseball Classic (WBC).

Before announcing his retirement, Teherán had helped Team Colombia get past Team Panama 3-1 in the WBC.

Teherán spent the bulk of his thirteen-year MLB career with the Atlanta Braves, with stints at the Los Angeles Angels, Detroit Tigers, Milwaukee Brewers, and New York Mets as well. He made his MLB debut for the Braves in 2011, aged 20, and stayed on for his first nine seasons, picking up All-Star selections in 2014 and 2016.

All up, his MLB career yielded an 81-82 record, a 3.85 ERA, 1.223 WHIP, and 1,260 strikeouts in 1,470.2 innings. He spent the 2022 and 2025 seasons playing in the Mexican Baseball League.
