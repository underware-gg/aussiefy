NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman will be at the controls of one of three F-5 fighter jets when the agency’s Inspiration Tour flyover gets the New York Jets vs. Cleveland Browns game going in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on Sunday, Oct. 11.

Before the game, fans can have a yarn with NASA team members at the agency’s Experience Zone in the Jets Tailgate Zone outside the stadium, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. EDT. Fans can get the good oil on NASA’s return to the Moon under the agency’s Artemis dome, get stuck into interactive games, and snap photos with a big, inflatable SLS (Space Launch System) rocket.

NASA’s Inspiration Tour is doing the rounds across the nation, bringing academic, industry, and public sector stakeholders together. It links the agency up with the people, technologies, and organisations behind American leadership in space.

The tour finishes up with MAX POWER, a public exposition of American air and space innovation, on Saturday, Nov. 7, and Sunday, Nov. 8, on and near the agency’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. It’s being held in honour of America’s historic 250th anniversary, runs over several days, and is a family-friendly affair. On show will be the next-generation aircraft, spacecraft, autonomous vehicles, and technologies that’ll help shape transport in the air and in space down the track.
