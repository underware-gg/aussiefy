## Examples of acceptable and unacceptable gifts

So what does “of value” mean? Keep in mind that gifts for government officials are out (ok, maybe stickers). Always ask what their policies allow and prohibit. If you’re not sure, run it past Legal to make sure. Many U.S. congresspeople, for instance, won’t let you shout them dinner.

For other customers or prospective customers, something you’d hardly give a thought to can be worth a lot to someone else. Getting your mate’s daughter hired as an intern might seem no big deal to you, but it could mean a great deal to them. If she isn’t qualified and you pull strings to get her in, though, that’s not on. And if the intention is to improperly influence, anything, however small, might count as "of value."

🙅 _Here are some other things that aren't ok:_

* First class airfare to GitHub offices for Executive Briefings ✈️+🍾
* Winery tours 🍷
* Recommending a friend’s kid for admission to a prestigious private school 🎓
* Tickets to Disneyland 👸, the World Cup 🥅, the Olympics 🏅
* A futbol ⚽️ signed by a famous player
* A contribution to a charity

🙆 _Here is some stuff that is actually ok for customers or prospective customers:_

* Swag
* Economy class airfare to GitHub offices for Executive Briefings ✈️
* Bleacher-type tickets to a sporting event ⚾️
* A reasonably priced dinner in town 🍽
* A small box of choccies as an end of year thank you 🍫

The same goes for _taking_ bribes. If a vendor or prospective vendor sends you something “of value,” don’t accept it—or at least run it by Legal before you do.

## Process

If you’re planning to give or accept an informal gift (something other than our promotional items, such as t-shirts and sticker swag), please make sure you follow the steps below.

Bear in mind that what counts as an acceptable amount can vary. A $50 dinner might be a fair bit of money in some countries and not so much in others. If you’re in doubt, ask!

1. Generally speaking, for any expense over $150, you’ll need Legal’s approval in writing, each time. For any gift you receive worth more than $150, you’ll need to report it to Legal in writing, each time. (Legal is only weighing in on the bribery question. You might still need to check with Finance for other reasons.)

1. When you submit your expense report for reimbursement, please:
     * Put the recipient and the purpose of the gift in the notes; and
     * Include a copy of the email approval

## Consequences

What happens if you don’t follow the policy and GitHub ends up paying for a prospective customer and his family to fly first class to Japan and go to the Tokyo Olympics? Or if you drop off a suitcase of Euros for a government official in a pre-arranged train station locker?

FINES and JAIL. And GitHub mightn’t be the only one. You could find yourself spending a few years in jail too. It won’t matter whether the customer ever put an order in to GitHub after the Japan trip, or whether the government official even picked up the suitcase of cash. Basically, it doesn’t matter whether you actually _made_ a bribe. Yoda notwithstanding, it’s the trying that counts.
