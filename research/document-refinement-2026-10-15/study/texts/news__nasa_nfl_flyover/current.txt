NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman will take the controls of one of three F-5 fighter jets for the agency’s Inspiration Tour flyover, kicking off the New York Jets vs. Cleveland Browns game in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on Sunday, Oct. 11.

Before the game, NASA team members will chat with fans at the agency’s Experience Zone in the Jets Tailgate Zone outside the stadium, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. EDT. Fans can get the lowdown on NASA’s return to the Moon under the agency’s Artemis dome, have a go at interactive games, and grab photos with a large, inflatable SLS (Space Launch System) rocket.

The Inspiration Tour stops around the nation, bringing academic, industry, and public sector stakeholders together to connect NASA with the people, technologies, and organisations driving American leadership in space.

The tour will wrap up with MAX POWER, a public exposition of American air and space innovation, on Saturday, Nov. 7, and Sunday, Nov. 8, on and near the agency’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Held in honour of America’s historic 250th anniversary, the event runs over several days and caters for families. It will show off the next-generation aircraft, spacecraft, autonomous vehicles, and technologies that will help shape the future of transport in air and space.
