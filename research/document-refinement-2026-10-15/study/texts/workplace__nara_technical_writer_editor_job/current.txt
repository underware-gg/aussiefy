These are the duties for this position at the GS-12. If you're selected at a lower grade level, you'll have the chance to learn all these duties, with training to help you grow in the position.

As a TECHNICAL WRITER-EDITOR, you will:

Handle the contents of documents and Federal Register publications properly so they're published accurately, on time and with legal effect.

Manage the work, whether you're working on your own, in a team or leading others, to publish all documents accurately and on time and achieve all program goals.

Understand and properly address customer needs across every part of the Federal Register publication process, fulfilling the Office of the Federal Register's program responsibilities.

Understand and properly use the technical resources available for and connected with Federal Register publication processes, including information technology, legacy systems and other technical resources.

Qualifications

You must meet these requirements by this announcement's closing date.

Specialized Experience: For the GS-09, you must have one year of specialised experience at a level of difficulty and responsibility equivalent to the GS-07 grade level in the Federal service. Specialized experience for this position includes:

Editing legal or regulatory documents written by others to make sure they meet style and formatting requirements before being developed for publication; AND

Recognising and analysing production problems and coming up with solutions; AND

Communicating information effectively and diplomatically to internal and/or external audiences to advance program goals.

OR

EDUCATION: A master's or equivalent graduate degree or 2 full years of progressively higher level graduate education leading to such a degree or LL.B. or J.D., if related.

OR

COMBINATION OF EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: Note: Only graduate study beyond the first year (18 semester hours) qualifies for the GS-9.

NOTE: Undergraduate and Graduate Education: Must have included a total of 15 semester hours in an appropriate scientific, technical, or social science field(s), and at least one course above the introductory level in the field(s) covered by the position. For technical manuals and specifications writers or editors, the equivalent of 15 semester hours may have come through vocational or educational training above high school level at a public, private, or Armed Forces school.
