I was knackered by the time I got to the ferry stop. I'd spent the mornin' helpin' me neighbour shift a heavy cupboard, and me back was givin' me grief. The next boat wasn't due for forty minutes. The woman beside me offered me half her sanger. I thanked her, but told her I'd already had a feed. Then me brother rang to say he could pick me up in ten minutes. That was a weight off me mind, though I still had to lug his broken bike home.
