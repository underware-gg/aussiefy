Requests for a refund must be submitted within 30 days of purchase. The applicant must provide the order number and a description of the fault. Approval does not depend on whether the original packaging is available. A replacement may be offered, but the applicant is not required to accept it. Approved refunds will be paid to the original payment method within five working days. If that method is no longer available, the accounts team will contact the applicant to arrange an alternative. This policy applies to demonstration orders placed through the test store.
