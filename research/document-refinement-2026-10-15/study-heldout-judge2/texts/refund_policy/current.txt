Requests for a refund must be sent in within 30 days of purchase. The applicant must give the order number and a description of the fault. Approval doesn't hinge on whether the original packaging is available. A replacement may be offered, but the applicant doesn't have to take it. Approved refunds will be paid to the original payment method within five working days. If that method is no longer available, the accounts team will get in touch with the applicant to sort out an alternative. This policy applies to demonstration orders placed through the test store.
