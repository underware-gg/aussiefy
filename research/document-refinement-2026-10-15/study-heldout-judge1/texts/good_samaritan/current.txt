A lawyer got to his feet and put him to the test, sayin', “Teacher, what do I have to do to inherit eternal life?”

He asked him, “What's written in the law? How do ya read it?”

He answered, “You're to love the Lord yer God with all yer heart, with all yer soul, with all yer strength and with all yer mind; and yer neighbour as yerself.”

He told him, “Ya got that right. Do that and you'll live.”

But the lawyer wanted to justify himself, so he asked Jesus, “Who's me neighbour?”

Jesus answered, “There was a cove headin' down from Jerusalem to Jericho who fell among robbers. They stripped him, bashed him and cleared off, leavin' him half dead. As it happened, a priest was headin' down that way. He saw the bloke and went past on the other side. A Levite did the same: he got to the spot, saw him and went past on the other side. But a Samaritan came to where he was as he was travellin'. When he saw him, his heart went out to him. He went over and bandaged his wounds, pourin' oil and wine on 'em. He put him on his own animal, took him to an inn and looked after him. Next day, as he was leavin', he took out two denarii, handed 'em to the host and told him, ‘Look after him. Whatever ya spend over and above that, I'll pay ya back when I come back.’ Now which of those three do ya reckon seemed like a neighbour to the bloke who fell among the robbers?”

He said, “The one who showed him mercy.”

Then Jesus told him, “Go and do the same.”
