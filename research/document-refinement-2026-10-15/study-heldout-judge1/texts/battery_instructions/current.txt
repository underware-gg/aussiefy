Before you give the demonstration lantern a clean, disconnect it from the charger and switch it off. Give it five minutes before you open the battery cover. Keep a firm hold on the cover while you loosen the two screws. Don't touch the metal contacts with a wet cloth. Give the outside a wipe with a dry cloth, put the cover back on, and tighten both screws by hand. If the case is cracked or the battery's leaking, stop using the lantern and get in touch with the supplier. These instructions are only for model L12.
