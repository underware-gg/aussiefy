Harbourtown Council announced on Tuesday that the library would be back in business on 18 November. Fixin' the roof had taken three weeks longer than expected after the workers found damaged timber. The kids' room will stay shut till 2 December, but ya can drop borrowed books back at the front desk. Council spokesperson Maya Chen said, “No late fees will be charged during the closure.” The stopgap collection point at the swimming pool will shut up shop when the main buildin' opens.
