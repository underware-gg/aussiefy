If you’re a program manager or federal web developer, you’ve probably been handed what sounds like a simple job: build a website for an initiative at your agency. Often, the hard bit isn’t putting the content together or designing the site. It’s sorting out security and privacy compliance so you can launch the thing and keep it running. You might have to bring in a contractor for that work, or put more pressure on your agency’s web team.

It shouldn’t be that way.

## Why Pages?

Pages helps our fellow federal partners by expertly handling the backend and compliance work needed to launch and manage a website. That leaves you free to put your expertise into your content.

- **FedRAMP Certified Platform** Pages runs on cloud.gov, a platform whose security and compliance have been verified by the FedRAMP Joint Authorization Board. That means you can trust your site to be fast, reliable, and ready for unexpected surges in traffic.

- **Easy previews and content approvals** Pages builds draft updates to your site at a special preview URL. Your team can check that changes will look right when they go live on the main site, and getting stakeholders to sign off on content updates is straightforward.

## Why static websites?

Pages generates static websites rather than using a more complex content management system (CMS), such as Drupal or WordPress. Static websites have a few things going for them:

- **Less complexity and vulnerability:** Without a traditional CMS, you avoid the hassles of maintaining server applications (no PHP or Apache / IIS), as well as database scaling and redundancy. Production-level static sites generally need one simple static file server or service rather than dozens. That makes the whole website easier to look after, with fewer vulnerabilities.

- **Update content without writing code:** Static websites often keep content in simple text files that anyone on the team can edit. With the GitHub editor, any team member can make basic edits to those files, whatever their coding experience.

- **Easy to host and maintain:** Hosting static website files on Pages is flamin' easy. Through Pages, you can readily use scaling services such as Amazon S3, paying only for what you use.

- **Fully customizable:** Designers and developers can shape static websites however they want, without stripping away features and defaults supplied by a CMS. Static sites can cover all sorts of sites, from organisation homepages or intranets to pre-generated web application program interfaces that display data from a separate server.
