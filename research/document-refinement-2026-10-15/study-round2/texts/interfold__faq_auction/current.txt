# The FOLD Auction

Here’s the rundown on FOLD Auction 2, the second public FOLD auction. This FAQ is for people who
took part or need to claim FOLD afterwards. The questions fall into four groups: the auction itself,
participation, claiming and transferability, and safety.

## About the auction

### What is FOLD Auction 2?

FOLD Auction 2 was the second public FOLD auction, run using the Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction
mechanism.

It came after the initial FOLD Auction, held July 8–10, 2026.

Auction 2 was deliberately kept to **2% of total FOLD supply** and held around the move to
general FOLD transferability. The aim was to spread distribution more widely and help get deeper,
longer-term on-chain liquidity off the ground.

### Why was there a second FOLD Auction?

Auction 2 was a limited distribution step before general FOLD transferability on Aug 19.

It was kept small on purpose, at **2% of total FOLD supply**. Its starting floor matched the
clearing price of the first FOLD Auction.

The auction provided an open, transparent way to distribute more FOLD before it became generally
transferable. It also supported long-term on-chain liquidity.

**50% of proceeds were committed to long-term on-chain liquidity support.**

Auction 2 closed shortly before general FOLD transferability was enabled.

### What happened in the first FOLD Auction?

The initial FOLD Auction ran from **July 8–10, 2026**. It used the Uniswap Continuous Clearing
Auction mechanism.

The first auction is complete. Its clearing price was **0.02154816 USDC / FOLD**, which is also the
floor for FOLD Auction 2.

If you took part in the first auction, you can still get claiming and post-auction information
through the official [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).

### What is a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction?

A Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) is a price-discovery mechanism for distributing tokens, built
on Uniswap infrastructure.

- The auction runs across multiple discrete periods.
- Participants submit bids through the official Uniswap CCA interface.
- Successful bids within a period clear at the same clearing price.
- The minimum price for each subsequent period is the greater of the original floor price or the
  clearing price of the previous period.
- The minimum price may rise in later periods. It does not fall below the applicable prior clearing
  price or the original floor.
- Any tokens left undistributed earlier are still available in the final period.

The CCA distributes tokens as it goes, with a uniform price within each period. The design aims to
cut gas competition, reduce sniping, and let clearing prices develop through auction participation.

[Read more about Uniswap CCAs](https://cca.uniswap.org).

### Did the auction determine the price of FOLD after transferability?

Nah. The auction set allocations and a clearing price within the auction itself. It didn’t set
FOLD’s market price when general transferability began.

There’s no predicting future market prices.

## Participation

<Callout type='warning'>
  Auction 2 closed on Aug 19, 2026 at 13:06 UTC. These steps explain how it worked.
  You can’t put in a new bid.
</Callout>

### How did participation work?

1. Participants went to the official [FOLD Auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).
2. They followed the verified link to the Uniswap CCA interface.
3. They completed the required verification process through the official flow.
4. They submitted bids during the auction window.
5. After the auction closed, they could claim any allocated FOLD and unspent USDC through the
   verified auction interface.

Participants needed USDC on Ethereum Mainnet. Before taking part, they had to go through the
auction terms and eligibility requirements.

### Who could participate?

Eligible participants could take part, subject to jurisdiction, identity verification, AML and
sanctions screening, and other applicable restrictions.

The official verification process determined eligibility. The auction terms governed eligibility.

Getting through verification didn’t guarantee eligibility or an allocation.

### Was KYC required?

Yep. Before taking part, participants had to complete identity verification through the official
verification process.

The verification process determined eligibility and carried out applicable AML and sanctions screening.

Participation was subject to the auction terms and applicable restrictions.

## Claiming and transferability

### When can participants claim FOLD?

Claiming was due to open as soon as the auction closed.

Participants claim allocated FOLD and any unspent USDC through the verified auction interface.

Claiming and general FOLD transferability are two separate actions.

### When did general FOLD transferability begin?

FOLD Auction 2 closed Aug 19 at **13:06 UTC (9:06 AM ET)**.

General FOLD transferability could be enabled shortly afterward, at or after **14:00 UTC (10 AM
ET)**.

At or after Unix timestamp **`1787148000`**, anyone could call the `tge()` function on the FOLD
contract to enable general transferability. The TGE transaction was executed on Aug 19, 2026 at
14:00:23 UTC. `tgeTimestamp()` returns `1787148023`. FOLD is now generally transferable.

FOLD Auction 2, claiming, general FOLD transferability, and Network Alpha are separate milestones
in the broader Interfold launch sequence. For the details, have a squiz at
[FOLD Transferability](/faq/transferability).

## Safety

### How do I know I’m using the official auction?

Stick to auction, claiming, verification, and support links published through official Interfold
channels.

Official Interfold channels are:

- [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction)
- [Interfold website](https://www.theinterfold.com/)
- [Interfold X account](https://x.com/theinterfold)
- [Interfold documentation](https://docs.theinterfold.com/)

Don’t trust:

- Auction, claiming, or support links that turn up in unsolicited DMs.
- Copied token or contract addresses from unofficial sources.
- Impersonator accounts.
- Private wallet-connection links.
- Requests for your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
- Requests to pay for support.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Interfold team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet
  backup, or access to your wallet.
</Callout>
