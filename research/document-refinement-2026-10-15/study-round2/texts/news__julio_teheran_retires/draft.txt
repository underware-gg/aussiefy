Julio Teherán, a two-time Major League Baseball All-Star, announced on Monday that he was retiring from baseball at 35. The decision came two days after a shoulder injury forced him out of a scheduled start for Team Colombia in the 2026 World Baseball Classic (WBC).

Before announcing his retirement, Teherán had helped Team Colombia beat Team Panama 3-1 in the WBC.

Teherán's thirteen-year MLB career was mostly with the Atlanta Braves, though he also played for the Los Angeles Angels, Detroit Tigers, Milwaukee Brewers, and New York Mets. He made his MLB debut with the Braves in 2011, aged 20, and stayed with the team for his first nine seasons. He picked up All-Star selections in 2014 and 2016.

Across his MLB career, Teherán finished with an 81-82 record, a 3.85 ERA, 1.223 WHIP, and 1,260 strikeouts in 1,470.2 innings. He spent the 2022 and 2025 seasons playing in the Mexican Baseball League.
