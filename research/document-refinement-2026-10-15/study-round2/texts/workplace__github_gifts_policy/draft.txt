## Examples of acceptable and unacceptable gifts

So what does “of value” mean? Remember, no gifts are acceptable for government officials (ok, maybe stickers). Always ask what their policies allow and prohibit. If you’re unsure, check with Legal to make sure. Many U.S. congresspeople, for instance, won’t let you take them out for dinner and pick up the bill.

With other customers or prospective customers, something that seems trivial to you can be worth a great deal to someone else. Getting your mate’s daughter hired as an intern might not seem like a big deal to you, for example, but it could mean a lot to them. If she isn’t qualified and you pull strings to get her in, though, that isn’t ok. And if the intention is to improperly influence, anything, however small, might count as "of value."

🙅 _Here are some other things that aren't ok:_

* First class airfare to GitHub offices for Executive Briefings ✈️+🍾
* Winery tours 🍷
* Recommending a friend’s kid for admission to a prestigious private school 🎓
* Tickets to Disneyland 👸, the World Cup 🥅, the Olympics 🏅
* A futbol ⚽️ signed by a famous player
* A contribution to a charity

🙆 _Here is some stuff that is actually ok for customers or prospective customers:_

* Swag
* Economy class airfare to GitHub offices for Executive Briefings ✈️
* Bleacher-type tickets to a sporting event ⚾️
* A reasonably priced dinner in town 🍽
* A small box of chocolates as an end of year thank you 🍫

All of that applies to _taking_ bribes too. If a vendor or prospective vendor sends you something “of value,” don’t accept it—or at least check with Legal before you do.

## Process

If you plan to give or accept an informal gift (something other than our promotional items, such as t-shirts and sticker swag), please make sure you follow the process below.

Remember, an acceptable amount can vary. Spending $50 on dinner might be a lot of money in some countries and less so in others. If you’re unsure, ask!

1. Generally speaking, any expense over $150 needs written approval from Legal, each time. Any gift you receive worth more than $150 must also be reported to Legal in writing, each time. (Legal is only weighing in on the bribery question. You might still need to check with Finance for other reasons.)

1. When you submit your expense report for reimbursement, please:
     * Give the recipient and purpose of the gift in the notes; and
     * Include a copy of the email approval

## Consequences

What happens if you don’t follow the policy and GitHub ends up paying for a prospective customer and his family to fly first class to Japan and attend the Tokyo Olympics? Or if you leave a suitcase of Euros for a government official in a pre-arranged train station locker?

FINES and JAIL. And that doesn’t necessarily stop at GitHub. You could spend a few years in jail yourself. It won’t matter whether the customer ever placed an order with GitHub after the Japan trip, or whether the government official even collected the suitcase of cash. Basically, it doesn’t matter whether you actually _made_ a bribe. Yoda notwithstanding, it’s the trying that counts.
