So one day her mum's knocked up a batch of custards, and she goes to her:

"Go on, love, nick over and see how your nan's goin', 'cause I heard she's been crook as buggery. Take her a custard and this little pot of butter."

Little Red pisses off straight away to her nan's, who lived in the next town over.

So she's goin' through the bush and she runs into this mangy old bastard, the Wolf, who reckons he'd fuckin' love to scoff her right there, but he hasn't got the balls, 'cause there's a bunch of blokes cuttin' firewood just over the way. "I'm goin' to see me nan and take her a custard and a little pot of butter from me mum."

"She live far, does she?" says the Wolf.

"Oh, yeah, fair way," says Little Red. "Past that mill over there, first house in the town."

"Well, bugger me," says the Wolf, "I might go see her an' all. I'll go this way, you go that way, and we'll see who gets there first."

So the Wolf takes off like a rat up a drainpipe, takin' the shortcut, and the little one goes the long way round, pissin' about pickin' nuts, chasin' butterflies and makin' little bunches out of whatever flowers she come across. Didn't take the Wolf long to get to the old girl's joint. He knocks on the door, tap, tap.

"Who's that?"

"It's your granddaughter, Little Red Riding Hood," says the Wolf, puttin' on her voice, the sneaky cunt, "and I've brought you a custard and a little pot of butter from Mum."

The good old nan, who's in bed 'cause she's a bit crook, sings out:

"Pull the bobbin and the latch'll go up."

So the Wolf pulls the bobbin, the door swings open, and next thing he's onto the poor old girl and scoffs her in about two seconds flat, 'cause the bastard hadn't had a feed in over three fuckin' days. Then he shuts the door and hops into Nan's bed, waitin' for Little Red, who rocks up a bit later and knocks on the door, tap, tap.

"Who's that?"

Little Red hears this big gravelly bloody voice and she's shittin' herself at first, but she reckons Nan's just got a cold and she's a bit hoarse, so she goes:

"It's your granddaughter, Little Red Riding Hood, and I've brought you a custard and a little pot of butter from Mum."

The Wolf sings out, puttin' on the softest voice the bastard can manage:

"Pull the bobbin and the latch'll go up."

Little Red pulls the bobbin and the door opens.

The Wolf sees her come in and he's hidin' under the bloody blankets, and he goes:

"Chuck the custard and the little pot of butter on the stool, love, and come hop into bed with me."

"Shit, Nan, what big arms you've got!"

"All the better to give ya a cuddle, love."

"Nan, what bloody big ears you've got!"

"All the better to hear ya with, darl."

"Nan, what bloody big eyes you've got!"

"All the better to see ya with, darl."

"Fuckin' hell, Nan, what big teeth you've got!"

"All the better to fuckin' eat ya with."
