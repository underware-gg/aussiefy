# Design review: hunger degree, 20 October 2026

Status: Focused review converged; integrated verification passed.

## Findings

The independent full-concern review found no remaining defects in the scoped hunger certificates,
bounded degree checker, lint wiring or evidence ledger. All six HUNGRY entries and their source triggers
are calibrated separately from style intensity. Existing modifier ownership is retained: unsupported
realised constructions stay plain rather than silently dropping `very` or asserting `very peckish`.

`semantic_checks.py` owns bounded original-aware composition, mapping and degree checks. Existing
composition/mapping behaviour is extracted without changing its pairing rules. Degree verification uses
canonical detection and `realised_degree`, requires one exact local source/target pairing, and abstains
for unknown/ambiguous grades, negation and other simultaneous edits. The lint severity coverage test
includes the new module and both literal quote styles; the architecture scan classifies its toolkit owner.

## What's done well

The domain mechanism remains generic: the tests add an independent graded family through an overlay,
without Python concept-name branches. Certifying conventional appetite does not claim clinical precision.
The source ledger distinguishes edited lexical evidence, attested comic constructions and composed meal
phrases. No word is removed and no style score or gate is repurposed as a severity certificate.

The prototype's 40 behavioural tests pass, covering three backends, plans/editor choices, unknown and
ambiguous targets, source repetition and bounded pairing. The reviewer independently repeated those
checks and probed figurative hunger exclusions. Root integration/full-suite results follow below.

The adopted root passed 182 focused checks; the independent reviewer repeated 56 hunger, verification
and layering checks. The actual-root review found no remaining issues.

The final integrated checkpoint passed 3,253 tests with only the two permitted local-corpus skips.
Ruff, publication and provenance checks passed. The review index links all three October 20 reviews;
plain-source relative-link corrections were validated by publication. No runtime or dictionary change
was made after the final full run.

## Related follow-ups

Pairing across contractions and additional edits could broaden verification coverage. Other graded
families, relational `enough` thresholds, and reviewed qualifier consumption into a different construction
remain independent work; this slice does not claim them.
