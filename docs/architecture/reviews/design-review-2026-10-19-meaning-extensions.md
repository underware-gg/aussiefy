## Findings

The implementation removes redundant direct hoon replacements that reintroduced both lout and driver
concepts, bypassing their split. Non-monetary wasted-effort coverage uses existing Dinkum permissions;
Deadset's intensifying-swear exclusion is not weakened to satisfy the test. Temporal classification is
scoped by compiled pattern structure rather than a global ban on time nouns.

Review resolved temporal-of complements and frequency/rate expressions, preserved quantified noun objects,
excluded non-person tramp compounds and required complete conditional appetite requests. Ambiguous bare
start was removed from the new activity triggers after generated-doc readback; widening noun-context
rejection would instead have suppressed valid mis-tagged finite verbs and questions.

Independent design review reports no remaining findings, with 135 focused tests observed passing.
Parent verification also passed 250 meaning/hunger/schema/context checks and 231 existing engine/matching/
fidelity checks. The full run found a stale assertion that hunger writer suggestions were unavailable
at level 2. Independent review confirmed the two new plain suggestions belong at level 2; the regression
now requires exactly those two and retains the level-1 exclusion. The resulting imagery, appetite, meaning
and argument checks passed together (188 tests). Complete pre-commit verification passed: **2,522 tests passed, two expected local-corpus skips**
in **775.08 seconds**. Ruff, generated-document freshness, provenance/licensing and whitespace checks
also passed.

Research-only batches 3–6 have independent cross-reviews and cover 480 additional original IDs, bringing
coverage to 700 of 1,254. Batch 5's misattribution of the Deadset sparrow-fart exclusion to incoherence
was corrected to the actual intensifying-swear policy; Dinkum already admits that problematic temporal
substitution. These ledgers preserve source-access and exact-construction limits. Their later source
proposals and runtime recommendations are not claimed implemented by this slice.

## What's done well

- Lexical distinctions live in dictionary concepts, with expressions preserved and source evidence linked.
- Specific names do not silently assign a drinking game or brand to a generic activity or object.
- Conditional appetite conversions preserve their source construction; writer templates cover freer uses.
- Shared argument reading reuses existing tags and pattern structure, preserving anchored temporal nouns.
