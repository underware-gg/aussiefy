# DD-048: Additive community extensions and content identity

Status: Accepted for slice 5 loading; response and selection integration remains slice 6
Date: 2026-10-26
Extends: [DD-032](dd-032-dictionary-overlays.md), [DD-047](dd-047-source-links-and-match-identity.md)

## Context

Community exports need namespaced vocabulary, evidence on existing entries, promotion and bounded pick
weights. Maintainer overlays can change a core record; accepting those operations in a community export
would make additive evidence into a way to change meanings or eligibility. Anonymous testimony totals
cannot safely be summed across exports. Package version alone cannot identify different data loads.

## Decision

Use `aussiefy-dictionary-extension/v1` with namespace, concepts, entries, evidence and optional adjustments.
Qualified identifiers share one grammar across schema parsing and match keys. Existing legacy entry lists
and maintainer overlays keep their semantics. Extensions reuse vocabulary addition, then evidence composes
after all additions and before validation, assessment and indexing. Same core form/frame/concept collisions
require explicit `evidence_for`; different senses remain distinct. No similarity-based coalescing occurs.

Until ED16 settles sense selection and guards, extension concepts cannot declare `no_entries: true`.
The extension boundary names the file, concept and core-only reason. Entryless extension concepts remain
meanings waiting for a word under ED13. This does not alter the core guard schema or existing core guards.

Community citations preserve their underlying kind as `basis`; their attribution never changes the score's
evidence category. Credit records distinguish term and definition credit and carry uninterpreted text.
Legacy single-string credit remains accepted for actual current data/surfaces until the coordinated card
contract, not a speculative migration framework. Definition credit is also supported on new concepts.

An evidence snapshot replaces a target's testimony total, never sums it. A snapshot older than the loaded
one fails. Distinct extension snapshots for the same target fail and require an upstream consolidated
export; identical snapshots deduplicate. Newer curator confirmations supersede older ones; conflicting
same-date confirmations fail. These are agent-chosen deterministic composition rules: the package has no
accounts, cannot infer overlap and must not invent independent people or silently pick between conflicts.

Promotion is an explicit former-qualified-id field on a core entry, retaining citation attribution and
credit. Duplicate promotions, extension-side promotions, missing credit and stale exports fail. The loaded
model provides the validated former-to-core map; it does not edit files or resolve accounts.

An adjustment export requires explicit normal/popularity bands. Both contain neutral weight 1 and the
popularity band contains the normal band. One factor is the full-strength signal, validated within the
popularity band. The product of factors is clipped to the selected band; overlapping exports use the
intersection of their matching bands. Log-space multiplication avoids overflow and preserves counteracting
signals before clipping. Only exact match/term/dial contexts participate. The pure query changes no score,
label, eligibility or source meaning. Wiring it into selection, preferences and public tuning options waits
for slice 6. These mathematical/composition choices are agent defaults, not new curatorial rulings.

The loader computes `aussiefy-dictionary/v1` content identity from core data names/bytes, built-in mode and
ordered extras' bytes. Hash framing and SHA-256 separate names/content unambiguously. Paths outside core,
mtime, documentation and generated bytecode are excluded; model/POS data and adjustment bytes are included.
Dictionary objects hold the load's identity without model-layer I/O. Response fields arrive with slice 6.

## Consequences

The untuned core has no community adjustments. A host retunes by shipping an export, not editing core or
releasing the package. Validation occurs once at loading; translation uses the resulting indexes normally.
Identity is reproducible under relocation but intentionally byte-sensitive. Manual in-memory mutation is
not a new file-load snapshot. The manifest is not a source-fetching or account-management API.

Public contract version, lookup ranking, near meanings and open vocabulary rulings do not change here.
Source application, searched outcomes and empirical attestation calibration remain gate-4 obligations.

## Normal-band selection checkpoint, 10 November 2026

Selection now applies the loaded normal-band factor to eligible substitution weights using the detected
source match and the run's resolved three dials. This happens after semantic, permission and grammar
checks, and before variety pruning. The substitution roll, evidence and garnish are unchanged.

For an actually adjusted spot, combine weights and factors in log space, then apply one common
normalisation before the pick. This preserves odds/order without overflowing on large finite factors;
weights already reduced to zero by repeat penalties stay zero. Unmatched spots retain their original
weights and explanations. Plans, changes and document adapters share the same selection owner.

This checkpoint does not expose popularity or preferences, report tuning on every response, or change
the changes-v2 contract. Those remain with the coordinated slices 3+6 contract work.


## Popularity and personal selection runtime checkpoint, 11 November 2026

`tuning.py` now owns per-call selection tuning. It reuses the existing adjustment factors and canonical
match parser, with the popularity band when requested. Personal inputs are a latest-first list of
`{match, term, weight}` records, with no account or dial fields: the caller supplies only preferences for
the current dials. Weights are finite, representable numbers at least 1; booleans are not weights.
Malformed record shapes fail with UserError. Unknown or obsolete matches and unknown term IDs warn and
are ignored, including malformed historical key strings; bad weights still fail on such rows.

There is one effective preference per source match, not one per candidate. The first valid exact-key
record wins, even if its term is not eligible here. Only when there is no exact record does the first
valid same-root record apply, including one made at another degree. An ineligible preferred term cannot
fall back to an older preference, unlock itself or change the source reading. Neutral weight 1 can be an
explicit exact choice and prevents fallback too. These choices follow engine Verification and website S13.

Community and personal factors combine with candidate weights in log space before variety, without
clipping personal weights to a band. The existing repeat penalty and substitution roll stay independent.
Factors cannot restore a weight already exhausted to zero. Inputs are snapshotted once per plain engine
call, without a per-text scan of all preferences at every spot.

The private runtime records the number of distinct input preferences used by positive-weight candidates
that survive repeat exclusions and variety in the actual selection pass. A preference repeated across
spots counts once; an eligible neutral preference counts, and a failed substitution roll does not erase
that the candidate pool was tuned. Raised-cap and alternative-rendering probes never increment it.
This is the chosen reporting interpretation; public reporting and document-wide aggregation remain with
the coordinated surface pass.

`Translator.translate` takes the internal `_tuning` input and retains a private result summary for that
next pass. No public option, hosted hook, changes/card version, identity envelope or invented output is
released by this checkpoint. The normal untuned output and changes-v2 fixtures remain unchanged. The
remaining combined-slice work is not complete, and the agreed 3+6 then 7 order is unchanged.


## Document tuning and draft contract checkpoint, 11 November 2026

One document prepares and snapshots its tuning input before processing any prose. Its internal composition
context supplies that prepared value to each plain/changes unit. Per-unit usage is frozen in TuningReport;
the document unions input positions rather than summing counts, while each unit retains its local report.
Warnings include preparation failures to resolve preferences even when a document has no eligible prose.
Public v2 envelopes remain unchanged. No account state or dial-scoping policy enters this aggregation.

Rob accepted the preceding runtime checkpoint and requested a draft schema before changes/card v3 freezes.
Review-only schemas/examples live under docs/drafts/changes-v3, outside package data. They retain v2's
Unicode code-point regions, masks and change identifiers, with new provenance and source-match fields.
JSON Schema alone does not establish reference, span/text or canonical-key equality; separate checks cover
those properties. Consumer review precedes freeze; deployment coordination belongs to the host
and is recorded in the project plan, not implemented in the package. This checkpoint grants no contract
freeze, publication or deployment by itself.


## Site review and r2 checkpoint, 12 November 2026

The site accepted r1's bytes and shape, conditional on option identity and attribution invariants. R2
specifies one entry_id-or-construction_id per change's combined alternatives/locked options, selected
construction membership and persistent construction content identity for a fixed dictionary identity.
The identity algorithm is extracted unchanged from compose and reused by semantic validation. Riffs do
not create duplicate entry options. Persistent expression identity does not imply availability in an
untuned request; request-side replay/pinning, including a persistent riff selector, remains required before
sharing tuned construction/riff picks ships.

Part/riff occurrence spans are local to their owner rendering, independently of document replacement
regions. The part join is (role, ingredient_id). Rich cards share theme objects and minimum vulgarity;
construction glosses come from existing concept descriptions. Shared review-stage projections derive an
agent coiner only for agent-produced bulldust with no explicit coiner and leave stored evidence intact.
Every bulldust location requires a non-null maker. Warning strings remain unchanged, with codes assigned
at the diagnostic origin and exposed through a parallel warning_details array in the v3 draft. Both
changes and every lookup outcome carry card_version at activation. None activates public v3 piecemeal.
