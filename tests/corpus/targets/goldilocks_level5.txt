Struth, once upon a bloody time, there was this little sheila called Goldilocks, right, and she was out wanderin' through the bush, mindin' her own business, when she got herself properly knackered. Buggered, she was. And hungry? Mate, she could've eaten a horse and chased the bloody jockey.
Anyway, she's traipsin' along when she comes across this little house, smack-bang in the middle of nowhere. Gives the door a knock. Nothin'. Not a bloody peep. So she reckons, ah, bugger it, and lets herself in. As ya do.
Well, stone the flamin' crows, there's three bowls of porridge sittin' on the table. Beauty, she reckons, don't mind if I do.
Has a crack at the first bowl. "Bloody hell!" she says. "That's hotter than a shearer's armpit!"
Tries the second. "Aw, get stuffed. That's colder than a witch's tit!"
Then she gets stuck into the third bowl, and wouldn't ya bloody know it, it's just right. So she scoffs the lot. Doesn't leave so much as a bloody skerrick.
Now, after all that, she fancies a bit of a sit-down. Tries the first chair. Too bloody big. Second chair? Too bloody hard. Third chair? Ah, you little ripper, just the ticket. But no sooner does she plonk her backside down than the whole bloody thing gives way underneath her.
Crack! Arse over tit she goes.
"Ah, bugger me dead," she says, pickin' herself up. "What a bloody shambles."
By now she's absolutely rooted, so she wanders upstairs lookin' for somewhere to have a kip. First bed's too hard, second one's too soft, but the third? Mate, it's bloody perfect. So she curls up, pulls the blankets over herself, and she's out like a light.
Well, wouldn't ya know it, along come the three bears, back from their morning constitutional.
Papa Bear takes one look at the table and goes, "Oi! Who's been at me bloody porridge?"
Mama Bear has a squiz at hers. "Well, bugger me, someone's been at mine an' all!"
And Baby Bear, poor little bastard, takes one look at his bowl and starts carryin' on like a pork chop.
"Some mongrel's eaten the bloody lot!"
Then they wander into the loungeroom.
"Oi!" bellows Papa Bear. "Some bugger's been sittin' in me chair!"
"Mine too!" says Mama Bear, gettin' a bit shirty.
Baby Bear looks at his chair, or what's bloody left of it, and just about spits the dummy.
"Aw, you've gotta be bloody jokin'! Some drongo's smashed me chair to buggery!"
Now the bears are properly spewin', so they head upstairs to see what other bloody mischief's been goin' on.
Papa Bear looks at his bed. "Stone the crows! Some bastard's been sleepin' in me bed!"
Mama Bear looks at hers. "Well, I'll be buggered. Mine too!"
And then Baby Bear pipes up, "Oi, Dad! Some little sheila's been sleepin' in me bed, and the cheeky bugger's still bloody here!"
Well, Goldilocks wakes up to find three great hairy bears standin' over her, lookin' none too pleased.
"Aw, FUCK ME DEAD!" she screams.
And before you can say Bob's your uncle, she's out of that bed, down the stairs, through the front door, and shootin' through like a Bondi tram. Didn't even stop to put her bloody shoes on.
And as for the three bears? Well, Papa Bear reckons they oughta call the coppers, Mama Bear's still spewin' about the porridge, and Baby Bear just wants his bloody chair back.
Goldilocks, meanwhile, never set foot in that neck of the woods again.
And fair dinkum, can't say I bloody blame her.
The end. Too bloody right.
