Donkey's years ago, our old fellas brought forth on this continent a brand-spankin'-new nation, conceived in a fair go, and dead-set dedicated to the notion that all blokes are created equal, no matter what some drongo reckons.
Now, here we are, smack-bang in the middle of a bloody great civil stoush, findin' out whether that nation, or any other mob put together on the same idea, can keep the whole bloody show goin' for the long haul. We've gathered here on a great battlefield of that stoush. We've come to set aside a bit of this paddock as a final restin' place for those poor buggers who carked it here so the nation could keep on kickin'. And fair dinkum, it's only right and proper we do just that.
But look, when ya get right down to it, we can't dedicate this ground, can't consecrate it, can't bloody well hallow it, no matter how hard we try. The diggers, livin' and dead, who had a proper crack here, have already done that, and there's bugger-all we can do to add to it or take away from it. Crikey, the world mightn't take much notice of what we rabbit on about here, nor remember a bloody word of it, but what those brave bastards did here? Nah, mate. That won't be forgotten in a hurry.
It's up to us, the lucky buggers still breathin', to get stuck into the unfinished business those who fought here have so nobly carried this far. Too bloody right, it's on us to see the job through, come hell or high water. That from these honoured dead we take a bit more bloody determination for the cause they gave their last full measure for, right down to their final breath. That we stand here today, fair dinkum, and swear these poor buggers haven't carked it for nothin'. Not on your bloody nelly.
That this nation, God bloody willing, gets another crack at a fair go, and that government of the battlers, by the battlers, and for the battlers, shall not bloody well cark it from the face of the earth.
Stone the flamin' crows.
